## **1. Purpose**

This matrix is the master retention schedule. It identifies each record class, its owner, its location, how long it is kept, how it is destroyed, the authority for the period, and who approves destruction.

**Rule:** Where retention periods differ by applicable law, use the **longest applicable requirement** unless legal counsel directs otherwise. This schedule sets minimums; the organization may keep records longer.

**Legal hold:** If litigation, audit, investigation, or regulatory review is pending or anticipated, destruction stops immediately for all related records.

## **3. Destruction Methods**

| Record Type | Method |
|-------------|--------|
| Paper containing sensitive data (SSN, medical, financial) | Cross-cut shredding or secure destruction service; certificate of destruction |
| Paper, non-sensitive | Shredding or recycling |
| Electronic media | Secure deletion / sanitization; degauss or physically destroy media containing sensitive data |
| All records | Destruction logged with date, method, authorizing officer, and records affected |

## **4. Destruction Log**

Every destruction event is recorded:

```text
Date | Record Class | Range/Dates | Method | Authorized By | Witness
```

The log is kept permanently.

## **5. Review**

This schedule is reviewed annually with counsel and CPA input, and whenever law changes. Updates follow `01-GOVERNANCE/05-Change-Control-Procedure.md`.
