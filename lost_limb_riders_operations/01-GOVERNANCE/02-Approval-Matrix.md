## **1. Purpose**

This matrix defines who may authorize expenses, approve purchases, sign contracts, approve payroll, approve reimbursements, authorize events, approve compensation, open or close accounts, authorize grants, dispose of assets, and approve emergency expenditures.

## **2. Authority Levels**

| Level | Holder | One-Time Limit | Notes |
|-------|--------|----------------|-------|
| L1 | Department / Program Director | Up to $250 | Within approved budget |
| L2 | Executive Director | Up to $1,000 | Within approved budget |
| L3 | Finance Committee (via Treasurer) | $1,000.01 – $5,000 | Review and recommendation; budget check |
| L4 | Board of Directors | Above $5,000 | Motion recorded in minutes; also any unbudgeted or multi-year obligation |

**Limits are Lost Limb Riders internal control, not legal requirements.** They are recommended for formal adoption by the Board. The Board may change them by resolution; the change is recorded here and in the minutes.

## **3. Transaction-Specific Authority**

| Transaction | Authority | Documentation | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Routine purchase within budget | L1–L4 per limit | Purchase authorization (FIN-PUR-002) | Approver must differ from requester |
| Purchase needing competitive quotes | L2+ | Quote comparison sheet | Required over $1,000 unless impractical; documented |
| Expense reimbursement | Supervisor (not the claimant) | Expense report (FIN-EXP-001) | Claimant never approves own reimbursement |
| Payroll run | Executive Director (approval), Finance (execution), Treasurer (review) | Payroll approval checklist | Two-person review; Treasurer reviews bank statement monthly |
| Compensation (new or change) | Board (officers/key employees); Executive Director proposes, Board approves (staff) | HR-COMP-001, HR-PROC-002 | Interested persons recuse; see conflict controls |
| Event authorization | Executive Director up to L2 risk; Board for events with contract > $5,000 or significant risk | EVT-AUTH-001, EVT-FIN-001 | Financial feasibility required for paid assignments |
| Contract signing | Per limits: ED ≤ $1,000; Finance Committee ≤ $5,000; Board > $5,000 | Written contract | Two signatures recommended; counsel review for real property, loans, leases |
| Bank account open/close | Board resolution | Board minutes | Two authorized signers; Treasurer as signer |
| Grant application | Executive Director (routine); Board for awards affecting budget or restrictions | Grant file (GRT-001) | Grant acceptance reviewed by Finance Committee |
| Asset disposal | ED up to $1,000; Board above | Disposal record in AST-001 | Proceeds recorded; restricted assets need funder consent |
| Emergency expenditure | Any authorized person, ratified ED ≤24h, Board next meeting | Emergency expenditure record | Documentation never waived |
| Debt / loan | Board only | Board resolution | Requires counsel review |

## **4. Separation of Duties**

For every money transaction, separate these roles where practical:

```text
Requester → Approver → Payer → Reconciler
```

**Compensating controls when the organization is too small to separate fully:**

- The approver is never the requester.
- Two people count cash donations (`FUND-PROC-001`).
- The Treasurer or designee who does not do bookkeeping reviews bank statements monthly.
- The Board reviews financial reports at every regular meeting.
- The Finance Committee reviews all payments above $1,000.
- Related-party transactions are reported under the Conflict of Interest Policy.

**Prohibited:** One person authorizing, executing, recording, and reconciling the same transaction without any second-person review.

## **5. Conflict of Interest**

No person may approve their own compensation, their own reimbursement, a payment to an entity they control, or any transaction where they have a financial interest. The conflicted person discloses, recuses, and leaves discussion when directed. See Conflict of Interest Policy (Organization Handbook) and HR-PROC-002.

## **6. Delegation**

Authority may be delegated in writing. The delegate operates within the delegating level's limits, never above. Delegation must be recorded (email or memo) and retained in the governing record file.

## **7. Legal vs. Internal Control**

- Anything labeled **Lost Limb Riders internal control** may be changed by the Board without legal process.
- Anything labeled **legal requirement** may not be waived by this matrix. Where the organization operates below a legal threshold, the matrix may still impose a tighter internal limit.
- Where this matrix conflicts with a specific legal requirement, the legal requirement governs and counsel is consulted.
