## **1. Purpose**

This policy establishes the single document-control system for Lost Limb Riders. Every controlled document receives a unique ID, a metadata block, a version, and an owner. One active version exists per document; everything else is archived or superseded.

## **2. Scope**

Applies to all organizational policies, procedures, checklists, forms, templates, registers, job descriptions, program manuals, handbooks, and compliance documents — in this repository and in any future location.

## **4. Controlled Document Header Block**

Every controlled document begins with this metadata block:

```text
Document ID:
Document Title:
Department:
Document Type:

Effective Date:
Review Date:
Document Owner:
Approving Authority:
Supersedes:

Related Forms:

```

Fields not applicable to a document are marked "None" or "N/A". No controlled document may be published without at least: Document ID, Version, Effective Date, Review Date, Document Owner, and Approving Authority.

## **5. Versioning**

- Versions are integers (1.0, 1.1, 2.0). The first published version is 1.0.
- Any substantive change increments the version and adds a row to the document's Revision History table.
- Minor typographical corrections that do not change meaning may be made without a version change, but the correction is noted in the register.
- Unversioned documents are drafts. Drafts do not authorize action.

## **6. Approval and Authority**

- Approval authority for each document type is set in the Approval Matrix (`FIN-CTRL-001`).
- Governance and financial-control documents require Board approval.
- Operational procedures require Executive Director approval.
- A document is active only after approval and registration. Emergency procedures may take effect immediately under `01-GOVERNANCE/05-Change-Control-Procedure.md` but require ratification.

## **7. The Document Register**

The Records & Compliance Officer maintains the Document Register listing every controlled document:

```text
Document ID | Title | Type | Version | Effective | Review Due | Owner | Approving Authority | Status | Location | Supersedes
```

Status values: **Active**, **Superseded**, **Retired**, **Draft**. A document marked Active must live in an active folder. A superseded document must not be cited as authority.

## **8. Justification Rule**

Every controlled document must map to a real workflow. Before creating a document, confirm:

1. What transaction or activity does it govern?
2. Who uses it?
3. What record does it produce?
4. Who keeps the record and for how long (per CMP-RET-001)?

Documents that do not produce a usable record or govern an actual workflow should not be created. The goal is maximum traceability with minimum administrative burden.

## **9. Related Documents and Cross-References**

- Every procedure links to its governing policy.
- Every checklist links to the procedure it operationalizes.
- Every form links to its procedure and checklist.
- Every transaction record links to the retention schedule.
- When a referenced document is retired, all references are updated.

## **10. Publication and Retirement**

Follow `14-RECORDS-MANAGEMENT/02-Document-Lifecycle-Procedure.md` to create, approve, publish, update, or retire a document.

## **11. Compliance Notes**

This policy is a Lost Limb Riders internal control. It is not legal advice. Record-retention minimums are governed by `CMP-RET-001` and applicable law; where a conflict exists, the longest applicable requirement governs unless legal counsel directs otherwise.
