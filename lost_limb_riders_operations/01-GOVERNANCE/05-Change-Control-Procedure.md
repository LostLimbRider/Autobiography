## **1. Purpose**

This procedure governs regulatory and policy updates so that compliance documents stay current, versioned, and consistent. It prevents stale law from driving operations.

## **2. When to Run Change Control**

- Law, regulation, or IRS/Iowa guidance changes.
- A compliance calendar item or review date expires.
- The Board adopts a policy change.
- A form, procedure, or workflow changes.
- An external reviewer (attorney, CPA, insurance professional) recommends a change.

## **3. The Change Process**

1. **Identify affected documents.** Use the compliance matrices (CMP-IRS-001, CMP-IA-001) and the Document Register to find every document touched by the change.
2. **Update documents in order:** policy → procedure → checklist → form → compliance matrix → calendar.
3. **Verify consistency.** Every cross-reference to the changed document is checked and updated. No document may contradict another.
4. **Increment versions.** Each substantive change increments the version and adds a Revision History row.
5. **Document the change.** Record what changed, why, the authority, and the date in each document's revision history and in the Document Register.
6. **Retire obsolete versions.** Superseded versions remain in the register as history and are never cited as authority.
7. **Notify.** Affected staff are told what changed and when it takes effect.

## **4. Review Cadence**

- **Compliance matrices and calendar:** at least annually, and when law changes.
- **Forms and procedures:** annually with their department owner.
- **Retention schedule:** annually with counsel and CPA input.
- **Source authorities:** each matrix entry records its source and the date last verified.

## **5. Emergency Change**

Emergency operational changes may be approved by the Executive Director, effective immediately, documented the same day, and ratified by the Board at the next meeting. Ratification updates version numbers and the register retroactively.

## **6. External Professional Review Items**

Changes that require an attorney, CPA, payroll professional, or insurance professional are identified in each document and are not made on internal authority alone. See the completion report (`00-START-HERE/TRANSACTIONAL-LAYER-COMPLETION-REPORT.md`) for the current list.
