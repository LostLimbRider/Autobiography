## **1. Purpose and Usage**

This matrix maps federal obligations for a 501(c)(3) to authority, action, record, responsibility, frequency, due date, and retention. It is a **working control**, not legal advice. Requirements change — verify each item against the current authority cited and update via CMP-CHG-001.

## **2. Legend for "Source Type"**

- **FR** = Federal requirement
- **IC** = Lost Limb Riders internal control
- **RP** = Recommended best practice
- Items marked IC/RP are organizational choices, not legal obligations.
