## **1. Purpose and Usage**

This matrix maps Iowa obligations to authoritative sources, actions, records, responsibilities, and due dates. Each item cites the authoritative source; verify against the current authority before relying on it. Items marked **IC** are Lost Limb Riders internal controls, not Iowa requirements.

## **2. Legend for "Source Type"**

- **IA** = Iowa requirement
- **LOC** = Local requirement (varies by city/county)
- **IC** = Lost Limb Riders internal control
- **RP** = Recommended best practice

## **4. Confirmation Items**

The following require confirmation by counsel, CPA, or the state agency at the time of filing (rates, deadlines, and thresholds change):

- Withholding rates and filing schedule (IDOR)
- Unemployment rate and reporting schedule (IWD)
- Charitable solicitation registration requirements (Iowa SOS — confirm applicability for the organization's solicitation activity)
- Sales tax obligations for fundraising events/merchandise (IDOR)
- Workers' comp classification and carrier requirements (Iowa Code Ch. 85)
- Local permit requirements (city/county)
