## **Purpose**

Run this checklist once per year (recommended: October–December) to confirm the organization's federal and Iowa compliance posture before the new fiscal year.

## **FEDERAL**

- [ ] Form 990 / 990-EZ / 990-N filed and confirmation recorded
- [ ] Exemption application and determination letter on file
- [ ] Form 941s reconciled and filed for the year
- [ ] Form 940 (FUTA) filed
- [ ] W-2s and W-3 filed; W-2c prepared if needed
- [ ] 1099-NEC and 1096 filed; threshold review completed (CTR-CHK-001)
- [ ] Payroll tax deposits current and reconciled
- [ ] Charitable acknowledgments issued per policy; records retained
- [ ] Public-inspection documents available (990, exemption application)
- [ ] Compensation of officers/key employees documented (HR-COMP-001)
- [ ] Conflict disclosures collected annually
- [ ] Governance records (minutes, policies) current
- [ ] UBI review completed with CPA (if any new revenue stream)

## **IOWA**

- [ ] Biennial report to Secretary of State filed (per schedule) / next due date confirmed
- [ ] Registered agent and office current
- [ ] Iowa withholding registered and filings current
- [ ] Iowa unemployment filings current with IWD
- [ ] New-hire reporting process current
- [ ] Workers' compensation coverage current
- [ ] Charitable solicitation registration reviewed (confirm applicability)
- [ ] Iowa sales/use tax obligations reviewed (merchandise, admissions)
- [ ] Required posters/notices current
- [ ] Local permits reviewed

## **GOVERNANCE AND RECORDS**

- [ ] Bylaws, policies, and committee charters reviewed (Board)
- [ ] Retention matrix reviewed with counsel/CPA input
- [ ] Records destruction performed per matrix (if any) and logged
- [ ] Insurance coverage reviewed (SAF-INS-001)
- [ ] Bank account signers and access reviewed
- [ ] Master Transaction Register reviewed; open items identified
- [ ] Compliance calendar confirmed for next year
- [ ] Financial audit/review/compilation decision made (Bylaws 9.04)
