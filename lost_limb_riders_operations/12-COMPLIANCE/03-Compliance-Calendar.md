## **1. Purpose**

The compliance calendar tracks every recurring filing and review. Every item has a responsible person, a backup, a due date, and a status. When an item is filed, the confirmation is recorded.

## **2. Status Values**

- **NOT STARTED**
- **IN PROGRESS**
- **READY FOR REVIEW**
- **FILED**
- **CONFIRMED**
- **OVERDUE**
- **NOT APPLICABLE**

## **3. Calendar Columns**

```text
Requirement | Authority | Filing | Responsible Person | Backup | Due Date | Recurring Interval | Preparation Lead Time | Completion Date | Confirmation/Reference # | Document Location | Status
```

## **5. Usage Rules**

1. The Records & Compliance Officer maintains the calendar and monitors statuses.
2. Each item has a named responsible person **and** a backup.
3. OVERDUE items are reported to the Executive Director immediately.
4. Completion is confirmed with the filing confirmation/reference number.
5. The calendar is reviewed quarterly at Finance Committee and annually with the Board.
