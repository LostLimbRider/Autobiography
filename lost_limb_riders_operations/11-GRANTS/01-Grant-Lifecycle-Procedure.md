## **1. Purpose**

This procedure governs the full grant lifecycle — opportunity through closeout — so every grant is documented, restricted, spent correctly, and reported on time.

## **2. Lifecycle**

```text
Opportunity
    ↓
Eligibility
    ↓
Application
    ↓
Approval (internal, before submission)
    ↓
Award Agreement
    ↓
Restricted / Unrestricted Classification
    ↓
Budget
    ↓
Expenditures
    ↓
Documentation
    ↓
Program Performance
    ↓
Financial Reporting
    ↓
Grant Closeout
```

## **3. Detailed Steps**

1. **Opportunity:** log the grant opportunity with deadline and requirements.
2. **Eligibility:** confirm Lost Limb Riders meets eligibility (mission fit, geographic, type).
3. **Application:** draft the application; **internal approval** before submission (Executive Director; Board for major commitments). Match any cost-share/matching requirements to available funds before committing.
4. **Award agreement:** execute per FIN-CTRL-001; counsel/CPA review of terms as needed.
5. **Classification:** restricted or unrestricted per the award agreement; record in FUND-REST-001.
6. **Budget:** build the grant budget aligned to the award; obtain Board approval if required.
7. **Expenditures:** spend only per the budget and grant terms; follow FIN-PUR-001 / FIN-EXP-002; code expenses to GRT-YYYY-###.
8. **Documentation:** retain receipts, time allocation support, and progress evidence in the grant file (GRT-001).
9. **Program performance:** track deliverables and outcomes as defined by the grant.
10. **Financial reporting:** complete financial reports by deadline; reconcile grant spend monthly (Finance reconciliation).
11. **Closeout:** final report, funder sign-off, unspent funds returned or per award terms, file closed.

## **4. Grant File Requirement**

Every grant has a dedicated file (GRT-001) containing: application, award agreement, budget, restrictions, expenditures, reports, deliverables, contacts, correspondence, and closeout.

## **5. Prohibitions**

- Spending grant funds outside the approved budget or restriction.
- Using grant funds for prohibited expenses (per award terms — e.g., certain lobbying or political activity).
- Missing a reporting deadline without escalation.
- Commingling grant funds with unrestricted funds in reporting.
