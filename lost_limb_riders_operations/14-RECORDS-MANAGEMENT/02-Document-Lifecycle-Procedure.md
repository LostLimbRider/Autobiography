## **1. Purpose**

This procedure walks an employee through creating, approving, publishing, updating, and retiring a controlled document.

## **2. Create a Document**

1. **Justify.** Confirm the document maps to a real workflow (ADM-DOC-001, section 8).
2. **Draft.** Use the controlled-document header block. Follow the Documentation Style Guide.
3. **Review.** Document owner + affected department lead review for accuracy and consistency.
4. **Approve.** Submit to the approval authority in the Approval Matrix (FIN-CTRL-001). Governance and financial controls → Board. Operational → Executive Director.
5. **Register.** Records & Compliance Officer assigns the Document ID and adds the document to the Document Register.
6. **Publish.** Move the approved version to its active folder; update cross-references.
7. **Notify.** Tell affected staff.

## **3. Update a Document**

1. Identify the change and why (CMP-CHG-001).
2. Edit, increment the version, add a Revision History row.
3. Re-approve per the Approval Matrix (substantive changes only).
4. Update the Document Register; update cross-references.
5. Notify affected staff.

## **4. Retire a Document**

1. Record the reason (superseded / no longer needed / merged).
2. Mark status in the Document Register. Keep the superseded version for audit history.
3. Update every cross-reference.
4. If replaced, confirm the replacement is active first.
5. Board approval is required to retire governance or financial-control documents.

## **5. Archive a Document**

Archived documents move to `ARCHIVE/` with their version and date noted in the register. Archived does not mean active.
