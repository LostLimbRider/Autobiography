## **1. Purpose**

This procedure defines the complete employee lifecycle — from the moment a position is needed through separation and record retention. Every transition requires documentation and approval. No step is "handled."

## **3. Detailed Steps**

### 3.1 Position Need and Authorization

A position is opened only when a real need exists and funding is available. The hiring manager completes **HR-POS-001 (Position Authorization)** stating duties, expected hours, classification, and funding source. Approval per FIN-CTRL-001 (Board for new compensated positions; Executive Director for replacements within approved budget).

### 3.2 Job Description

Use the matching position manual in `employees/` as the job description. Confirm duties, reporting line, and required skills.

### 3.3 Compensation Analysis

Complete **HR-COMP-001 (Compensation Worksheet)** using `employees/Wage-Structure-and-Benefits.md` wage bands, comparable organizations, and current market data. Obtain approval per **HR-PROC-002**. The organization determines reasonable compensation for actual services — it does not let anyone set their own pay.

### 3.4 Recruitment and Application

Follow **HR-REC-001**. Every applicant completes **HR-APP-001**. Records are maintained per HR-FIL-001.

### 3.5 Interview and Selection

Follow **HR-INT-001**. Selection requires a documented decision. Before an offer, complete the **conflict check**: confirm the candidate has no undisclosed conflict with the organization, board, or staff (Conflict of Interest Policy).

### 3.6 Offer and Acceptance

Extend a written offer (**HR-OFR-001**) with compensation, classification, start date, and contingencies (reference checks, I-9 eligibility). Acceptance is the signed offer letter. Record the employee Transaction ID (`EMP-YYYY-###`) in the Master Transaction Register.

### 3.7 Onboarding and Payroll Setup

Complete **HR-ONB-001**. This includes W-4, I-9, Iowa withholding, direct deposit, emergency contact, and new-hire reporting. Payroll setup per HR-PROC-004.

### 3.8 Active Employment

- **Timekeeping:** hourly employees record time on **HR-TIME-001** each pay period.
- **Payroll:** runs per HR-PROC-004.
- **Performance:** annual review per HR-PERF-001.
- **Compensation review:** annual per HR-PROC-002.

### 3.9 Separation

Voluntary resignation, termination, or layoff follows **HR-SEP-001**: final payroll (HR-CHK-001, Checklist C), access revocation, property return, and record closeout. The employee file and payroll records are retained per CMP-RET-001.

## **4. Document Trail**

Every lifecycle step produces a document that goes into the personnel file (HR-FIL-001) and is indexed in the Master Transaction Register under the employee's `EMP-YYYY-###` ID.

## **5. External Review Note**

Classification (employee vs. contractor), wage and hour compliance, and Iowa employment law should be reviewed by counsel and a payroll professional before the first hire.
