## **1. Purpose**

This checklist establishes Lost Limb Riders as a lawful employer before anyone is hired or paid. Complete it before the first payroll.

## **2. Federal Setup**

- [ ] **Employer Identification Number (EIN):** Obtained from IRS (Form SS-4 or online). Organization has its own EIN; never use the founder's SSN for payroll.
- [ ] **Federal tax deposit account:** Set up at the IRS (e.g., EFTPS) for payroll tax deposits.
- [ ] **Payroll provider selected:** Approved by Board; handles computation, deposits, and filings, or the Bookkeeper is authorized to file directly.
- [ ] **Form 941 (Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return):** Filing responsibility assigned (payroll provider or Bookkeeper).
- [ ] **Form 940 (FUTA):** Filing responsibility assigned.
- [ ] **W-2 process:** Year-end process defined and assigned.
- [ ] **E-verify / I-9 process:** I-9 completion process defined. (E-verify use is optional for most employers but confirm current rules.)

## **3. Iowa Setup**

- [ ] **Iowa Workforce Development — employer account:** Registered for unemployment insurance and new-hire reporting.
- [ ] **Iowa Department of Revenue — withholding account:** Registered for Iowa income tax withholding (withholding permit).
- [ ] **Iowa withholding forms:** Current Iowa W-4 equivalent obtained for employees.
- [ ] **Workers' compensation insurance:** Coverage secured through the organization's insurance program as required by Iowa Code Chapter 85.
- [ ] **Iowa new-hire reporting:** Process defined (report new hires to IWD within statutory timeframes — confirm current deadlines).
- [ ] **Iowa unemployment insurance tax rate:** Confirmed with IWD annually.

## **4. Employer Responsibilities Defined**

- [ ] **Payroll calendar:** Pay frequency, pay days, and cutoff dates established (see HR-PROC-004).
- [ ] **Payroll responsibilities assigned:** Who computes, who approves, who deposits, who reconciles (two-person minimum).
- [ ] **Required employment notices:** Current federal and Iowa posters/notices obtained and posted (or provided electronically where permitted) — wage notices, workplace rights, etc.
- [ ] **Recordkeeping plan:** Personnel, payroll, time, and I-9 files established per HR-FIL-001 and CMP-RET-001.
- [ ] **Employer vs. employee records separated:** Organization-level records (registration, tax rates, notices) stored separately from employee-level confidential records.
- [ ] **Compensation authority:** Compensation approval procedure (HR-PROC-002) adopted; wage bands in `employees/Wage-Structure-and-Benefits.md` board-approved.
- [ ] **Direct deposit:** Bank account and authorization forms ready (employee records — stored restricted).
- [ ] **Insurance:** Liability, D&O, workers' comp, and any required coverage confirmed by the Insurance & Risk Manager.

## **5. Source References**

- Federal payroll obligations: IRS (irs.gov) and U.S. Department of Labor (dol.gov).
- Iowa obligations: Iowa Workforce Development (IWD), Iowa Department of Revenue, Iowa Code Chapter 85 (workers' comp).
- Verify current rates and deadlines at the time of setup. Do not rely on this checklist for rates.
