## **1. Purpose**

This procedure defines how payroll is computed, approved, paid, recorded, reconciled, and filed. It ensures every dollar paid to an employee is traceable to approved time and approved compensation.

## **2. Payroll Calendar**

The Board sets the pay frequency and pay dates before the first payroll run (see HR-ADM-001). Record the decision in board minutes and reference it here. Defaults below apply until the Board adopts a different schedule.

- **Pay frequency:** [Set by Board resolution — Board decision required before first payroll. Default practice: semimonthly. Confirm with payroll provider.]
- **Payroll cutoff:** Time records due to supervisors by 5:00 p.m. on the last business day of the pay period; approved records due to payroll by noon the next business day. Late records are paid in the following run unless payroll approves an off-cycle correction (HR-CHK-001, Checklist B).
- **Pay date:** [Day set by Board resolution — Board decision required before first payroll.] Pay date must meet applicable Iowa final-pay and regular-pay timing rules (Iowa Code Ch. 91A — confirm current rules with Iowa Workforce Development).
- **Internal deadline:** Payroll register must be fully reviewed and approved by the Executive Director no later than one business day before the pay date.
- **Change control:** Any change to the calendar is a board decision per FIN-CTRL-001 and is recorded in board minutes before it takes effect.

## **3. Payroll Workflow**

```text
Time Records (HR-TIME-001)
    ↓
Supervisor Verification (HR-PROC-003)
    ↓
Payroll Review (HR-CHK-001)
    ↓
Gross Wage Calculation
    ↓
Withholding (federal, Iowa, FICA)
    ↓
Employer Taxes (matching, FUTA/SUTA)
    ↓
Payroll Approval (Executive Director)
    ↓
Payment (direct deposit / check)
    ↓
Payroll Journal Entry
    ↓
Tax Deposit (per IRS/Iowa schedule)
    ↓

    ↓

```

## **4. Detailed Steps**

1. **Collect records.** Gather approved HR-TIME-001 records and any stipend/salary pay information, plus compensation changes approved per HR-PROC-002.
2. **Compute gross wages.** Hours × approved rate, plus pre-approved overtime. Stipends/salaries are prorated per pay period.
3. **Apply withholdings** from W-4 and Iowa withholding forms: federal income tax, Iowa income tax, and FICA (Social Security/Medicare) using the current IRS/Iowa tables. **Do not hard-code rates** — the payroll system uses current published tables; verify rates at each update.
4. **Compute employer taxes:** employer FICA match, federal unemployment (FUTA), Iowa unemployment. Verify current rates with IRS/IWD.
5. **Review and approve** per HR-CHK-001. Executive Director approves the payroll run; Finance reviews totals; Treasurer reviews bank statement monthly.
6. **Pay employees** by approved method (direct deposit or check) on pay date.
7. **Post the payroll journal entry:** debit wage expense by department/program/event; credit payroll liabilities and cash. Cross-reference the event/program ID (e.g., EVT-2026-001) for labor allocated to events.
8. **Deposit taxes** per current federal and Iowa schedules (payroll provider handles if contracted).
9. **Reconcile:** payroll register matches approved time × rates; totals match journal entry; deposits match liability balances.
10. **File records** per CMP-RET-001.

## **5. Payroll Checks**

- Regular payroll: HR-CHK-001, Checklist A
- Correction payroll (pay adjustments, over/underpayments): HR-CHK-001, Checklist B
- Final payroll (separation): HR-CHK-001, Checklist C (and HR-SEP-001)
- Year-end payroll (W-2s, final 941/940, Iowa annual): HR-CHK-001, Checklist D

## **6. Separation of Duties**

At minimum two people are involved: one computes/executes, one reviews/approves. The Executive Director approves the run; the Treasurer or designee who does not prepare payroll reviews bank statements and the monthly reconciliation.

## **7. Iowa Payroll Notes**

- Iowa new hires reported to IWD per current law.
- Iowa withholding filed per Iowa Department of Revenue schedule (quarterly or annual as required).
- Unemployment insurance reports filed with IWD per its schedule.
- **External review item:** confirm current forms, deadlines, and rates with the payroll provider, IWD, and Iowa Department of Revenue at setup and annually.
