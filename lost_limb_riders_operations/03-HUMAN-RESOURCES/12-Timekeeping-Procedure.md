## **1. Purpose**

This procedure requires every paid employee to produce a certified, supervisor-approved time record each pay period. Time records are the source for payroll. No time record, no pay.

## **2. Who Records Time**

- **Hourly / non-exempt employees:** complete HR-TIME-001 (Employee Time Record) for every pay period.
- **Stipend employees:** complete a monthly activity summary per wage structure; hours inform accountability, not hourly pay.
- **Salaried employees:** track hours for planning and reporting per wage structure.

## **3. Recording Rules**

1. Record actual start and end times for each work date; record unpaid breaks of 20+ minutes if applicable to the schedule.
2. Record the program, project, or event worked so labor can be allocated (e.g., EVT-2026-001).
3. Record overtime hours separately. **Overtime must be pre-approved by the supervisor** where the organization requires it; report actual hours regardless.
4. Record time daily or at shift end. Do not backfill from memory at month end.
5. The employee certifies the record (signature/electronic attestation).

## **4. Supervisor Verification**

The supervisor reviews each record for accuracy, approves it, and resolves discrepancies before payroll. Supervisor approval is required before the record feeds payroll.

## **5. Payroll Feeding**

Approved time records flow into payroll per HR-PROC-004. Payroll is computed from approved records only.

## **6. Retention**

Approved time records are filed in the payroll file and retained per CMP-RET-001 (2-year FLSA minimum; 7 years internal).
