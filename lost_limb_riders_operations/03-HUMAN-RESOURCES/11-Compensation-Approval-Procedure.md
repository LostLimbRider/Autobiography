## **1. Purpose**

This procedure ensures that compensation is reasonable, documented, and approved by disinterested decision makers. It specifically prevents one person — including the founder — from deciding their own salary.

## **2. Governing Principle**

Compensation for legitimate work is an organizational operating expense, not an impermissible distribution of nonprofit earnings. Reasonable compensation for actual services is lawful and appropriate. The process below makes reasonableness demonstrable.

## **3. Who Decides What**

| Decision | Proposer | Approver | Conflict Rule |
|----------|----------|----------|---------------|
| Executive Director salary | Board-designated committee (no ED) | Board | ED recuses |
| Founder/CEO compensation | Board-designated committee | Board | Founder recuses; independent review |
| Officer / key employee stipend | Executive Director | Board | Interested persons recuse |
| All other staff stipends | Executive Director | Board (as part of budget) | Direct supervisor proposes; ED approves within budget |
| Off-cycle adjustment >10% | Executive Director | Board | Interested persons recuse |
| Benefits changes | Executive Director | Board | Interested persons recuse |

## **4. Required Process**

1. **Document the position and duties** (HR-COMP-001) with a current job description.
2. **Gather comparability data:** wage structure bands, nonprofit salary surveys, comparable positions, peer organizations. Note the source and date.
3. **Propose compensation** and complete the worksheet.
4. **Disinterested approval:** the deciding body consists of people with no financial interest in the decision. Interested persons disclose and recuse (Conflict of Interest Policy).
5. **Approve and record:** the approval is captured in board minutes or an approved worksheet with resolution reference (BRD-YYYY-###).
6. **Contemporaneous documentation:** the worksheet is dated at decision time. Retroactive documentation is avoided.
7. **Annual review:** compensation is reviewed annually during budget planning (September–October) and at performance review (HR-PERF-001).

## **5. Prohibitions**

- No person sets their own compensation.
- No compensation is based on a fixed percentage of donations or revenue.
- No insider compensation without board approval and conflict controls.
- No retroactive compensation approval as a substitute for the process above.

## **6. Excess Benefit and Inurement Note**

Excess benefit transactions (including unreasonable compensation to disqualified persons) can subject the organization and the recipient to IRS excise taxes under IRC §4958. This procedure is designed to document the reasonable-compensation rebuttal factors (approval by authorized body, comparability data, contemporaneous documentation). **External review item:** counsel/CPA should confirm compliance with IRC §4958 and Form 990 disclosure for key employees and disqualified persons.
