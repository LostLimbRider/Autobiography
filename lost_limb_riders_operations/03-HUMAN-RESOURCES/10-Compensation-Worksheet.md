## **Purpose**

Lost Limb Riders determines compensation by documented analysis of actual services, not by arbitrary percentages of donations. This worksheet records the analysis for every paid position, including the Executive Director, the Founder/CEO, officers, and key employees.

## **REASONABLENESS NOTE**

Compensation is reasonable if it falls within documented market ranges for comparable services and is approved by disinterested decision makers. For insiders, officers, and key employees, the Board follows HR-PROC-002 and the Conflict of Interest Policy. Documentation is contemporaneous (prepared at the time of the decision).
