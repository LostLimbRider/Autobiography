## **1. Purpose**

This procedure controls how organizational money is spent and how employees, volunteers, and officers are reimbursed for approved out-of-pocket expenses. It prevents undocumented, unauthorized, or personal spending.

## **2. Every Expense Must Answer**

1. Who spent the money? 2. When? 3. Where? 4. How much? 5. Why? 6. What program/event? 7. What account? 8. Was it authorized? 9. Receipt? 10. Reimbursement or direct organizational payment? 11. Who approved it? 12. When paid? 13. Accounting reference?

These are captured on **FIN-EXP-001 (Expense Report)**.

## **3. Rules**

1. **Authorization first.** Expenses expected to exceed $100 require pre-approval before purchase. This is a Lost Limb Riders internal control (stricter than the FIN-CTRL-001 approval thresholds, which govern who may approve at each amount). Pre-approval is recorded (email or form). Unauthorized expenses may not be reimbursed. The $100 threshold is changed only by board resolution recorded in the minutes.
2. **Receipts required.** Every expense requires a receipt unless it is mileage (FIN-EXP-003) or covered by a missing-receipt declaration (FIN-EXP-004).
3. **Business purpose only.** Personal, family, or fundraising-commingled expenses are not reimbursed. Alcohol, gifts, and entertainment require policy/counsel review before purchase.
4. **Direct payment preferred** for organizational purchases (FIN-PUR-001). Reimbursement is for approved out-of-pocket costs.
5. **Claimant never approves their own reimbursement.** The approver differs from the claimant.
6. **Timing.** Reimbursement requests are submitted within 60 days of the expense per wage structure. Late requests require approval with an explanation.
7. **Mileage.** Reimbursed at the IRS standard business rate in effect at the time; mileage logged on FIN-EXP-003.
8. **Event expenses** are coded to the Event ID (EVT-YYYY-###) and reconcile into the event closeout (EVT-CLOSE-001).

## **4. Workflow**

```text
Expense incurred (with authorization where required)
    ↓
Receipts and documentation collected (FIN-EXP-003 mileage if applicable)
    ↓
Expense Report completed (FIN-EXP-001)
    ↓
Supervisor / approver review (approver ≠ claimant)
    ↓
Missing receipt declaration if needed (FIN-EXP-004)
    ↓
Approval per FIN-CTRL-001
    ↓
Payment (reimbursement or direct) — PAY-YYYY-###
    ↓
Accounting entry (account + program/event)
    ↓
Reconciliation and retention
```
