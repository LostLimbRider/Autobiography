## **1. Purpose**

Bank reconciliations verify that recorded cash matches the bank. They are the organization's most important fraud control and are performed monthly.

## **2. Procedure**

1. Obtain the bank statement and the ledger cash account for the month.
2. Match deposits to the ledger (identify and resolve differences).
3. Match cleared checks/EFT/payments to the ledger (identify and resolve differences).
4. Record and explain bank fees, interest, and adjustments.
5. Identify outstanding items (deposits in transit, outstanding checks).
6. Confirm the reconciled balance equals the ledger balance; investigate all differences.
7. Record the reconciliation, date it, and have it reviewed by the Treasurer or designee who does not perform bookkeeping.

## **3. Control Rule**

**The person who performs bookkeeping does not perform the independent review.** If the organization has only one finance person, the Treasurer performs the review and the Board sees the reconciliation summary monthly.

## **4. Red Flags**

- Unexplained or recurring differences
- Payments without supporting authorization
- Checks/EFTs to unfamiliar or related parties
- Deposits not matching donation/event logs
- Signs of splitting or cash handling issues

Any red flag is escalated to the Executive Director and Treasurer immediately.
