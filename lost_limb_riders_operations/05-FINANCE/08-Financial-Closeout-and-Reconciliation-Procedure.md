## **1. Purpose**

This procedure defines monthly, event, grant, and year-end financial closeout so every transaction is posted, reconciled, and documented.

## **2. Monthly Closeout**

1. Post all journal entries for the month (payroll, donations, grants, expenses, payments).
2. Reconcile bank accounts and payment processor statements to the ledger (Treasurer or designee who does not do bookkeeping reviews).
3. Reconcile donations (DON log ↔ deposits ↔ donor records).
4. Reconcile payroll (register ↔ journal ↔ tax deposits).
5. Reconcile restricted funds (FUND-REST-001) — spend vs. restriction.
6. Review accounts payable and accounts receivable aging.
7. Produce monthly report: statement of activity, statement of financial position, budget vs. actual, cash balance, restricted funds, major payables, grant status, fundraising pipeline.
8. Board receives financial reports at every regular meeting.

## **3. Event Closeout**

Follow EVT-CLOSE-001: revenue, labor, expense reconciliation, final result, variance explanation.

## **4. Grant Closeout**

Follow GRT-PROC-001: expenditures vs. budget, reporting deadlines, deliverables, final report, funder sign-off.

## **5. Year-End Closeout**

1. Complete all journal entries; adjust accruals per CPA guidance.
2. Reconcile all balance sheet accounts (cash, receivables, payables, restricted funds, fixed assets).
3. Run W-2/1099 processes (HR-CHK-001 Checklist D, CTR-CHK-001).
4. Complete Form 990 (CMP-IRS-001) and Iowa filings (CMP-IA-001) with CPA review.
5. Produce annual financial statements; complete audit/review/compilation as determined by the Board (Bylaws 9.04).
6. Archive records per CMP-RET-001.

## **6. Separation of Duties at Closeout**

The reconciler differs from the preparer. The Treasurer reviews the completed closeout. The Board approves the annual closeout.
