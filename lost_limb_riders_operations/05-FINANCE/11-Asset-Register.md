## **1. Purpose**

The asset register tracks organizational property: what it is, where it is, who holds it, what it cost, and what happened to it. It covers vehicles, trailers, computers, equipment, tools, furniture, significant technology, and other capital assets.

## **2. When an Asset Is Entered**

An asset is entered when purchased, donated, or otherwise acquired if it meets the capitalization threshold set by the Board (recommended: $500) **or** if it is valuable, portable, or sensitive regardless of cost (laptops, phones, cameras, adaptive equipment, tools, trailer, motorcycle equipment).

## **5. Periodic Checks**

- **Annual inventory:** custodian confirms assets on hand; discrepancies reported.
- **Transfers:** custodian changes are logged.
- **Insurance:** significant assets are included in the insurance review (SAF-INS-001).

## **6. Disposal**

Disposal requires authorization per FIN-CTRL-001 (Board above $1,000 or for restricted assets). Record:

- Reason for disposal (obsolete / damaged / sold / donated / trade-in)
- Disposal method and date
- Proceeds received (credited to the funding source or per donor restriction)
- Approval reference (BRD-YYYY-### where Board approved)
- Register update

**Restricted assets:** assets purchased with restricted or grant funds are disposed of per the donor/funder terms or with funder consent.
