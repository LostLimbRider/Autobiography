## **1. Purpose**

This procedure controls how the organization buys goods and services: authorization, quoting, approval, payment, and records. It prevents unauthorized purchases, conflicts of interest, and unrecorded spending.

## **2. Purchasing Thresholds**

These are **Lost Limb Riders internal controls**, not legal requirements.

| Amount | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Routine purchases (under the department limit) | Approved within budget; purchase authorization (FIN-PUR-002) |
| Over $1,000 | Competitive quotes required unless impractical; documented |
| Above $1,000 (expense) | Finance Committee review per FIN-CTRL-001 |
| Above $5,000 | Board approval; quotes; written decision in minutes |
| Unbudgeted or multi-year obligation | Board approval regardless of amount |
| Related-party vendor | Disclosure + conflict review + approval by disinterested party |

## **3. Workflow**

```text
Need identified
    ↓
Budget check (line item + available funds)
    ↓
Authorization (FIN-PUR-002) per FIN-CTRL-001
    ↓
Quoting if over threshold (2-3 quotes; document)
    ↓
Purchase (organizational card/payment preferred)
    ↓
Receive goods/services; verify
    ↓
Invoice match (PO/authorization, receipt, invoice)
    ↓
Payment authorization (FIN-PAY-001)
    ↓
Payment + accounting entry
    ↓
Reconciliation and retention
```

## **4. Rules**

1. **Budget first.** No purchase that would exceed the budget line without approval.
2. **Authorization before purchase.** Purchases expected to exceed $100 (or per policy) require pre-authorization.
3. **Organizational payment preferred.** Reimbursement is the exception (FIN-EXP-002).
4. **Quotes documented.** For quote-required purchases, attach the comparison. If quoting is impractical, document why on the authorization.
5. **Conflict check.** Purchases from board members, staff, relatives, or entities they control require disclosure and disinterested approval (Conflict of Interest Policy).
6. **No splitting.** A purchase may not be split into smaller invoices to avoid a threshold.
7. **Capital items.** Purchases over the capitalization threshold are added to the Asset Register (AST-001).
8. **Grants/sponsorships.** Purchases under a grant follow grant rules (11-GRANTS).
9. **Sales tax.** Lost Limb Riders is exempt from Iowa sales tax on purchases made in its exempt capacity when a valid Iowa sales/use tax exemption certificate is on file (Iowa Code Ch. 423 — confirm current rules with the Iowa Department of Revenue). Provide the exemption certificate at purchase time; verify vendor invoices do not charge tax where exemption applies; use the certificate only for organizational purchases, never personal purchases. See CMP-IA-001.
10. **Services and contractors.** Purchases of services from non-employees must go through the contractor system (04-CONTRACTORS) when the individual is not an employee.
