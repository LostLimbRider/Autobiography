**Missing Receipt Declaration — receipts are required. This form documents a genuine, exceptional loss of a receipt. It is not a loophole for undocumented spending.**

**IMPORTANT:** The missing-receipt process is for rare, legitimate cases (receipt lost in transit, vendor failed to provide one, natural wear). Repeated missing-receipt declarations by the same claimant will be treated as a control concern and may result in denial or disciplinary review. These limits are Lost Limb Riders internal controls and are changed only by board resolution:

- **Per-declaration cap:** $100 per declared line item.
- **Cumulative cap:** No more than $300 per claimant per calendar year in missing-receipt declarations.
- **Finance Committee review:** Any declaration above the per-declaration cap, or any claimant's third declaration in a calendar year, requires Finance Committee review and Executive Director approval.
- Declarations never waive the underlying documentation standard — if no corroborating evidence exists, the expense is not reimbursed.
