## **1. Purpose**

The annual budget is the financial plan the Board approves and management executes. It funds programs, staff, events, and reserves within revenue expectations.

## **2. Fiscal Year**

January 1 through December 31 (Bylaws 9.02).

## **3. Timeline**

1. **August–September:** Department directors submit program goals and budget requests (revenue, expenses, staffing needs, assumptions).
2. **September–October:** Executive Director and Finance Director consolidate; draft budget prepared; compensation proposals per HR-PROC-002.
3. **October:** Finance Committee reviews the draft and recommends approval to the Board.
4. **November:** Board approves the final budget and records the approval in minutes (BRD-YYYY-###).
5. **Year-round:** Budget vs. actual reported monthly; significant variances are explained; spending follows FIN-CTRL-001.

## **4. Budget Content**

- Revenue by source (donations, events, sponsorships, grants, contracts, other)
- Expense by department and program (personnel, direct program costs, admin, fundraising)
- Restricted fund budget (grant and restricted revenue mapped to restricted expenditures)
- Reserves policy and target
- Capital purchases (assets)

## **5. Mid-Year Review**

The Board reviews the budget at mid-year (June) and approves amendments as needed. Amendments are recorded in minutes.
