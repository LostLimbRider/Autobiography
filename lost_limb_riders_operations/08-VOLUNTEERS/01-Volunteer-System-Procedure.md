## **1. Purpose**

This procedure keeps volunteers distinct from employees. A volunteer provides unpaid service under an agreement — a volunteer is **not merely an unpaid employee**. Classification matters for labor law, workers' comp, and fairness. (Iowa and federal law: confirm current rules; a volunteer does not replace a paid employee for the same role.)

## **2. Volunteer Lifecycle**

```text
Application (Volunteer Application form)
    ↓
Screening (background check where required; reference check)
    ↓
Orientation (mission, policies, boundaries, safety)
    ↓
Agreement (VOL-001)
    ↓
Assignment (role, supervisor, schedule)
    ↓
Time Tracking (Volunteer Hours Tracking form)
    ↓
Expense Reimbursement (VOL-002) — approved out-of-pocket costs only
    ↓
Safety Training (per program/activity)
    ↓
Incident Reporting (SAF-INC-001)
    ↓
Recognition / Performance / Feedback
    ↓
Separation / Inactive Status
```

## **3. Steps**

1. **Application:** volunteer completes the Volunteer Application (including background-check authorization where required).
2. **Screening:** the Volunteer Coordinator conducts screening per policy (references, background check per Board-approved screening policy and disqualifying criteria).
3. **Orientation:** orientation covers the Member Handbook, code of conduct, boundaries, safety, and confidentiality.
4. **Agreement:** volunteer signs VOL-001 (no cash compensation; reimbursement of approved expenses; at-will participation; incident expectations).
5. **Assignment:** assign role, supervisor, and schedule; record in the volunteer file.
6. **Time tracking:** volunteers log hours (Volunteer Hours Tracking form) for reporting and recognition — not payroll.
7. **Expenses:** approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses may be reimbursed per VOL-002 (not a substitute for compensation).
8. **Safety:** activity-specific safety training per program manual; incident reporting per SAF-INC-001.
9. **Separation:** volunteer may step away anytime; the coordinator closes the file (retain per CMP-RET-001).

## **4. Rules and Boundaries**

- Volunteers receive no cash compensation for services.
- Volunteers are not substituted for paid employees where law requires employees.
- Volunteer expenses are reimbursed only when pre-approved and documented.
- Volunteers who begin performing integral, supervised, ongoing work for pay must be classified as employees (see CTR-001 / HR-PROC-001). Labeling a worker a "volunteer" does not override the facts.
