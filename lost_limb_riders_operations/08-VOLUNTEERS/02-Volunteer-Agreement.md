I understand and agree:

1. **Volunteer status.** I serve Lost Limb Riders as a volunteer providing unpaid service. I am not an employee of the organization and am not entitled to wages, benefits, or employee protections.
2. **No compensation.** I will not receive cash compensation for my service. Approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses may be reimbursed per the organization's policy (VOL-002).
3. **Commitment.** I will honor my committed schedule, communicate absences to my supervisor, and perform my assigned duties to the best of my ability.
4. **Conduct.** I will comply with the organization's policies, code of conduct, and safety requirements, and treat participants, staff, and other volunteers with respect and dignity.
5. **Confidentiality.** I will keep confidential information private, including participant, donor, financial, and personnel information.
6. **Safety.** I will follow safety procedures, report hazards, and report any incident to my supervisor immediately (SAF-INC-001).
7. **At-will participation.** Either the organization or I may end my volunteer service at any time.
8. **Release.** My participation is subject to the Participant/Volunteer Release of Liability as signed separately.
9. **No implied employment.** Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship.
