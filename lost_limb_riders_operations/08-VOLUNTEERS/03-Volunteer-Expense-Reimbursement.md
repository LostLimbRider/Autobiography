## **1. Purpose**

Volunteers may be reimbursed for **approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses** incurred in service. Reimbursement is not compensation and does not change volunteer status.

## **2. Rules**

1. **Pre-approval:** expenses over $100 require pre-approval (per FIN-EXP-002). Ask before spending.
2. **Documentation:** submit an Expense Report (FIN-EXP-001) with receipts; mileage on FIN-EXP-003 at the IRS standard rate.
3. **Business purpose:** expenses must be for approved volunteer service (travel to approved events, approved supplies, etc.).
4. **Approver differs from claimant.**
5. **Timing:** submit within 60 days per organization policy.
6. **Missing receipts:** use FIN-EXP-004 only for genuine, exceptional cases — not as a routine route.
7. **Volunteers are not employees:** reimbursement does not create employment status. No hourly or wage payments to volunteers.

## **3. Workflow**

```text
Approved expense incurred
    ↓
Receipts collected (FIN-EXP-003 for mileage)
    ↓
Expense Report (FIN-EXP-001) submitted
    ↓
Approved (approver ≠ volunteer) per FIN-CTRL-001
    ↓
Reimbursed (EXP-YYYY-###) and recorded
    ↓
Filed in volunteer file / finance records
```
