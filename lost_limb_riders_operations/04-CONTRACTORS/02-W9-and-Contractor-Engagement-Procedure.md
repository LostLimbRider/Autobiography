## **1. Purpose**

This procedure governs engaging independent contractors: classification, documentation, payment, and year-end reporting. Every contractor gets a written agreement, a W-9, an invoice process, and a payment authorization — in that order.

## **2. Workflow**

```text
Business Need
    ↓
Classification Review (CTR-001)
    ↓
Selection (documented; conflict review)
    ↓
W-9 collected (Form W-9)
    ↓
Written Agreement (CTR-002) + Scope of Work (CTR-003)
    ↓
Rate and Approval (per FIN-CTRL-001)
    ↓
Work performed
    ↓
Invoice (CTR-004)
    ↓
Verification (work received as specified)
    ↓
Payment Authorization (CTR-005)
    ↓
Payment (accounting entry; PAY-YYYY-###)
    ↓
1099 determination (CTR-CHK-001)
    ↓
Closeout
```

## **3. Detailed Steps**

1. **Business need:** document what service is needed and why a contractor (not an employee) is appropriate. Complete CTR-001.
2. **Selection:** choose a contractor; document the basis. Check for conflicts of interest (related-party contractor = disclosure + board/ED review).
3. **W-9:** collect a completed Form W-9 (requesting TIN) before any payment. Verify the name/TIN matches the W-9. A contractor who will not provide a W-9 should not be paid until counsel confirms the situation.
4. **Written agreement:** execute CTR-002 with the scope (CTR-003) attached. Confirm contractor maintains its own insurance where applicable.
5. **Rate and approval:** the rate is approved per FIN-CTRL-001 before work begins. No work before approval except documented emergencies.
6. **Work and invoice:** contractor submits an invoice (CTR-004) for completed work. 
7. **Verification:** the requesting supervisor verifies the work and services were delivered as specified before payment.
8. **Payment:** payment is authorized on CTR-005 by an approver who did not request the work, paid, and recorded with a journal entry.
9. **1099 determination:** at year-end, review CTR-CHK-001 to determine whether Form 1099-NEC is required.
10. **Closeout:** close the CTR-YYYY-### transaction in the Master Transaction Register.

## **4. Prohibitions**

- Paying for contractor work without a W-9.
- Paying a person misclassified as a contractor (see CTR-001).
- Paying a related party without disclosure and approval.
- Processing an invoice without verification.
