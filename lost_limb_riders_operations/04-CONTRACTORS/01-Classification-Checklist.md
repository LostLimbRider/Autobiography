## **WARNING — READ FIRST**

**Calling someone a contractor does not make them one.** Classification is determined by the facts of the working relationship, not by the label, the agreement title, or the person's preference. Misclassification creates liability for unpaid taxes, penalties, and wage claims. If unsure, ask counsel before paying the person.

## **CLASSIFICATION CHECKLIST**

For each factor, indicate where the relationship sits. Worker is more likely a contractor when the factors fall to the right.

| Factor | Employee (usually) | Contractor (usually) | This Worker |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| Control over how work is done | Organization directs how/when/where | Worker controls method | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Control over business/financial side | No risk of profit/loss; expenses reimbursed | Worker has profit/loss risk, unreimbursed business expenses, services to others | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Relationship | Ongoing/indefinite; integral to organization | Finite project/term; distinct service | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Method of payment | Hourly/salary; regular pay period | Per project/task; per invoice | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Tools and equipment | Provided by organization | Provided by worker | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Benefits | Eligible | None | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Integration | Work is core to the organization | Work is peripheral or specialized | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |
| Training | Organization trains | No training by org | ☐ Emp ☐ Ctrl ☐ Mixed |

## **DECISION**

- [ ] **Employee** — most factors indicate employment. Do NOT engage as contractor. Route through HR-PROC-001.
- [ ] **Contractor** — most factors indicate independent contractor status. Proceed with CTR-PROC-001.
- [ ] **Mixed / uncertain** — stop. Obtain counsel review before proceeding.

**Documentation:** Retain this checklist with the contract (CTR-002). For each checked factor, add a one-line note with the supporting facts.

**Safeguard:** Workers performing work that is integral to and continuous within the organization (e.g., ongoing event staffing, administrative work) are presumed employees unless counsel determines otherwise.
