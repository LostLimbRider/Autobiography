## **Purpose**

Use this checklist at each year-end to determine which contractors must receive Form 1099-NEC (nonemployee compensation) and to confirm filings. Current IRS thresholds and rules change; verify against the current IRS instructions before filing.

## **1099 REVIEW**

- [ ] All contractor payments for the year compiled from the Master Transaction Register (CTR-YYYY-###) and payment records
- [ ] Payments to each payee totaled (including cash, check, and electronic payments)
- [ ] Payee's W-9 TIN matched to payment records (mismatches resolved or flagged for B-notice process)
- [ ] Threshold test applied per current IRS rules (e.g., 1099-NEC requirement for nonemployee compensation above the IRS threshold; confirm current rules)
- [ ] Exceptions identified: payments to corporations, purchases of merchandise, payments by credit card/payment processor (generally reported by the processor), and other IRS-defined exceptions
- [ ] Payments made to employees excluded (these are W-2, not 1099)
- [ ] Payments made to attorneys / providers reviewed per IRS rules
- [ ] 1099-NEC forms prepared and furnished to payees by the IRS deadline
- [ ] Transmittal (1096) filed with IRS by the deadline
- [ ] Records of 1099 filings retained per CMP-RET-001
- [ ] Any backup withholding obligations confirmed with the IRS (if the contractor failed to provide a TIN)
- [ ] Iowa 1099 reporting requirements confirmed with the Iowa Department of Revenue (Iowa may have its own filing requirements)
