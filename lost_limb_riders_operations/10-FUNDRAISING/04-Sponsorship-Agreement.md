## **1. Purpose and Key Distinction**

A sponsorship is **a payment for promotional/benefits delivered** — advertising, recognition, logo placement, booth space — not a pure charitable gift. See FUND-REF-001 for the tax distinction. This agreement documents exactly what Lost Limb Riders promised in exchange for the sponsorship.
