## **1. Purpose**

Restricted funds — grants, restricted gifts, and event-designated donations — are held for a stated purpose. This procedure tracks them so restricted money is never spent on unrestricted purposes.

## **2. Classification at Receipt**

At receipt (FUND-PROC-001), classify each contribution:

- **Unrestricted** — no donor-imposed purpose
- **Temporarily restricted** — purpose and/or time restriction (e.g., fund the Ride Forward program, a scholarship, or a named event)
- **Permanently restricted** — principal held in perpetuity (endowment-type) — obtain counsel before accepting
- **Grant** — restrictions set by the award agreement (11-GRANTS)

The restriction is recorded exactly as stated by the donor. Ambiguity is resolved in writing with the donor.

## **3. Tracking**

- Maintain a **restricted fund ledger** per fund:

```text
Fund | Source (DON/GRT ref) | Restriction | Received | Available | Committed | Spent | Balance | Reports Due
```

- Expenditures against a restricted fund require a restriction match: the expense's purpose, event ID, or grant line must satisfy the restriction.
- Restricted and unrestricted funds are never commingled in reporting. Bank accounts may be combined with separate ledger tracking, subject to grant/donor terms.

## **4. Spending and Closeout**

1. Confirm expense qualifies under the restriction before payment.
2. Record the draw against the fund (cross-reference EXP/PAY/EVT/GRT).
3. When the purpose is fulfilled, close the fund, notify the donor/funder, and retain records per CMP-RET-001.
4. If the purpose cannot be fulfilled, seek donor agreement to redirect or return the funds. Document the outcome.

## **5. Reporting**

Restricted fund balances and activity appear in monthly financial reports and the annual closeout (FIN-CLOSE-001). Grant reporting follows 11-GRANTS.
