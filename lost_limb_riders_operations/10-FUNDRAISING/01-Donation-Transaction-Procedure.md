## **1. Purpose**

This procedure governs how donations are received, identified, restricted, acknowledged, deposited, recorded, and reconciled. It distinguishes donations from sponsorships and sales (FUND-REF-001) because their tax treatment differs.

## **2. Donation Workflow**

```text
Donation Received
    ↓
Identify Donor (name, address, date, amount, form)
    ↓
Determine Restriction (unrestricted / restricted to program, event, fund)
    ↓
Receipt / Acknowledgment (FUND-PROC-002)
    ↓
Deposit (prompt, two-person count for cash)
    ↓
Accounting (DON-YYYY-###; revenue entry)
    ↓
Donor Record (CRM / donor file, confidential)
    ↓
Restricted Fund Tracking (FUND-REST-001)
    ↓
Reconciliation (monthly; FIN-CLOSE-001)
```

## **3. Handling by Form**

| Form | Handling |
|------|----------|
| **Cash** | Two-person count; receipted immediately; deposited promptly; logged in donation log |
| **Check** | Restricted endorsement at receipt; deposit log; copy to donation file |
| **Electronic / online** | System capture; match to donor record; verify the processor settlement: transaction cleared and processor fees recorded; net amount reconciled to the bank statement; chargebacks/refunds handled per rule 9 below; donor record marked "settled" once funds arrive |
| **In-kind** | Describe goods/services and fair market value (with donor acknowledgment for FMV > threshold); record value; treat per FUND-REF-001; acknowledge per IRS rules for non-cash contributions |
| **Restricted** | Record restriction exactly; track in FUND-REST-001; use only for stated purpose or return/convert with donor agreement |
| **Anonymous** | Log without name; no acknowledgment receipt issued for tax purposes beyond general policy; document decision |
| **Sponsorship** | NOT a donation — treat per FUND-SPON-001 (benefits provided in exchange) |
| **Event donation** | Link to Event ID (EVT-YYYY-###); acknowledge; deposit per EVT-REV-001 |

## **4. Rules**

1. **Identify the donor** whenever possible before acknowledgment.
2. **Record immediately:** donor, amount, date, form, restriction, campaign, acknowledgment status.
3. **Cash is counted by two people** and signed for.
4. **Deposit promptly** — do not let cash or checks sit.
5. **Acknowledge** per FUND-PROC-002 (required for most contributions over the IRS threshold; confirm current rules).
6. **Restricted funds are never commingled** with unrestricted funds at the reporting level (FUND-REST-001).
7. **No benefit in exchange** for a true donation (or the amount of benefit is not deductible). See FUND-REF-001.
8. **Quid pro quo:** if goods/services are provided, the acknowledgment states the value of what was received in exchange (confirm current IRS rules).
9. **Processor settlements and chargebacks:** reconcile each processor settlement to the donation log when it hits the bank statement (see FIN-CLOSE-001). Record processor fees as an organizational expense, not a reduction of the donation amount. On chargeback or refund, reverse the donation record, note the reason, and return/reverse any acknowledgment issued. Donations recorded before settlement are cleared to "settled" only when funds arrive; the same gift is never recorded twice (once as pending, once as settled).
