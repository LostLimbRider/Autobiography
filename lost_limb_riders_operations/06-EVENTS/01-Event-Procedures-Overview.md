## **1. Purpose**

This procedure governs every event: fundraisers, ride events, community gatherings, paid assignments, and volunteer events. Every event receives an Event ID, is authorized, is staffed with a plan, and is closed out with a financial result. No event ends with "someone handled it."

## **2. Event ID and Master Record**

- Each event receives an **Event ID** (`EVT-YYYY-###`) assigned from the Master Transaction Register.
- The Events Director opens an **Event Master Record (EVT-MSTR-001)** at the first serious request.
- All event documents — feasibility, budget, staffing, time records, expense reports, revenue logs, closeout — carry the Event ID.

## **3. When an Event Requires Pre-Authorization**

The event must not be accepted or committed to until **EVT-AUTH-001 (Event Authorization Checklist)** is approved when Lost Limb Riders is: providing labor, receiving money, entering a contract, incurring material expenses, or assuming meaningful risk. A verbal "sure, we'll help" is a commitment — follow the checklist first.

## **4. Financial Feasibility Requirement**

For **paid assignments** (organization receives money for labor/services), **EVT-FIN-001 (Financial Feasibility Worksheet)** is mandatory before accepting. The organization must know the economics before committing:

```text
TOTAL EVENT REVENUE
− EMPLOYEE LABOR
− EMPLOYER PAYROLL COST
− CONTRACTOR COST
− DIRECT EXPENSES
= PROJECTED EVENT NET
```

If the projection is a loss, labor is underfunded, or required documents (contract, insurance, permits) are missing, the event is **flagged** and must not proceed without an explicit decision by the approving authority.

## **5. Event Workflow**

```text
Request → Event ID assigned (EVT-YYYY-###)
    ↓
Authorization & Feasibility (EVT-AUTH-001, EVT-FIN-001)
    ↓
Budget (EVT-BUD-001)
    ↓
Staffing Plan (EVT-HR-001)
    ↓
Event Day (EVT-DAY-001; time records HR-TIME-001)
    ↓
Revenue & Expense Capture (EVT-REV-001, FIN-EXP-001)
    ↓
Payroll / Contractor Invoices
    ↓
Event Closeout (EVT-CLOSE-001) within defined period
    ↓
Postmortem / Lessons Learned (EVT-POST-001)
    ↓
Event record filed; transaction closed in register
```

## **6. Worker Classifications at Events**

- **Employees** — paid staff performing integral, supervised work. Paid through payroll (HR-PROC-004) with time records.
- **Contractors** — engaged per 04-CONTRACTORS (agreement + W-9 + invoice).
- **Volunteers** — unpaid, per 08-VOLUNTEERS. A volunteer is not a free employee; volunteers serve under the volunteer agreement, and labor performed for pay must be classified correctly (see CTR-001).

## **7. Closeout Deadline**

Closeout (EVT-CLOSE-001) is completed within **30 days** of the event (or per the contract), and the financial result is posted. Variances from the approved budget are explained in writing. The 30-day window is a Lost Limb Riders internal control; the Executive Director may grant an extension in writing, with the new deadline documented on the closeout. An event is not complete until closeout is signed and the transaction is closed (EVT-CLOSE-001).

## **8. Incident at an Event**

Any incident is reported immediately per 09-SAFETY-RISK (SAF-INC-001) with the incident form. The Event ID is cross-referenced on the incident record.
