## **1. Purpose**

This procedure turns every reportable incident into a tracked, documented, closed transaction with an Incident ID. Every incident is escalated, reviewed, and closed — nothing is "handled" without a record.

## **2. Incident ID**

Each reportable incident receives **INC-YYYY-###** from the Master Transaction Register and is logged in the Incident Log (SAF-REG-001). The Event ID (EVT-YYYY-###) is cross-referenced when the incident occurs at an event.

## **3. What Is Reportable**

Any injury, accident, medical emergency, fall, property damage, safety violation, near miss, altercation, harassment, discrimination complaint, or psychological distress event involving participants, staff, volunteers, or the public in connection with organizational activity.

## **4. Workflow**

```text
Incident
    ↓
Immediate Response (safety first; first aid; secure scene)
    ↓
Incident Report (Incident Report Form) within 24 hours
    ↓
Supervisor Review
    ↓
Risk / Insurance Review (SAF-INS-001)
    ↓
Corrective Action (assigned, tracked to completion)
    ↓
Board / Executive Escalation if required
    ↓
Closeout (log updated; lessons captured)
```

## **5. Detailed Steps**

1. **Immediate response:** tend to the person, stabilize the situation, protect the scene for investigation. Medical emergencies call emergency services first.
2. **Report:** the on-site lead or first responder completes the Incident Report Form within 24 hours (or immediately for serious incidents). Include facts only; no speculation. Medical details stay in restricted files.
3. **Assign ID and log:** Records & Compliance or Risk assigns INC-YYYY-###; log the entry in SAF-REG-001.
4. **Supervisor review:** the supervisor reviews the report, confirms facts, and identifies immediate actions.
5. **Risk / insurance review:** the Insurance / Risk Manager determines notification to the insurance carrier, counsel, or authorities; initiates claims per SAF-INS-001.
6. **Corrective action:** actions (training, repair, procedure change) are assigned with owners and due dates; tracked to completion.
7. **Escalation:** serious incidents (hospitalization, criminal conduct, media exposure, allegations against leadership) are reported to the Executive Director immediately and to the Board per policy.
8. **Closeout:** when all actions are complete, the log entry is closed with the closeout date and lessons learned noted.

## **6. Confidentiality**

Incident reports may contain sensitive personal or medical information. Completed reports are restricted records — store in the incident/insurance file (REC-LOC-001), never in this repository. Only the Incident Report Form template lives here.
