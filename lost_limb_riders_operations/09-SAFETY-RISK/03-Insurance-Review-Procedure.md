## **1. Purpose**

This procedure defines how the organization reviews insurance, notifies carriers, and handles claims — including the insurance check required before events.

## **2. Annual Insurance Review (Board)**

- [ ] General liability limits and endorsements reviewed
- [ ] Directors and officers (D&O) coverage confirmed
- [ ] Event and volunteer coverage confirmed
- [ ] Motorcycle/ride-related coverage confirmed per program
- [ ] Workers' compensation coverage confirmed (Iowa requirement)
- [ ] Property coverage for assets (AST-001) reviewed
- [ ] Cyber coverage reviewed (per Board decision)
- [ ] Carrier certificate of insurance obtained as needed for venues/clients
- [ ] Broker relationship current; renewal deadlines tracked

## **3. Event Insurance Check (per event)**

Before event authorization (EVT-AUTH-001), the Risk Manager confirms:

- [ ] Policy covers this event type, venue, and activity
- [ ] Certificate of insurance available for the venue/client if required
- [ ] Coverage limits meet venue/client contract
- [ ] Waiver/release requirements in place for participants

## **4. Claim Handling**

1. On any incident with a potential claim, notify the insurance carrier promptly per policy timelines.
2. Gather the incident report (SAF-INC-001) and supporting records.
3. Do not admit fault or negotiate settlement without carrier/counsel direction.
4. Track the claim number in the incident log (SAF-REG-001) and the incident file.
5. Update the claim status through resolution; record outcomes.
6. Provide records to the carrier as requested; preserve all related records under legal hold.

## **5. Records**

Policy documents, certificates, claims, and coverage confirmations are retained per CMP-RET-001 and stored in the insurance file (REC-LOC-001).
