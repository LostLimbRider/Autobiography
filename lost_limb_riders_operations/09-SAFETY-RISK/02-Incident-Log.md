## **1. Purpose**

The incident log indexes every reportable incident by Incident ID and tracks it from report through closeout. It is the starting point for any incident audit trail.

## **2. Log Columns**

```text
Incident ID | Date | Location | Program/Event | Type | Persons Involved |
Severity | Report Received | Supervisor Review | Insurance Notified | Corrective Action |
Escalation | Status | Closeout Date | Lessons Learned
```

## **3. Status Values**

- **OPEN** — report received, review in progress
- **IN REVIEW** — supervisor/risk review underway
- **ESCALATED** — executive/board or external notified
- **ACTION PENDING** — corrective action assigned but not complete
- **CLOSED** — corrective action complete, closeout dated
- **DISPUTED / LEGAL** — under dispute or legal review (adds hold under CMP-RET-001)

## **4. Usage Rules**

- The Insurance / Risk Manager maintains the log.
- Serious incidents are flagged and reviewed at the next board meeting (or immediately per policy).
- The log contains no medical details — those stay in the restricted incident file. The log references the file location.
