# Chairperson — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Chairperson serves as the primary leader of the Board of Directors and guardian of organizational governance. This is a volunteer board position requiring strategic thinking, ethical judgment, and unwavering commitment to Lost Limb Riders' mission.

## Core Purpose

Lead board governance and protect mission accountability through effective meeting facilitation, committee oversight, executive relationship management, and governance discipline. The Chairperson ensures the Board functions as a cohesive governing body while respecting the autonomy of executive leadership.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Board Leadership
- Set board meeting agendas in collaboration with Executive Leadership and committee chairs
- Facilitate board meetings with clear structure, inclusive discussion, and decisive closure
- Ensure all directors receive meeting materials at least five calendar days in advance
- Maintain accurate records of all board actions through coordination with the Secretary
- Call emergency meetings when necessary with reasonable notice

### Committee Oversight
- Ensure all standing committees function effectively
- Confirm committee chairs provide regular reports to the full Board
- Resolve conflicts or inefficiencies within committee structure
- Monitor committee work against adopted governance standards

### Executive Relationship
- Coordinate the annual executive performance review process
- Serve as primary liaison between the Board and Executive Leadership
- Address governance or policy questions from executive staff
- Provide counsel on executive transition planning and succession strategy

### Governance Integrity
- Protect the Board's fiduciary duty to the organization and its mission
- Ensure all Board decisions comply with bylaws, policies, and legal obligations
- Enforce the Board Code of Conduct and Conflict of Interest Policy
- Represent the Board in external communications when authorized

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Leadership:** Ability to guide a diverse board toward consensus
- **Judgment:** Strong ethical compass in governance matters
- **Nonprofit Ethics:** Understanding of fiduciary duty and accountability
- **Communication:** Skill in facilitating productive dialogue
- **Conflict Resolution:** Ability to address disagreements constructively

## Preferred Experience

- Prior board service on nonprofit or community organizations
- Executive leadership experience with governance knowledge
- Building and maintaining high-functioning volunteer teams
- Strategic planning and organizational change management

## Time Commitment

6-12 hours monthly, including meeting preparation, committee coordination, and executive oversight.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Supervises: Board officers and committee chairs (governance)
- Works with: Executive Director/CEO, committee chairs

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Volunteer

**Compensation:** Unpaid volunteer position. Eligible for expense reimbursement and organizational liability coverage.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, training and professional development

## Success Measurements

- Board meetings are productive, focused, and conclude with clear action items
- Committees are active and report regularly to the Board
- Executive leadership relationship is healthy and productive
- Board operates with integrity and legal compliance
- Directors report satisfaction with board leadership

## Key Relationships

- **Board of Directors:** Leadership, facilitation, accountability
- **Executive Director/CEO:** Performance oversight, strategic partnership
- **Committee Chairs:** Coordination and support
- **Secretary:** Records and procedural partnership

## Development & Support

- **Board Orientation:** Comprehensive governance training
- **Peer Network:** Connection with other nonprofit board chairs
- **Professional Development:** Governance and leadership training
- **Legal Support:** Access to legal counsel for governance questions
