# Secretary — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Secretary serves as the Board's record-keeper and governance documentation officer. This volunteer board position ensures the organization maintains accurate, accessible, and compliant official records.

## Core Purpose

Maintain official records and governance documentation through comprehensive minutes, proper notice, resolution archiving, and policy record management.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Meeting Documentation
- Take comprehensive minutes at all Board meetings
- Document motions, votes, abstentions, and recusals
- Record action items and assignments
- Distribute approved minutes within designated timeframe

### Records Management
- Maintain official director roster and contact information
- Keep bylaws and adopted policies in accessible format
- Preserve Board resolutions and formal decisions
- Archive and organize all governance records

### Notice & Communication
- Send proper notice of all Board meetings per bylaws
- Maintain meeting attendance records
- Coordinate with Chairperson on agenda distribution
- Ensure compliance with notice requirements

### Governance Support
- Support committee documentation standards
- Maintain policy adoption and revision records
- Assist with annual filing and compliance documentation

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Accuracy:** Precise documentation and attention to detail
- **Confidentiality:** Protection of sensitive governance information
- **Organization:** Systematic record-keeping
- **Writing:** Clear and neutral minute-taking

## Preferred Experience

- Records management or administrative governance
- Nonprofit board service
- Legal or compliance documentation

## Time Commitment

4-8 hours monthly, including meeting preparation, minutes, and records maintenance.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Works with: Chairperson, committee chairs

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Volunteer

**Compensation:** Unpaid volunteer position. Eligible for expense reimbursement and organizational liability coverage.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, training and professional development

## Success Measurements

- Minutes are complete, accurate, and distributed promptly
- Records are organized and accessible to authorized directors
- Meeting notices are sent per bylaws
- Policy and resolution archives are current

## Key Relationships

- **Chairperson:** Agenda and meeting coordination
- **Board of Directors:** Records and documentation service
- **Executive Director:** Coordination on operational records
