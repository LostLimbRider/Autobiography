## Director of Administration — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Director of Administration leads all administrative systems, records management, and operational support functions. This position ensures organizational efficiency, compliance, and smooth operations.

## Core Purpose

Lead administrative systems including records, calendars, document control, compliance tracking, and administrative workflows. The Director of Administration ensures that the organization's operational foundation is strong and efficient.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Records Management
- Establish and maintain official organizational records
- Develop and implement records retention and destruction procedures
- Ensure secure storage of sensitive documents
- Provide record access coordination with Director of Administration
- Support compliance audits and legal document requests

### Administrative Operations
- Manage office space, equipment, and supplies
- Coordinate facilities maintenance and repairs
- Oversee administrative technology and systems
- Manage vendor relationships (office supplies, equipment, services)
- Ensure safe, accessible work environment

### Calendar & Scheduling
- Maintain master organizational calendar
- Coordinate meeting scheduling (Board, staff, committees)
- Manage Executive Director calendar
- Resolve scheduling conflicts
- Support virtual meeting technology

### Compliance Tracking
- Track required licenses, permits, and registrations
- Monitor compliance deadlines
- Coordinate with legal and insurance advisors
- Document compliance verification
- Alert management to compliance issues

### Administrative Staff Supervision
- Recruit and hire administrative staff
- Supervise administrative team
- Conduct performance reviews
- Provide professional development
- Manage administrative department budget

### Administrative Workflows
- Develop efficient administrative processes
- Train staff on procedures and systems
- Continuously improve administrative effectiveness
- Manage document flow and communication
- Support Board and committee processes

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Organization:** Strong systems and process management abilities
- **Confidentiality:** Commitment to protecting sensitive information
- **Attention to Detail:** Accuracy in records and compliance
- **Communication:** Clear written and verbal skills
- **Technology:** Competence with office systems and software
- **Leadership:** Ability to supervise and develop administrative team

## Preferred Experience

- Office management or administrative operations background
- Nonprofit or healthcare organization experience
- Records management or compliance background
- Project management or process improvement experience

## Time Commitment

5-15 hours weekly (typically 20-30 hour position)
- Administrative operations and staff supervision (10-15 hours)
- Records and compliance (3-5 hours)
- Board support and special projects (2-5 hours)

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Executive Director
- Supervises: Administrative Assistant, Records Compliance Officer (if applicable)
- Works with: All department directors

## Success Measurements

- Office operations are smooth and efficient
- Records are organized and accessible
- Compliance issues are minimal
- Staff report positive administrative experience
- Board meetings are well-organized and documented
- Administrative budget is managed effectively
