# Director of Administration — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Director of Administration leads the organization's administrative systems, records management, and operational infrastructure. This position ensures Lost Limb Riders runs smoothly behind the scenes.

## Core Purpose

Lead administrative systems and operations through records management, office operations, compliance tracking, and administrative staff supervision.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Records & Documentation
- Establish and maintain official organizational records
- Develop records retention and destruction procedures
- Ensure compliance with records management policies
- Maintain master organizational calendar

### Office Operations
- Manage office space, equipment, and supplies
- Coordinate with vendors and service providers
- Maintain administrative budget
- Supervise administrative staff

### Compliance Support
- Track required licenses, permits, and registrations
- Support compliance documentation and filing
- Coordinate with Records Compliance Officer
- Maintain policy and procedure documentation

### Administrative Leadership
- Supervise Administrative Assistant
- Develop administrative procedures
- Support Board and committee meeting logistics
- Coordinate technology and systems needs

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Organization:** Systematic approach to administration
- **Confidentiality:** Protection of sensitive information
- **Systems Thinking:** Understanding of interconnected operations
- **Supervision:** Staff management and development

## Preferred Experience

- Office management or administration
- Records management or compliance
- Supervisory experience

## Time Commitment

5-15 hours weekly, with additional hours during peak periods.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Executive Director
- Supervises: Administrative Assistant, Records Compliance Officer
- Works with: All department directors

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Stipend

**Compensation:** $6,000–$12,000 annual stipend. Set by Executive Director and approved by Board during annual budget.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, workers’ compensation coverage, training and professional development, 5 days unpaid leave annually

## Success Measurements

- Office operations are smooth and efficient
- Records are organized and accessible
- Compliance issues are minimal
- Staff report positive administrative experience
- Administrative budget is managed effectively

## Key Relationships

- **Executive Director:** Supervision and reporting
- **Administrative Assistant:** Supervision and training
- **All Departments:** Administrative support and coordination
