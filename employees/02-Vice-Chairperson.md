# Vice Chairperson — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Vice Chairperson supports board governance continuity and serves as the Chairperson's primary backup. This volunteer board position ensures the Board functions effectively even in the Chairperson's absence.

## Core Purpose

Support governance continuity through Chairperson backup, special project leadership, director recruitment, and committee participation.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Consult with Chairperson on meeting agendas and governance strategy
- Assume all Chairperson duties when Chairperson is unavailable
- Lead special board initiatives and governance projects
- Support recruitment and orientation of new directors
- Mentor newer directors on governance responsibilities
- Serve on at least one standing committee

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Reliability:** Dependable backup and follow-through
- **Facilitation:** Ability to lead meetings and discussions
- **Strategic Thinking:** Understanding of governance and mission
- **Collaboration:** Works well with diverse board members

## Preferred Experience

- Board service or nonprofit governance experience
- Committee leadership or project management
- Community leadership background

## Time Commitment

4-8 hours monthly, including meetings, committee work, and special projects.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Works with: Chairperson, committee chairs, Secretary

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Volunteer

**Compensation:** Unpaid volunteer position. Eligible for expense reimbursement and organizational liability coverage.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, training and professional development

## Success Measurements

- Leadership continuity maintained when Chairperson is absent
- Special projects are completed on time
- New directors are effectively onboarded
- Committee participation is active and productive

## Key Relationships

- **Chairperson:** Backup and strategic partnership
- **Board of Directors:** Governance support
- **Committee Chairs:** Collaboration
