# Deputy Executive Director — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Deputy Executive Director serves as the Executive Director's primary operational support and potential successor. This position requires strong management skills, operational expertise, and readiness to lead the organization.

## Core Purpose

Support executive operations, coordinate cross-department initiatives, manage special projects, support chapter development, and ensure operational continuity. The Deputy Executive Director functions as both a trusted advisor and backup leader.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Cross-Department Coordination
- Facilitate communication and collaboration between departments
- Resolve conflicts and coordinate resource allocation
- Manage inter-department projects and initiatives
- Ensure consistent application of policies across organization
- Support efficiency and eliminate silos

### Special Projects
- Lead special organizational initiatives as assigned by Executive Director
- Manage organizational changes and transitions
- Coordinate new program launches or expansions
- Handle urgent operational issues
- Support strategic planning implementation

### Chapter Development Support
- Support growth and development of local chapters
- Provide resources and training to chapter leadership
- Resolve chapter operational issues
- Coordinate national/chapter relationship
- Track chapter performance and compliance

### Continuity Planning
- Maintain documentation of critical operations
- Know key operational procedures and relationships
- Be prepared to step into Executive Director role as needed
- Support transition planning and new ED onboarding
- Cross-train on major operational functions

### Staff Support
- Provide day-to-day support to Executive Director
- Manage Executive Director's calendar and priorities
- Represent Executive Director in meetings when appropriate
- Support staff with questions or escalated issues
- Help coordinate Board meetings and retreats

### Operations Efficiency
- Identify operational inefficiencies and improvements
- Implement process improvements
- Support technology and systems updates
- Manage office operations and workflow
- Develop and maintain operational procedures documentation

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Project Management:** Ability to manage complex projects with multiple stakeholders
- **Communication:** Strong written and verbal skills; ability to facilitate across teams
- **Operations:** Deep understanding of nonprofit operations and workflow
- **Problem-Solving:** Ability to identify and solve operational challenges
- **Leadership:** Credible leadership presence; respect from staff
- **Organizational Ability:** Strong systems and process orientation
- **Flexibility:** Ability to adapt to changing priorities and organizational needs

## Preferred Experience

- Nonprofit operations or program management background
- Project management experience
- Program coordination or administration
- Experience working with distributed organizations (chapters/branches)
- Healthcare, disability services, or community organization experience

## Time Commitment

Part-time to full-time (typically 30-40 hours weekly)
- Includes staff meetings, Board meetings, project work, special initiatives
- Availability for emergency response when Executive Director unavailable
- Flexibility for increased hours during major projects or crisis

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Executive Director
- Board oversight: Included in Executive Director performance conversations
- Direct relationships: All Department Directors; Executive Director's team
- If Executive Director unavailable: Reports to Board Chairperson

## Success Measurements

### Cross-Department Collaboration
- Departments report improved communication and coordination
- Inter-department conflicts are resolved constructively
- Projects involving multiple departments are completed on time
- Staff report better understanding of organizational priorities

### Project Management
- Special projects are completed on schedule and within scope
- Stakeholders report satisfaction with project outcomes
- New initiatives are effectively implemented
- Documentation and learning are captured

### Operational Continuity
- Critical operations are well-documented and understood
- Organization can function effectively if Executive Director unavailable
- Chapter support is timely and effective
- Continuity procedures are in place and tested

### Executive Director Support
- Executive Director reports satisfaction with operational support
- Priorities are managed effectively; nothing falls through cracks
- Executive Director has time for strategic work
- Board meetings and special events are well-organized

### Staff Effectiveness
- Staff report Deputy ED as accessible and helpful resource
- Escalated issues are resolved appropriately
- Cross-functional projects move forward
- Staff development is supported

## Key Relationships

- **Executive Director:** Primary relationship; close collaboration
- **All Department Directors:** Support and coordination
- **Board Chairperson:** Communication when Executive Director unavailable
- **Staff:** Accessible resource and operations support
- **Chapter Leadership:** Support and relationship management

## Authority & Decision‑Making

The Deputy Executive Director holds authority to:
- Manage day-to-day operations when Executive Director unavailable
- Make time-sensitive operational decisions (with ED input when possible)
- Approve routine expenditures within delegated limits
- Represent Executive Director in internal meetings
- Recommend changes to operations and processes
- Escalate issues appropriately

The Deputy Executive Director does NOT hold authority to:
- Make strategic decisions (Executive Director/Board function)
- Hire/fire or discipline staff (Executive Director function)
- Approve major expenditures (Executive Director/Board function)
- Make policy decisions (Board function)
- Represent organization publicly without authorization
- Commit to partnerships or agreements (Executive Director function)

## When Executive Director Is Unavailable

The Deputy Executive Director will:
- Assume all Executive Director responsibilities
- Make necessary operational decisions
- Consult with Board Chairperson on major decisions
- Maintain regular communication with Board leadership
- Keep organization operating smoothly
- Document decisions for Executive Director review

## Development & Support

- **Project Management Training:** Professional development in project management
- **Executive Development:** Leadership and management training
- **Nonprofit Management:** Ongoing education on nonprofit operations
- **Peer Learning:** Connection with other nonprofit operations leaders
- **Executive Coaching:** Access to coaching as successor develops
- **Succession Preparation:** Intentional preparation for potential ED promotion

## Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Meet with Executive Director to understand role expectations
- [ ] Review all organizational policies and procedures
- [ ] Meet with all Department Directors
- [ ] Learn financial systems and reporting
- [ ] Understand chapter structure and leadership
- [ ] Identify key operational relationships and contacts
- [ ] Review organizational systems and technology
- [ ] Establish regular check-in meetings with Executive Director
- [ ] Create backup operation procedures for critical functions

## Potential Transition Path

The Deputy Executive Director may be:
- **Internal candidate for Executive Director role** — trained and prepared as successor
- **Successor to specific department** — promoted to Director position
- **Long-term operations role** — supporting Executive Director indefinitely

The organization should discuss career trajectory with Deputy ED and establish clear expectations.
