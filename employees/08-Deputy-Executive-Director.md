# Deputy Executive Director — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Deputy Executive Director supports organizational operations and ensures leadership continuity. This position bridges departments, manages special projects, and steps into executive leadership when needed.

## Core Purpose

Support executive operations and ensure continuity through cross-department coordination, special project leadership, chapter support, and backup executive leadership.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Operations Support
- Facilitate communication between departments
- Manage inter-department projects and initiatives
- Lead special organizational initiatives
- Support chapter development and growth

### Executive Continuity
- Assume Executive Director duties when ED is unavailable
- Maintain documentation of critical operations
- Ensure smooth leadership transitions
- Support organizational planning

### Department Coordination
- Bridge gaps between departments
- Identify operational improvements
- Support department directors as needed
- Coordinate cross-functional teams

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Project Management:** Ability to manage multiple initiatives
- **Communication:** Strong written and verbal skills
- **Operations:** Understanding of nonprofit operations
- **Leadership:** Ability to lead teams and initiatives

## Preferred Experience

- Nonprofit operations or program management
- Project management experience
- Supervisory or team leadership

## Time Commitment

Part-time to full-time (30-40 hours weekly depending on organizational phase).

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Executive Director
- Works with: All department directors
- Back-up to: Executive Director

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Stipend

**Compensation:** $24,000–$36,000 annual stipend. Set by Executive Director and approved by Board during annual budget.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, workers’ compensation coverage, training and professional development, 5 days unpaid leave annually

## Success Measurements

- Cross-department projects are completed on time
- Executive Director reports effective operational support
- Organizational continuity is maintained during ED absences
- Chapter development goals are met

## Key Relationships

- **Executive Director:** Direct supervision and partnership
- **Department Directors:** Coordination and support
- **Chapter Directors:** Development and growth support
