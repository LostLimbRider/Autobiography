# Treasurer — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Treasurer provides financial oversight and governance for Lost Limb Riders. This volunteer board position ensures the organization's financial integrity through proper oversight, reporting review, and internal control monitoring.

## Core Purpose

Provide financial oversight and governance through monthly report review, budget monitoring, internal control oversight, and Finance Committee leadership.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Financial Oversight
- Review monthly financial statements and variance reports
- Verify accuracy of income, expense, and fund balance reporting
- Monitor financial health and flag concerns to the Board
- Ensure proper classification of restricted and unrestricted funds

### Budget & Planning
- Lead the annual budget development process with staff
- Present budget to Board with narrative explanation
- Monitor budget-to-actual performance throughout the year
- Support long-term financial planning

### Internal Controls
- Ensure segregation of duties in financial management
- Confirm that approval authorities are followed
- Review financial policies and recommend improvements
- Prepare for external audits or reviews

### Committee Leadership
- Chair the Finance Committee
- Ensure regular Finance Committee meetings
- Report committee findings to the full Board

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Financial Literacy:** Understanding of nonprofit financial statements
- **Integrity:** Commitment to accurate financial reporting
- **Detail Orientation:** Careful review of financial data
- **Oversight Mindset:** Governance perspective on financial management

## Preferred Experience

- Accounting, bookkeeping, or finance background
- Nonprofit treasury or board service
- Audit or internal control experience

## Time Commitment

6-10 hours monthly, including report review, committee meetings, and Board meetings.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Chairs: Finance Committee
- Works with: Finance Director, Executive Director

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Volunteer

**Compensation:** Unpaid volunteer position. Eligible for expense reimbursement and organizational liability coverage.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, training and professional development

## Success Measurements

- Financial reports are reviewed and understood by the Board
- Budget is developed on time with Board approval
- Internal controls are documented and followed
- Audit or review findings are minimal

## Key Relationships

- **Board of Directors:** Financial reporting and governance
- **Finance Director:** Day-to-day financial management partnership
- **Executive Director:** Budget and financial planning
- **Finance Committee:** Chair and facilitator
