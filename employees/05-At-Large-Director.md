# At-Large Director — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The At-Large Director provides independent oversight and mission support as a voting member of the Board of Directors. This volunteer position brings community perspective and helps ensure the Board represents diverse stakeholder interests.

## Core Purpose

Provide independent oversight and mission support through committee service, fundraising participation, community representation, and informed policy review.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Attend all Board meetings and actively participate in discussions
- Serve on at least one standing committee
- Champion the organization's mission within personal and professional networks
- Support fundraising goals and make an annual personal contribution
- Participate in policy review and strategic recommendations
- Represent community perspective in Board discussions
- Review financial reports and ask informed questions

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Judgment:** Independent thinking and governance perspective
- **Mission Commitment:** Passion for serving amputee community
- **Teamwork:** Collaborative board participation
- **Inquiry:** Willingness to ask questions and seek understanding

## Preferred Experience

- Healthcare, disability services, safety, or recovery community
- Fundraising, business, or community leadership
- Prior board or committee service

## Time Commitment

4-8 hours monthly, including Board meetings, committee work, and preparation.

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Serves on: One or more standing committees

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Volunteer

**Compensation:** Unpaid volunteer position. Eligible for expense reimbursement and organizational liability coverage.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, training and professional development

## Success Measurements

- Regular attendance and active participation at Board meetings
- Committee contributions are meaningful and productive
- Mission is advanced through personal networks
- Annual personal contribution is made

## Key Relationships

- **Board of Directors:** Voting member and governance partner
- **Committee Members:** Collaboration on specific initiatives
- **Community:** Representation and connection
