## At‑Large Director — Position Manual

## Position Overview

An At‑Large Director serves on the Board as an independent voice providing oversight, strategic guidance, and support for organizational mission. This volunteer position provides flexibility in expertise and perspective across the governance structure.

## Core Purpose

Provide independent oversight and mission support through committee service, fundraising support, community representation, and policy review. At‑Large Directors ensure that the Board represents diverse viewpoints and that mission integrity is maintained across all organizational decisions.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Board Governance
- Attend all Board meetings and actively participate in discussion
- Review meeting materials before each meeting
- Vote thoughtfully on organizational decisions
- Maintain confidentiality of Board deliberations
- Fulfill fiduciary duty to act in organization's best interest

### Committee Service
- Serve on at least one standing committee (Governance, Finance, Programs, Safety and Risk, Fundraising, or Chapter Development)
- Attend committee meetings and contribute expertise
- Support committee work and recommendations
- Report committee findings to full Board

### Mission Support
- Champion the organization's mission within personal networks
- Advocate for Lost Limb Riders in the broader community
- Support connection between Board work and frontline operations
- Provide voice for communities served

### Fundraising
- Support organizational fundraising goals
- Make annual personal contribution (financially meaningful to individual)
- Help cultivate major donors or sponsors
- Participate in fundraising events
- Provide introductions to potential supporters

### Policy Review
- Participate in Board evaluation of organizational policies
- Recommend improvements to governance or operational procedures
- Support informed Board decisions on policy matters
- Ensure policies align with mission and values

### Community Representation
- Represent Board perspective in external relationships
- Build connections with partner organizations
- Support Board understanding of community context
- Identify opportunities for partnership or collaboration

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Judgment:** Independent thinking and discernment in governance decisions
- **Mission Commitment:** Deep belief in Lost Limb Riders' mission
- **Teamwork:** Ability to collaborate effectively with diverse directors
- **Communication:** Clear voice in Board discussion; good listening
- **Accountability:** Follow-through on commitments and responsibilities
- **Respect for Diversity:** Openness to diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- **Community Connection:** Ability to translate between Board and community

## Preferred Experience (varies by Board composition goals)

**Preferred backgrounds may include:**
- Lived experience with limb loss, limb difference, amputation, or family experience
- Healthcare, rehabilitation, disability services, or safety expertise
- Veteran, recovery, or mental health community connection
- Fundraising, business development, or nonprofit experience
- Community leadership or civic engagement
- Law, legal, or compliance background
- Marketing, communications, or public relations expertise

## Time Commitment

4 to 8 hours monthly, including:
- Board meeting attendance (2-3 hours per meeting)
- Committee meeting attendance (1-2 hours monthly)
- Preparation and materials review (30-60 minutes before Board)
- Fundraising events and solicitation (as applicable)
- Committee or special project work (as assigned)
- Network engagement and community connection (ongoing)

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors (elected position; no direct supervisor)
- Board oversight: Annual performance review by full Board
- Committee chair: If chairing a committee, leads that committee's work

## Success Measurements

### Board Participation
- Director attends at least 75% of Board meetings
- Materials are reviewed and questions are asked
- Director participates meaningfully in Board discussion
- Votes align with fiduciary responsibility to mission

### Committee Contribution
- Committee meetings attended and active participation
- Committee work contributes meaningfully to governance
- Expertise is applied to committee decisions

### Mission Support
- Director demonstrates understanding of mission in Board discussion
- External advocacy reflects organizational values and direction
- Community connections strengthen organizational relationships

### Fundraising Support
- Annual personal contribution made
- Donor cultivation efforts yield relationships or contributions
- Fundraising event participation is active
- Support for fundraising goals is evident

### Governance Engagement
- Director complies with Board Member Agreement
- Conflict of interest is disclosed when relevant
- Confidentiality is maintained on sensitive matters
- Professional conduct and respectful relationships

## Key Relationships

- **All Board Members:** Peer relationships with equal Board authority
- **Chairperson & Officers:** Board leadership consultation and cooperation
- **Executive Director:** Understanding of organizational operations
- **Committee Chair:** Working relationship on assigned committee
- **Community Partners:** External representation of Board values

## Authority & Decision‑Making

At‑Large Directors hold authority to:
- Vote on all Board decisions (equal authority with other directors)
- Speak and participate in Board discussion
- Serve on committees and provide committee leadership
- Recommend actions to the Board
- Question organizational decisions and request explanations

At‑Large Directors do NOT hold authority to:
- Make unilateral organizational decisions
- Hire or fire employees or contractors
- Approve budgets or major expenditures independently
- Represent the organization publicly without authorization
- Override executive leadership decisions (Board decision required)

## Board Member Standards

All At‑Large Directors must comply with:
- Board Member Agreement (attendance, preparation, committee service, contribution)
- Board Code of Conduct (respectful behavior, no harassment, ethical conduct)
- Conflict of Interest Policy (disclose and manage conflicts)
- Confidentiality Agreement (protect sensitive information)
- Fiduciary Duty (act in organization's best interest, not personal interest)

## Development & Support

- **Board Orientation:** Attend comprehensive nonprofit governance training
- **Ongoing Learning:** Annual governance or nonprofit leadership training
- **Mentoring:** Pairing with experienced director for guidance
- **Networking:** Access to nonprofit director networks and peer learning groups
- **Resources:** Nonprofit governance toolkits, policy templates, best practices

## Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Attend Board orientation and governance training
- [ ] Review bylaws, policies, Board Code of Conduct, Confidentiality Agreement
- [ ] Meet with Chairperson and fellow directors
- [ ] Review recent Board minutes and financial reports
- [ ] Meet with Executive Director to understand operations
- [ ] Select committee for service
- [ ] Attend first committee meeting to learn committee work
- [ ] Confirm annual contribution commitment and amount
- [ ] Ask questions and establish relationship with fellow directors

## Term & Commitment

- **Initial Term:** Varies (typically two years)
- **Renewals:** Renewable for up to three consecutive terms (six years total)
- **Rotation:** After six consecutive years, director rotates off for minimum one year
- **Board Vote:** Elections held annually at first fiscal year Board meeting

## Common Board Responsibilities During Year

- When: Monthly | Task: Board meeting | Detail: 2-3 hours; active participation
- When: Monthly | Task: Committee work | Detail: 1-2 hours; committee-specific
- When: Quarterly | Task: Preparation | Detail: Review materials before Board meeting
- When: Annually | Task: Personal contribution | Detail: Make financially meaningful gift
- When: Annually | Task: Fundraising events | Detail: Attend and participate in 1-2 events
- When: Annually | Task: Committee commitment review | Detail: Confirm continued committee service

## Transitioning From Board

When leaving the Board:
- Maintain confidentiality of Board information
- Return all confidential materials
- Transition committee responsibilities
- Support onboarding of successor director
- Remain available for consultation (as agreed)
