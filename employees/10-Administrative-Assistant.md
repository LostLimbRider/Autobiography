# Administrative Assistant — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Administrative Assistant provides essential administrative support to organizational leadership, ensuring smooth daily operations and effective communication across departments.

## Core Purpose

Provide administrative support to leadership through calendar management, scheduling, correspondence, filing, and meeting coordination.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Maintain calendars for Executive Director and leadership
- Schedule meetings and coordinate participants
- Manage incoming mail and email routing
- Maintain organized filing systems
- Support Board and committee meetings with logistics
- Monitor office supplies and equipment
- Assist with correspondence and document preparation
- Support event and meeting logistics as needed

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Reliability:** Dependable follow-through on tasks
- **Communication:** Professional written and verbal skills
- **Organization:** Ability to manage multiple priorities
- **Technology:** Proficiency with office software and tools

## Preferred Experience

- Administrative support role
- Calendar and scheduling management
- Office software proficiency

## Time Commitment

3-10 hours weekly (typically 15-20 hour part-time position).

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Director of Administration
- Works with: All staff and departments

## Compensation & Benefits

**Category:** Stipend

**Compensation:** $3,000–$8,000 annual stipend. Set by Executive Director and approved by Board during annual budget.

**Benefits:** Expense reimbursement, liability insurance coverage, workers’ compensation coverage, training and professional development, 5 days unpaid leave annually

## Success Measurements

- Leadership reports effective scheduling support
- Meetings are well-organized and start on time
- Files are organized, accessible, and secure
- Supply levels are maintained efficiently
- Office environment is well-maintained

## Key Relationships

- **Director of Administration:** Supervision and reporting
- **Executive Director:** Primary administrative support
- **All Staff:** Coordination and support
