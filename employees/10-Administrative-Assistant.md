## Administrative Assistant — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Administrative Assistant provides essential support to administrative operations and organizational leadership. This position requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and commitment to supporting organizational efficiency.

## Core Purpose

Provide administrative support through scheduling, correspondence, filing, meeting logistics, and supply management. The Administrative Assistant ensures that organizational systems operate smoothly and that leadership can focus on mission-critical work.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Scheduling & Calendar Management
- Maintain calendars for Executive Director and leadership
- Schedule meetings and coordinate with participants
- Send meeting reminders and agendas
- Manage meeting room reservations
- Coordinate travel and logistics

### Correspondence & Communications
- Manage incoming mail and email routing
- Prepare correspondence and communications
- Maintain distribution lists
- Process invoices and vendor communications
- Coordinate document distribution

### Filing & Record Organization
- Maintain organized filing systems (physical and digital)
- File documents accurately and timely
- Retrieve requested documents and records
- Support records management procedures
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive files

### Meeting Support
- Prepare meeting materials and agendas
- Set up meeting spaces and technology
- Take notes during meetings (as needed)
- Follow up on meeting action items
- Coordinate Board and committee meetings

### Supply & Inventory Management
- Monitor office supplies and equipment
- Place orders and track deliveries
- Maintain inventory systems
- Manage equipment maintenance
- Track supply budgets

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Reliability:** Dependable and follow-through on commitments
- **Communication:** Clear written and verbal skills
- **Organization:** Strong systems and process orientation
- **Technology:** Competence with office software and systems
- **Attention to Detail:** Accuracy in administrative tasks
- **Discretion:** Ability to maintain confidentiality

## Preferred Experience

- Office administration or secretarial background
- Experience in nonprofit or healthcare settings
- Event coordination or meeting planning
- Database management experience
- Customer service excellence

## Time Commitment

3-10 hours weekly (typically 15-20 hour part-time position)

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Director of Administration
- Works with: All staff and departments

## Success Measurements

- Executive Director and leadership report effective scheduling support
- Meetings are well-organized and start on time
- Files are organized, accessible, and secure
- Supply levels are maintained efficiently
- Office environment is well-maintained
