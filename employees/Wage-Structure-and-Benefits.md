# Wage Structure and Benefits

## Compensation Philosophy

Lost Limb Riders is a nonprofit organization. Every dollar spent on compensation is a dollar not spent on mission. We pay fairly, transparently, and only when a role requires it. Most positions in this organization are volunteer. Paid positions exist only where the time commitment and operational necessity demand it, and they are compensated at rates that reflect nonprofit sector norms for Iowa, our stage of development, and our budget.

No one gets rich here. That\u2019s not the point. The point is the mission.

## Benefits

### Benefits for All Positions (Volunteer and Paid)

- **Expense Reimbursement:** Approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses related to organizational duties (travel, supplies, meals, etc.) are reimbursable. Requires prior approval for expenses over $100 and submission of receipts.
- **Liability Insurance Coverage:** All roles are covered under the organization\u2019s general liability and directors and officers insurance policies while acting within the scope of their duties.
- **Training and Professional Development:** Access to organizational training, conference attendance (when budget permits), and peer learning opportunities.
- **Community Membership:** All position holders are eligible for Lost Limb Riders membership and participation in rides, events, and programs.

### Additional Benefits for Stipend and Salaried Positions

- **Worker Classification:** Stipend and salaried positions are classified as employees (not independent contractors) unless otherwise approved by the Board and reviewed by counsel. Payroll taxes are withheld where applicable.
- **Workers\u2019 Compensation Coverage:** Provided for all paid positions as required by Iowa law and organizational insurance.
- **Paid Time Off:** Salaried full-time Executive Director receives 15 days PTO annually (combined vacation/sick). Stipend positions receive 5 days unpaid leave annually for absences beyond normal scheduling flexibility. No paid holiday schedule is established beyond normal operational closure.

### Benefits Not Currently Offered

The organization does not currently offer:
- Health insurance
- Dental or vision insurance
- Retirement plan or 401(k)
- Paid parental leave
- Mileage or vehicle allowance
- Housing or relocation assistance

These benefits may be added as the organization grows and budget allows. The Board shall review benefits eligibility annually during budget planning.

## Compensation Decisions

### Setting Compensation

1. The Executive Director\u2019s salary is set by the Board, documented in the employment agreement, and reviewed annually.
2. Stipend amounts for all other paid positions are proposed by the Executive Director and approved by the Board as part of the annual budget.
3. Compensation must be reasonable, comparable to similar nonprofit roles in Iowa, and supported by budget documentation.
4. No Board member may set or influence their own compensation. The Founder/CEO\u2019s compensation (if any) is set by the Board with independent review.

### Annual Review

1. Compensation is reviewed annually as part of the budget process (September\u2013October for the following fiscal year).
2. The Executive Director receives a formal performance review before any salary adjustment.
3. Stipend adjustments are based on organizational budget, role responsibility changes, and sector benchmarks.
4. Cost-of-living adjustments are not automatic. Any increase must be approved by the Board.

### Off-Cycle Adjustments

The Executive Director may recommend off-cycle stipend adjustments for exceptional circumstances (significant role expansion, retention need, budget surplus). Adjustments over 10% require Board approval.

## Time Tracking and Accountability

- **Volunteer positions** track hours informally for reporting purposes. Use the Volunteer Hours Tracking Form.
- **Stipend positions** track hours for accountability. Hours are not tied directly to hourly pay (stipend is fixed), but regular reporting ensures time commitment expectations are met.
- **Salaried position** (Executive Director) tracks hours for organizational planning and reporting.
- All paid positions must submit a brief monthly activity summary to the Executive Director (or Board, for the ED).

---

## Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Expenses must be pre-approved for any single expense over $100.
2. Receipts are required for all reimbursements.
3. Mileage is reimbursed at the IRS standard business rate (annual rate set by IRS; currently $0.70/mile for 2025\u20132026).
4. Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of the expense.
5. The Finance Director reviews and processes reimbursements monthly.

---

## Conflict of Interest

No position holder may participate in setting their own compensation, approving their own expenses, or evaluating their own performance. All compensation decisions must follow the organization\u2019s Conflict of Interest Policy, with recusal of interested parties.
