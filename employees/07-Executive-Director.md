# Executive Director — Position Manual

## Position Overview

The Executive Director serves as the chief operating officer of Lost Limb Riders, responsible for all daily operations, staff management, program delivery, and organizational effectiveness. This is typically a full-time position requiring nonprofit management expertise and operational leadership.

## Core Purpose

Manage daily operations, lead and supervise staff and volunteer teams, execute the Board-approved budget, ensure program accountability and quality, maintain compliance, and achieve organizational goals. The Executive Director translates Board strategy into operational reality.

## Primary Responsibilities

### Staff & Volunteer Leadership
- Recruit, hire, and supervise all staff positions
- Establish performance expectations and conduct annual reviews
- Manage compensation, benefits, and personnel administration
- Coordinate volunteer recruitment, training, and deployment
- Provide professional development and growth opportunities
- Address performance issues and misconduct

### Operations Management
- Manage daily operations of all departments and programs
- Supervise Department Directors and ensure accountability
- Coordinate inter-department communication and collaboration
- Troubleshoot operational issues and implement solutions
- Maintain organizational systems and procedures
- Ensure compliance with policies and legal requirements

### Budget Execution
- Execute Board-approved annual budget
- Monitor spending against budget with Finance Director
- Make budget adjustments within approved authority
- Manage cash flow and financial planning
- Support fundraising to achieve revenue goals
- Submit required financial reports to Board

### Program Accountability
- Ensure all programs operate safely and effectively
- Monitor program outcomes and quality
- Address participant concerns and feedback
- Maintain participant records and compliance documentation
- Support continuous program improvement
- Report program performance to Board

### Board Relationship
- Provide executive reports to Board at each meeting
- Support Board strategic planning and annual review
- Implement Board decisions and policies
- Inform Board of significant issues or decisions
- Seek Board guidance on major decisions
- Support committee work and governance process

### Community Partnership
- Maintain relationships with healthcare, nonprofit, and community partners
- Represent organization at community events and meetings
- Support partnership development by staff
- Address partner concerns and feedback
- Contribute to sector collaboration and learning

### Compliance & Risk Management
- Ensure compliance with nonprofit laws and regulations
- Maintain required licenses, permits, and registrations
- Manage insurance and risk management
- Address safety concerns and incident response
- Oversee data privacy and confidentiality
- Support audit and financial compliance

## Required Skills & Competencies

- **Nonprofit Management:** Deep understanding of nonprofit governance and operations
- **Supervision:** Ability to hire, manage, and develop staff effectively
- **Budgeting:** Financial management and budget administration skills
- **Strategic Thinking:** Ability to advance organizational goals and mission
- **Communication:** Strong written and verbal communication
- **Problem-Solving:** Ability to identify and resolve operational issues
- **Integrity:** Unwavering commitment to mission and ethical operations
- **Flexibility:** Ability to adapt to organizational changes and challenges

## Preferred Experience

- Executive nonprofit leadership experience
- Nonprofit program management background
- Experience managing diverse teams in complex environment
- Healthcare, disability services, or community organization experience
- Fundraising and revenue development experience
- Grant management and compliance experience

## Time Commitment

Full-time position (40-60 hours weekly depending on organizational phase)
- Includes staff meetings, Board meetings, community partnership, program oversight
- Additional hours during fundraising campaigns, strategic planning, or crises
- Travel as needed for partnerships and organizational representation

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Board of Directors (through Chairperson)
- Board oversight: Annual performance review by Board Chairperson and officers
- Direct reports: All Department Directors (typically 6-8 positions)
- Works with: CEO/Founder (if separate position), Finance Director, legal counsel

## Success Measurements

### Operational Excellence
- All departments operate effectively and meet defined goals
- Programs deliver quality outcomes for participants
- Compliance issues are minimal or resolved promptly
- Staff and volunteers report positive work environment

### Financial Stewardship
- Budget is managed and expenditures are within approved amounts
- Financial reporting is accurate and timely
- Audit findings are minimal
- Revenue goals are achieved

### Board Partnership
- Board reports confidence in Executive Director's leadership
- Board decisions are implemented effectively
- Executive Director reports are clear and informative
- Organization advances Board-approved strategic direction

### Staff & Volunteer Effectiveness
- Staff retention is strong (minimal turnover)
- Volunteer retention and satisfaction are high
- Staff performance reviews are completed annually
- Professional development opportunities are provided

### Community Impact
- Programs serve target population effectively
- Participant feedback is positive
- Community partners report effective relationships
- Organization reputation is strong

## Key Relationships

- **Board of Directors:** Accountable to Board; regular reporting and consultation
- **Department Directors:** Direct supervision and performance management
- **Finance Director:** Partnership on budget and financial management
- **Founder/CEO:** Close collaboration on mission and strategy (if separate role)
- **Major Partners:** Healthcare, nonprofit, community relationship maintenance
- **Staff & Volunteers:** Leadership, support, professional development

## Authority & Decision‑Making

The Executive Director holds authority to:
- Hire, supervise, and (with cause) terminate employees
- Make operational decisions within Board policy
- Approve expenditures within Board-approved authority thresholds
- Implement Board decisions and policies
- Negotiate contracts and partnerships (within authority limits)
- Establish internal procedures and systems
- Discipline staff for policy violations

The Executive Director does NOT hold authority to:
- Make Board decisions or policy changes (requires Board vote)
- Approve major expenditures beyond delegated threshold (Board function)
- Commit organization to major partnerships without Board approval
- Override Board decisions or governance requirements
- Determine compensation beyond Board-approved salary ranges
- Authorize fundraising commitments beyond delegated authority

## Delegation of Authority Framework

The Executive Director typically delegates to:
- **Finance Director:** Budget administration, accounting, financial controls
- **Director of Administration:** HR, office operations, records management
- **Program Directors:** Daily program operations, staff supervision (within functions)
- **Fundraising Director:** Revenue development and donor relations
- **Communications Director:** External communications and public relations

## Policies & Compliance

The Executive Director must ensure compliance with:
- Nonprofit governance laws and regulations
- Board-adopted policies and procedures
- Employment laws and HR regulations
- Participant safety and protection policies
- Confidentiality and data privacy requirements
- Financial management and accounting standards
- Grant requirements and funder regulations
- Insurance coverage and risk management requirements

## Development & Support

- **Executive Coaching:** Access to nonprofit executive coach
- **Professional Development:** Nonprofit management training and conferences
- **Peer Networks:** Connection with other nonprofit executive directors
- **Legal/Accounting Support:** Access to qualified counsel and accountants
- **Board Support:** Board provides guidance and strategic counsel

## Annual Responsibilities

- Develop annual operating budget with Finance Committee
- Conduct staff performance reviews
- Participate in Board strategic planning
- Submit annual report to Board and funders
- Manage annual audit process
- Update organizational policies and procedures as needed
- Lead annual organizational retreat or planning session

## Performance Review Process

- Annual review conducted by Board Chairperson and officers
- Based on achievement of organizational goals and Board expectations
- 360-degree feedback from staff and Board
- Compensation adjustment discussion
- Development plan for coming year
