## 1. Purpose

Volunteers are valuable and managed fairly: defined roles, consent, training, supervision, records, and safety — consistent with the Organization Handbook’s volunteer program.

## 2. Policy

- **Volunteer status** is distinct from employment: volunteers are not employees and receive no wages or benefits.
- **No coercion:** no one volunteers under threat of losing wages or employment; volunteers give time freely.
- **Roles and supervision:** each volunteer has a defined role, a named supervisor, and training for that role.
- **Onboarding:** volunteers complete VOL-FORM-001, review expectations and safety, and are logged in VOL-REG-001 before the activity (VOL-CHK-001).
- **Suitability:** role-appropriate screening applies, including VOL-APP-001 background check for roles with minors, vulnerable individuals, or financial access.
- **Minors:** minor volunteers require guardian consent and comply with applicable rules; roles are age-appropriate and supervised.
- **Safety:** volunteers receive the safety briefing (SAF-CHK-001); incidents follow SAF-POL-002.
- **Hours:** volunteer hours are recorded for grants and program reporting (VOL-REG-001); hours are not wages.
- **Misconduct:** volunteer issues are handled fairly; serious misconduct may result in removal. Incidents/allegations follow SAF-POL-002 and GOV-POL-006.

## 3. Records

Volunteer records (applications, consents, screening, training) are confidential per GOV-POL-002 and retained per REC-MATRIX-001.
