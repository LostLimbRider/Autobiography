## 1. Purpose

To apply role-appropriate screening so that volunteers placed with minors, vulnerable persons, or financial access are suitable, while respecting privacy and applicable law.

## 2. When Screening Applies

- **Standard** (all volunteers): application review, orientation, references where required.
- **Enhanced** (minors, vulnerable persons, drivers of participants, financial handling): background check and enhanced vetting.
- **Special** (roles mandated by contract, grant, or law): follow the applicable requirement.

## 3. Procedure

1. Identify whether the role requires enhanced screening (VOL-FORM-001 role selection).
2. Obtain written consent before any background check (as required by law).
3. Run the background check (state/national records as available and lawful); document the date and vendor.
4. Review results against the role’s requirements; make decisions fairly and document them.
5. Record completion in VOL-REG-001 and VOL-CHK-001 without storing unnecessary detail.
6. Retain screening records per REC-MATRIX-001, with access restricted per GOV-POL-002.

## 4. Confidentiality and Rights

- Screening information is confidential; used only for suitability decisions.
- Individuals are given the opportunity to explain adverse information per applicable law.
- No retaliation and no unlawful discrimination.

## 5. For-Credit / Court-Ordered Volunteers

Such placements require written arrangements, role definition, and documentation per the applicable agency; coordinate with the Volunteer Coordinator before start.
