## 1. Purpose

Fundraising supports mission programs, is honest, lawful, and donor-protective, and is recorded and reported transparently. This policy is consistent with the Organization Handbook’s fundraising procedures.

## 2. Policy

- **Mission fit:** all fundraising serves the mission; donor intent is honored (restrictions per FIN-PROC-006).
- **Honesty:** materials are accurate; the organization never misrepresents how funds are used or the tax treatment of gifts.
- **No coercion or pressure:** no high-pressure tactics; solicitations respect the donor’s wishes.
- **Conflict of interest:** fundraising activity (including solicitation of interested persons) follows GOV-POL-004; compensation of fundraising professionals follows GOV-POL-005.
- **Licensing/compliance:** raffles, games of chance, and other regulated fundraising require the applicable permits (see CMP-IA-001, CMP-IRS-001) before they run.
- **Processing and records:** donations processed and acknowledged per FIN-PROC-005 and FUND-PROC-001; recorded in FIN-REG-002; sponsorships per FUND-PROC-002 and FUND-SPON-001.
- **Professional fundraiser requirements:** engaging commercial fundraisers follows the applicable law (registration/contract requirements where they apply); confirm with CMP-IA-001 before engagement.
- **Annual reporting:** fundraising revenue and expenses are reported per the Form 990 functional-expense allocations (CMP-IRS-001).

## 3. Donor Privacy

Donor information is confidential per GOV-POL-002; it is not sold or shared except as required by law or as the donor authorizes.

## 4. Authority

Solicitations, campaigns, and fundraising commitments are approved per the approval matrix (FIN-CTRL-001); major campaigns are approved by the Board.
