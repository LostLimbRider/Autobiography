## 1. Purpose

To receive donations consistently: capture donor intent, record accurately, deposit promptly, acknowledge correctly, and reconcile.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Receive.** Collect the gift (cash, check, card, online, in-kind, pledge, matching gift). Record method, date, amount, and donor.
2. **Capture intent.** Note any restriction or campaign; record in FIN-REG-002 and FIN-REG-003 (FIN-PROC-006).
3. **Count and secure.** Cash counted by two people (FIN-POL-002); checks endorsed for deposit.
4. **Record.** Assign `DON-YYYY-NNN`; enter in FIN-REG-002; post to the ledger.
5. **Deposit.** Per FIN-CHK-004 within the deposit schedule.
6. **Acknowledge.** Issue FIN-FORM-006 per FIN-PROC-005 (≥$250 requires contemporaneous written acknowledgment; in-kind per FIN-FORM-008).
7. **Donor record.** Update the donor record (consent, preferences, contact) per donor-privacy rules.
8. **Reconcile.** Monthly: donations = deposits = bank; restricted funds match FIN-REG-003; report in month-end package (FIN-CHK-002).

## 3. Pledges and Multi-Year Gifts

Pledges are recorded (nonbinding acknowledgment of intent) and recognized per the accounting policies when the receivable is established; each payment received follows this procedure.

## 4. Refunds and Corrections

Donation errors/refunds are processed with documentation and approved per FIN-CTRL-001; the register and acknowledgment are corrected and cross-referenced.
