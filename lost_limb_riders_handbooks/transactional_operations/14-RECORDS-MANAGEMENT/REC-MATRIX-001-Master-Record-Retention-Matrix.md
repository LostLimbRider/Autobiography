## 1. How to Use This Matrix

- Retain for the **longest applicable requirement** when multiple periods apply.
- Retention generally starts at the close of the fiscal year or termination of the relationship, per the applicable rule.
- Where a period is shown as “per law,” the organization relies on the current requirement at the time; the compliance source log (CMP-REF-001) tracks verification.
- Litigation holds, audits, and investigations suspend retention.
- The periods below reflect general federal and Iowa requirements as understood at the effective date; **confirm current law and any professional requirements** with the organization’s CPA and counsel. Several entries require professional review (see completion report).

## 3. Notes and Professional-Review Items

- FLSA and IRS minimums differ; the organization’s policy is to retain **longer** where cost is trivial and value is high.
- I-9, medical, and certain tax records have specific statutory periods; the organization follows the longer of the rule and its policy.
- Destruction of restricted records uses secure methods (REC-SOP-002).
- Confirm current minimum periods with the organization’s CPA, counsel, and Iowa Workforce Development / Iowa Department of Revenue guidance.
