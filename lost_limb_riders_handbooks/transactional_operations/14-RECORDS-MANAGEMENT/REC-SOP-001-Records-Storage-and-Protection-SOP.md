## 1. Purpose

To store records securely, control access, protect confidential information, and keep public and restricted content separated.

## 2. Storage Tiers

| Tier | Content | Controls |
|------|---------|----------|
| Public repository | Policies, procedures, blank forms, templates, instructions | Review before commit; no completed records |
| Secure digital records | Financial, governance, program records | Encrypted storage; access-controlled; backups |
| Restricted records | Personnel, payroll, I-9, medical, donor, participant | Encryption at rest; separate folders; need-to-know access; audit log of access |
| Confidential records | Medical/accommodation files | Strongest protection; separate from personnel file; limited access |

## 3. Procedure

1. **Capture** — file each completed record into the correct tier per REC-REF-001 at the time of the transaction.
2. **Label** — name records with the transaction ID or record class plus date.
3. **Protect** — apply encryption and access controls; restrict access to authorized staff; maintain backups.
4. **Separate** — keep I-9s separate from personnel files; keep medical files separate; never consolidate sensitive information unnecessarily.
5. **Control access** — log access to restricted records; require approval for unusual access.
6. **Review** — the Records Compliance Officer reviews storage quarterly and storage + destruction annually (REC-CHK-001).

## 4. Digital Security

- Encryption at rest for restricted and confidential records.
- Strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication.
- Backups tested regularly.
- No sharing of credentials; no storing credentials in the public repository.

## 5. Physical Records

Where paper records exist: locked cabinets, restricted access, controlled key issuance, and secure storage against fire and water.

## 6. Confidentiality Breach

Any actual or suspected breach is reported immediately per SAF-POL-002 and is evaluated for notification obligations under applicable privacy law.
