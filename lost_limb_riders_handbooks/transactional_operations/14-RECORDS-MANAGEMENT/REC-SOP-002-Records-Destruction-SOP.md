## 1. Purpose

Records are destroyed only when the retention period has ended, no hold applies, and an authorized officer approves. Destruction is documented.

## 2. When Destruction Is Allowed

- The retention period in REC-MATRIX-001 has expired, AND
- No litigation hold, audit, investigation, or regulatory request applies, AND
- The record has no historical or grant value, AND
- An authorized officer (per FIN-CTRL-001) approves.

## 3. Procedure

1. The Records Compliance Officer identifies records eligible for destruction during the annual review (REC-CHK-001).
2. Eligible records are listed with: record class, date range, retention basis, and proposed destruction date.
3. An authorized officer approves the destruction list in writing.
4. Records are destroyed by the appropriate method below.
5. The destruction log is completed: what was destroyed, date, method, approver, and destroyer. The log is retained per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 4. Destruction Methods

| Record Form | Method |
|-------------|--------|
| Paper (confidential) | Cross-cut shredding or secure destruction service |
| Paper (non-confidential) | Shredding or recycling |
| Digital (confidential) | Secure erase / verified wipe; destroy encrypted media if needed |
| Digital (non-confidential) | Normal deletion after backup cycle |

## 5. Prohibitions

- No destruction during an active litigation hold, audit, or investigation.
- No destruction of records identified as needed by law, grant, or contract.
- No casual deletion of restricted records without the log.
