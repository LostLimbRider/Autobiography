## 1. Purpose

This calendar tracks every recurring compliance deadline for a calendar-year Iowa nonprofit with employees. **All deadlines shift to the next business day when they fall on a weekend or holiday — confirm each due date in the current year** (CMP-PROC-001). Tick each item off in the annual checklist.

## 2. Monthly

- Payroll run; withhold and remit federal deposits (FICA, FIT) and Iowa withholding per deposit schedule (FIN-PROC-004, FIN-CHK-001).
- Verify federal deposit schedule (monthly vs. semiweekly) is current per lookback rule (CMP-IRS-001).
- Sales tax collection/reporting for permit-required activities, if any occurred (per current frequency).
- Update registers: expense, donation, restricted funds, event, program (FIN-REG series).

## 3. Quarterly

| Deadline | Item |
|---|---|
| Apr 30 / Jul 31 / Oct 31 / Jan 31 | Form 941 (federal) — with 10 extra days if all deposits were timely |
| Apr 30 / Jul 31 / Oct 31 / Jan 31 | Iowa withholding return (Form 44-095) |
| Apr 30 / Jul 31 / Oct 31 / Jan 31 | Iowa unemployment insurance quarterly wage/report |
| Same quarters | Quarterly checklist CMP-CHK-002 |

## 4. Annual

| Deadline | Item |
|---|---|
| Jan 31 (Feb 1, 2027 for 2026 income) | Furnish and file W-2/W-3 |
| Jan 31 (Feb 1, 2027 for 2026 income) | Furnish and file 1099-NEC |
| Feb 15 | Iowa annual reconciliation (year-end withholding, W-2/1099 with Iowa withholding) via GovConnectIowa |
| Feb 28 (paper) / Mar 31 (electronic) | 1099-MISC filing |
| Jan 31 | Form 940 (FUTA) if Q4 liability > $500; deposit if due |
| Feb 1 – Apr 30 | Post OSHA 300A summary where applicable |
| Jan 1 – Apr 1 (odd-numbered years) | Iowa biennial report (Secretary of State) — 2027 is next due year |
| May 15 | Form 990/990-EZ/990-N (15th day of 5th month after year end) |
| Aug (this month) | Annual compliance review and checklist CMP-CHK-001 |

## 5. Rolling / As-Needed

- New hire: report within 15 days of hire (Iowa).
- I-9: complete within 3 business days of hire; monitor retention window (3 years after hire / 1 year after termination, whichever is later).
- Event with taxable sales or games of chance: sales tax permit + gambling license before event (EVT-AUTH-001).
- Professional fundraiser engagement: verify fundraiser registration with Iowa AG (Chapter 13C) before contract.
- Change of address/officers: file updated biennial report information with Secretary of State promptly.

## 6. Owner and Review

The Compliance Officer owns this calendar, confirms all dates against current guidance, and reports completion to the Executive Director. Dates confirmed as of August 12, 2026 (CMP-REF-001).
