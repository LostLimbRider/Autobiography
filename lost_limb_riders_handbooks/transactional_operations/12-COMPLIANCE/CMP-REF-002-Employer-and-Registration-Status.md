## 1. Purpose

This record is the live, authoritative answer to “what is actually filed and what is still open” for Lost Limb Riders as an employer and a tax-exempt organization. It supersedes the dated legal review queue that previously carried this status and went stale. This record is updated only through the change-control process (ADM-PROC-002), and only when the facts change **outside the repository** (a filing completed, an account opened, a carrier bound).

## 2. Status Key

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Complete** | Filing/action finalized and evidenced outside the repository; record updated to match |
| **Open** | Not yet done; required before first hire / first payroll / first restricted-fund acceptance as applicable |
| **Reconfirm** | Done previously; requires periodic reconfirmation (rate, threshold, or account detail) |

## 5. Pre-Hire Professional Reviews Still Required

These are not “filings” but are required before the first hire / first payroll. They remain **Open** until completed:

- Worker classification (employee vs. contractor) and exempt/non-exempt review by Iowa counsel or a payroll professional.
- Offer-letter / employment-agreement language review.
- Stipend and executive-compensation (insider) review against IRC §4958 excess-benefit rules (see GOV-POL-005).
- Harassment/EEO policy and employee-handbook review by Iowa counsel.
- Payroll setup review by a CPA or payroll professional before the first run.

## 6. How to Update This Record

1. Complete the action outside the repository (e.g., bind coverage, open the payroll account).
2. Follow ADM-PROC-002 (change control) to revise this file.
3. Move the corresponding row in sections 3–5 from **Open** to **Complete**, add the effective date and evidence reference (e.g., account number confirmation, policy number, filing confirmation), and log it in CMP-REF-001.
4. Update HR-CHK-001 (Employer Setup Checklist) to reflect the completion.
