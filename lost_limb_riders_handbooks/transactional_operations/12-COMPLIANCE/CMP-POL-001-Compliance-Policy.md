## 1. Purpose

To keep Lost Limb Riders lawful and in good standing: federal filings, state filings, employment obligations, fundraising rules, and records are tracked, filed on time, and verified against current law. **No tax rate, threshold, or deadline in this handbook is a legal guarantee** — current law is verified before each filing (CMP-REF-001).

## 2. Policy

- **Calendar discipline.** All filing and payment deadlines are tracked in CMP-CAL-001 and met. The Compliance Officer maintains the calendar and confirms each completion.
- **Current-law verification.** Rates, thresholds, and forms change. Before each payroll run, tax season, and annual filing, the current requirements are verified (CMP-PROC-001) and the verification is logged in CMP-REF-001.
- **Professional confirmation.** Federal and Iowa tax requirements are confirmed with the organization’s CPA/payroll professional before the first payroll run and annually. Legal matters are confirmed with legal counsel where this handbook says so.
- **No hard-coded numbers.** Where a figure must be current (rates, wage bases, thresholds, dates), this handbook states the verified figure with its verification date and instructs re-verification — it never treats the number as permanent.
- **Record keeping.** Compliance records (filings, confirmations, permits) are retained per REC-MATRIX-001 and GOV-POL-003.
- **Escalation.** Missed or at-risk filings are reported to the Executive Director and Board promptly; remediation and cause are documented.
- **Audit readiness.** All records must support inspection/audit per ADM-PROC-003.

## 3. Scope

Federal compliance (CMP-IRS-001): Form 990 series, payroll tax filings, information returns, I-9, records retention. Iowa compliance (CMP-IA-001): biennial report, withholding, unemployment insurance, workers’ compensation, new-hire reporting, sales tax, fundraising, postings, minimum wage.

## 4. Roles

- **Compliance Officer:** owns the calendar, checklists, and verification register.
- **Finance Director:** payroll tax, 990, and financial filings.
- **Executive Director:** accountability and escalation.
- **Board:** annual review of compliance status (CMP-CHK-001).
