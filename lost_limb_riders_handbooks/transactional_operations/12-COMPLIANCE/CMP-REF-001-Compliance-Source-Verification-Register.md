## 1. Purpose

This register records every live verification of the compliance figures used in this layer. **Each entry captures the fact, the source, and the verification date** so any auditor, CPA, or attorney can confirm currency. Re-verify before reliance.

## 2. How to Add an Entry

- New verifications are appended with today’s date and source.
- Superseded entries are marked “Superseded — see Entry XX” rather than deleted.
- Source = the URL, title, and publication (with edition/date) actually consulted.

## 4. Flagged Items for CPA / Attorney Review

- **1099-NEC reporting threshold:** current IRS guidance for certain payments made after 2025 may change the aggregate reporting threshold ($600 → $2,000 for certain reportable payments). Confirm the current 1099-NEC/MISC threshold with the CPA before the 2027 filing season.
- **990-EZ thresholds** are indexed and should be re-confirmed each year.
- **Iowa withholding deposit frequency** thresholds and **UI contribution rate** are organization-specific; confirm with the CPA and IWD.
- Any **gambling activity** (raffle/bingo) should be reviewed by counsel for Iowa Code Ch. 99B licensing before launch.
