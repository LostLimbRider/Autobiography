## 1. Purpose

This reference compiles Iowa obligations for Lost Limb Riders (an Iowa nonprofit corporation with employees and occasional fundraising events), verified August 2026 (see CMP-REF-001 for sources and dates). **Figures and rules change; re-verify before each use per CMP-PROC-001.**

## 2. Corporate Good Standing

- **Biennial report** (Iowa Code 504.1613): due between January 1 and April 1 of **odd-numbered years**. First report is due in the first odd-numbered year following incorporation; after that, every two years. Filed with the Iowa Secretary of State.
- Contents: name, registered office and agent, principal office, and names/addresses of officers.
- Failure to file can lead to administrative dissolution. Confirm current fee with the Secretary of State.

## 3. Payroll and Withholding

- **Iowa income tax withholding:** required for employers that must withhold federal income tax.
- **Withholding returns (Form 44-095):** filed quarterly, due the last day of the month following the quarter (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31). Due dates shift to the next business day when they fall on a weekend/holiday (Iowa Code 421.9A).
- **Filing frequency:** varies by withholding amount (monthly, semimonthly, quarterly). New/small employers generally file quarterly. Confirm current thresholds with the Department of Revenue.
- **Annual reconciliation:** year-end reporting and W-2/1099 submission with Iowa withholding done via GovConnectIowa; confirm current filing window.
- **File even if no tax withheld** for the period; verify current reporting rules.

## 4. Unemployment Insurance

- **Quarterly contribution and wage reports** (Iowa Code 96.7; IAC 871–23.8): due the last day of the month following each quarter (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31).
- **Must file even if no wages paid** in the quarter.
- Delinquency penalties apply (Iowa Code 96.14(2)).
- Confirm current contribution rate each year (varies by employer experience rating).

## 5. New Hire Reporting

- **Iowa law: report new hires within 15 days** of hire (federal standard is 20 days — Iowa’s rule governs). Report rehired and returning employees too.
- Report to the Iowa Child Support Recovery Unit via the Iowa Directory of New Hires (P.O. Box 10322, Des Moines, IA 50305) or electronically through GovConnectIowa.
- Contractors engaged for services are also reported.

## 6. Workers’ Compensation

- **Insurance required** (Iowa Code 87.1): every employer must insure workers’ compensation liability or qualify as self-insured.
- Evidence of compliance must be exhibited on demand (e.g., to the state).
- Motor carrier owner-operator exception: independent owner-operators are generally not employees (Iowa Code 85.61(12)(c)) — but confirm each relationship with counsel.
- Post the Iowa workers’ compensation notice for employees.

## 7. Sales and Use Tax

- **Nonprofits are not automatically exempt.** A 501(c)(3) is generally exempt on sales when net proceeds are used for exempt purposes — but **admission charges are generally taxable** and **gambling receipts are always taxable**.
- **Gambling (games of chance, raffles, bingo):** receipts subject to sales tax regardless of use; requires a gambling license **and** a sales tax permit; separate accounting required.
- Any organization making taxable sales must register via Business Tax Registration and collect/report Iowa sales tax on the permit-required activities.
- Confirm every event’s tax treatment before the event (FIN-CHK-002, EVT-FIN-001). When in doubt, a permit is required.

## 8. Fundraising and Solicitation

- **No general registration** requirement for a charity soliciting in Iowa.
- **Professional fundraisers** must register with the Iowa Attorney General’s Office under Iowa Code Chapter 13C and disclose the charitable purpose of each solicitation.
- **Sponsorship payments** made for actual services (marketing, tickets, meals) are not charitable contributions; quasi-contributions must follow FIN-PROC-005.
- Raffle/games-of-chance licensing falls under Iowa Code Chapter 99B (gambling license) — obtain before any raffle.

## 9. Employment Postings and Minimum Wage

- **Iowa minimum wage:** $7.25/hour (Iowa Code 91D; equals the federal floor — employers pay the higher of the two).
- Required postings (workforce.iowa.gov):
  - Unemployment Insurance notice — all employers
  - Your Rights Under Iowa’s Minimum Wage — covered employers
  - Safety & Health Protection on the Job — all employers (minimum 8½” × 14”)
  - No Smoking Iowa Smokefree Air Act — all employers
  - Employee Polygraph Protection Act (EPPA) — all employers
  - Equal Opportunity poster (EEOC) — all employers
  - USERRA notice — all employers
  - OSHA Job Safety and Health — all employers
  - FLSA poster — covered employers
  - FMLA poster — covered employers
  - OSHA 300A summary — posted Feb 1 through Apr 30 where applicable
- Post in a conspicuous place readily visible to employees.

## 10. Records Retention

- Iowa sales tax records: retain 3 years (confirm per statute).
- Employment tax records: retain at least 4 years after tax due or paid (federal standard; keep aligned with REC-MATRIX-001).
- New-hire/withholding records: per REC-MATRIX-001 and current Iowa guidance.
