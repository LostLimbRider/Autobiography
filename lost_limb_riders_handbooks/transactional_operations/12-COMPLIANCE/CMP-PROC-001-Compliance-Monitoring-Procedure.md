## 1. Purpose

Procedure for keeping compliance obligations verified, filed on time, and documented. Applies to all federal and Iowa obligations in CMP-IRS-001 and CMP-IA-001.

## 2. Steps

### 2.1 Calendar Maintenance (weekly)

- Compliance Officer reviews CMP-CAL-001 each week.
- All due dates are confirmed for the current year (shift to next business day when a date falls on a weekend/holiday).
- Deadlines are added to the organization’s shared calendar at least 30 days out.

### 2.2 Current-Law Verification (before each filing)

- Before each payroll run, tax season, and annual filing, confirm the current version of: rates, wage bases, thresholds, forms, and due dates.
- Use the sources listed in CMP-REF-001 (IRS, Iowa DOR, Iowa Workforce Development, Iowa SOS, Iowa AG, USCIS).
- Log every verification in CMP-REF-001 with the date, source, and page/URL used.
- Any conflict between this handbook and current guidance: current guidance wins; update the handbook at the next review.

### 2.3 Filing and Payment

- Prepare and review filings (prepare/review segregation per FIN-CTRL-001).
- Submit on or before the deadline; save the confirmation/receipt.
- Make deposits by electronic methods (EFTPS/Direct Pay for federal; GovConnectIowa as directed by Iowa DOR).
- Escalate any risk of missing a deadline to the Executive Director at least 3 business days prior.

### 2.4 Record Keeping

- File all confirmations and receipts per REC-MATRIX-001.
- Retain employment tax records at least 4 years after the tax becomes due or is paid.
- Retain compliance correspondence (including penalty notices) with the related filing.

### 2.5 Quarterly and Annual Review

- Run CMP-CHK-002 each quarter and CMP-CHK-001 each August.
- Present annual results to the Board; note variances and corrective actions.
- Use the annual review to confirm the next year’s calendar (including the odd-year Iowa biennial report).

### 2.6 Penalty or Issue Response

- Immediately notify the Executive Director and Board chair of any missed filing, penalty notice, or revocation risk.
- Determine cause, correct the filing, document remediation, and note the preventive control change.
- Engage CPA/attorney where the issue involves a filing already due or a penalty assessment.

## 3. Roles

- Compliance Officer: owns monitoring and the register.
- Finance Director: prepares tax filings.
- Executive Director: approves and escalates.
- Board: reviews annual status.
