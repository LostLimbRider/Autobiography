## 1. Purpose

This reference compiles federal obligations for a 501(c)(3) organization with employees, verified August 2026 (see CMP-REF-001 for sources and dates). **Figures change; re-verify before each use per CMP-PROC-001.**

## 2. Tax-Exempt Status and Annual Return (Form 990 Series)

- **990-N (e-Postcard):** gross receipts normally ≤ $50,000. Filed electronically only. Due the 15th day of the 5th month after tax year end (calendar year → May 15).
- **990-EZ:** gross receipts < $200,000 **and** total assets < $500,000 at year end.
- **990:** gross receipts ≥ $200,000 **or** total assets ≥ $500,000.
- **Failure to file for 3 consecutive years = automatic revocation of tax-exempt status.**
- Organizations eligible for 990-N may voluntarily file 990 or 990-EZ.

## 3. Payroll Taxes (per current year — 2026 figures verified)

- **FICA:** Social Security 6.2% each (employee/employer), wage base **$184,500** for 2026 (was $176,100 for 2025). Medicare 1.45% each, no wage base. Combined 7.65% each.
- **Additional Medicare:** 0.9% withheld on wages over $200,000 (no employer match). Use current threshold each year.
- **FUTA:** 6.0% on the first $7,000 per employee; credit of up to 5.4% for timely-paid state UI → effective 0.6% ($42/employee max). File Form 940 annually. Deposit if Q4 liability > $500.
- **Form 941** (quarterly): due last day of month following quarter (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31). If all deposits made timely, 10 extra days to file.
- **Federal deposits:** via EFTPS/Direct Pay (electronic). Monthly depositor if lookback-period liability ≤ $50,000 (deposit by 15th of following month); semiweekly if > $50,000 (Wed/Thu/Fri paydays → following Wed; Sat–Tue paydays → following Fri). $100,000 next-day rule. New employers are monthly depositors.
- **W-2/W-3:** furnish W-2 to employees by Jan 31; file Copy A + W-3 with SSA by Jan 31 (2026 income → Feb 1, 2027). Extensions via Form 8809 are not automatic and do not extend furnishing deadlines.
- **1099-NEC:** furnish and file by Jan 31 for nonemployee compensation (2026 income → Feb 1, 2027). Confirm the current reporting threshold with the CPA — for certain payments after 2025 the aggregate threshold changed from $600 to $2,000 (see CMP-REF-001, flagged).
- **1099-MISC:** paper by Feb 28, electronic by Mar 31 (other reportable payments).
- **Records:** employment tax records retained at least 4 years after the tax becomes due or is paid.

## 4. New Hires and Identity

- **Form I-9:** completed and retained for 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination, whichever is later; available for inspection within 3 business days (CMP-IA-001 has the stricter Iowa new-hire reporting deadline).

## 5. Contributions and Acknowledgments

- **Gifts ≥ $250:** contemporaneous written acknowledgment stating amount and whether any goods/services were provided (with good-faith value estimate). See FIN-PROC-005.
- **Quid pro quo** contributions above the threshold require a written disclosure of the benefit value.
- **In-kind gifts:** describe the property; the organization does not assign a deductible value.
- Organization’s 990 reporting requires donation and program revenue classification; maintain registers (FIN-REG-002).

## 6. Other Federal Obligations

- **Unrelated business income (UBI):** taxable, unrelated, regularly carried-on activity may trigger Form 990-T; confirm with CPA (Iowa parallel: Form 1120 if 990-T filed).
- **Employment posters** (federal): FLSA minimum-wage poster, OSHA Safety and Health Protection, EEOC Know Your Rights, USERRA, EPPA, and OSHA 300A summary (posted Feb 1 – Apr 30) where applicable. See workforce.iowa.gov combined poster resources.

## 7. Penalty Awareness

- Late 990: up to $20/day (higher for large orgs; indexed) and personal officer penalties possible.
- Late W-2/1099 and late deposits: penalties apply per form/amount; amounts indexed.
