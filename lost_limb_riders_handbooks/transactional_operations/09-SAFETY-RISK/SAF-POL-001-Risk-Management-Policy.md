## 1. Purpose

To identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks across events, rides, programs, volunteers, employees, and property — so the organization runs safe, well-planned activities and handles problems consistently.

## 2. Risk Areas

- **Participant/guest safety** (rides, concerts, events).
- **Staff and volunteer safety.**
- **Property and equipment** (vehicles, gear, venues).
- **Financial risk** (unbudgeted costs, fraud, restricted funds).
- **Compliance risk** (permits, taxes, filings, employment).
- **Reputational risk** (incident handling, media).

## 3. Risk Assessment Process

1. For each event/activity, complete the safety checklist (SAF-CHK-001) as part of authorization (EVT-AUTH-001).
2. Identify hazards, who could be harmed, and likelihood/severity.
3. Define controls and the person responsible.
4. Confirm insurance coverage and certificates before the activity.
5. Document and retain the assessment with the event file.

## 4. Insurance

Insurance coverage (general liability, event-specific, workers’ compensation where required, vehicle, directors & officers as determined) is confirmed before any event. The Board reviews coverage annually. No event proceeds without confirmed coverage.

## 5. Reporting and Review

- Incidents are reported per SAF-POL-002 and logged in SAF-REG-001.
- The Board reviews incident and risk matters regularly; recurring risks get corrective action.
- Serious incidents and insurance notifications follow SAF-POL-002 immediately.

## 6. Prohibitions

No activity proceeds without the required safety checklist, insurance confirmation, or authorizations. Alcohol/tobacco/cannabis rules and minor protections follow the Organization Handbook and applicable law.
