## 1. Purpose

To report, document, investigate, and follow up on incidents involving participants, staff, volunteers, property, or activities — consistently and promptly. This policy is consistent with the Organization Handbook’s incident reporting procedure and its Incident Report Form.

## 2. What Is an Incident

Any of: injury or illness requiring care; accident or near-miss; property damage or loss; unsafe condition; behavioral/security issue; allegation of abuse, harassment, or misconduct; or anything that could result in a claim, insurance matter, or regulatory report.

## 3. Reporting

1. **Immediate care and safety** come first (first aid, emergency services, secure the scene).
2. **Immediate notice** to the Event Lead/Supervisor and Safety Officer; to the Executive Director for serious incidents; to insurance if coverage is affected.
3. **Written report** (SAF-FORM-001) completed promptly (within the timeframe in the form) by the person involved or their supervisor; witnesses documented.
4. **Register:** log the incident in SAF-REG-001 with an incident number.

## 4. Investigation

- The Safety Officer (or designee) investigates; serious incidents are reviewed by the Executive Director and, where warranted, the Board.
- Root cause and corrective actions are documented; responsible owners and due dates assigned.
- Mandatory reporting to authorities (e.g., child/adult protective services, worker’s compensation, law enforcement) occurs as required by law; the Board is informed.

## 5. Confidentiality and No Retaliation

- Incident records are confidential per GOV-POL-002 and retained per REC-MATRIX-001.
- No retaliation for reporting in good faith (GOV-POL-006).
