## EVENT AND ACTIVITY SAFETY CHECKLIST

**Event ID (EVT):** ____________ **Activity:** ____________
**Date:** ____________ **Location:** ____________
**Prepared by:** ____________ **Reviewed by (Safety Officer):** ____________

### Pre-Event
- [ ] Risk assessment completed for the activity (SAF-POL-001)
- [ ] Venue/site inspected; hazards identified and controlled
- [ ] Insurance coverage confirmed; certificate obtained
- [ ] Permits/licenses confirmed (raffle, noise, special events)
- [ ] Emergency plan: evacuation routes, rally points, emergency contact numbers posted
- [ ] First aid kit(s) and trained personnel confirmed
- [ ] AED/defibrillator plan (if applicable) confirmed
- [ ] Weather/emergency procedures established (heat, storm, cancellation)
- [ ] Vehicle/safety inspection of any equipment or trailers completed
- [ ] Communication plan (radios/cell phones, contact list) confirmed
- [ ] Staff/volunteer roles and safety briefing delivered (including minors rules)
- [ ] Attendee safety briefing delivered as applicable (ride rules, waivers, route maps)
- [ ] Waivers/releases in place where required (see Organization Handbook)
- [ ] Restrooms/water/shade provisions confirmed

### During Event
- [ ] Crowd and entry/exit control in place
- [ ] Road/route safety for rides (marking, marshals, sweep vehicles)
- [ ] Alcohol/controlled substances policy enforced
- [ ] Incidents handled per SAF-POL-002 (first aid, reports, registration)

### Post-Event
- [ ] Site cleanup and condition restored; hazards reported
- [ ] Incidents logged in SAF-REG-001 and followed up
- [ ] First aid supplies replenished
- [ ] Safety checklist filed with the event file
