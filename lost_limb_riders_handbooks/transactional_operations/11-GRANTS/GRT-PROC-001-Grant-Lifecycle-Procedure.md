## 1. Purpose

Grants are pursued, accepted, spent, reported, and closed with discipline: authorized applications, restricted tracking, timely reports, and grantor-compliant closeout.

## 2. Lifecycle

```text
Identify → Authorize Application → Prepare → Submit → Award/Accept
→ Set Up (Restricted Fund) → Spend & Track → Report → Close
→ Retain Records
```

## 3. Procedure

1. **Identify.** Research opportunities aligned with mission and programs; check eligibility and grantor terms.
2. **Authorize.** Application approval per FIN-CTRL-001 (Executive Director; Board for major applications); record `GRT-YYYY-NNN` in GRT-REG-001.
3. **Prepare and submit.** Complete the application (budget per GRT-FORM-002) by the deadline; keep a copy and proof of submission.
4. **Accept.** On award, review the grant agreement; confirm the budget, reporting, and restriction terms; accept with authorized signature; enter in FIN-REG-003 (restricted fund) and GRT-REG-001.
5. **Set up.** Establish the grant budget (GRT-FORM-002) and a spending/reporting schedule; align the chart of accounts.
6. **Spend and track.** Tag grant expenditures to the grant ID; approve per FIN-CTRL-001; reconcile to budget monthly (FIN-CHK-002); do not overspend the grant line.
7. **Report.** Submit interim and final reports per the grant agreement on time (dates in CMP-CAL-001); file confirmations.
8. **Close.** On completion: final report accepted, remaining balances addressed per the grantor (return or spend per agreement), grant marked Closed in GRT-REG-001 and FIN-REG-003.
9. **Retain.** Grant records retained per REC-MATRIX-001 (often grantor requires retention beyond organizational policy — the longer of the two).

## 4. Controls

- Grant funds are restricted funds (FIN-PROC-006); never spent outside the grant terms.
- Grantor acknowledgments/report confirmations are filed.
- Grant-specific audit requirements are met; coordinate with the independent review (FIN-POL-001).
