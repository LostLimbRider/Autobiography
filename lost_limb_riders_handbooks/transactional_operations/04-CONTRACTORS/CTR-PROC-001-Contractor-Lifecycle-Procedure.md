## 1. Lifecycle

```text
Business Need → Classification Review (CTR-CHK-001) → Contractor Selection → Conflict Review
→ W-9 (CTR-CHK-002) → Written Agreement (CTR-FORM-001) → Scope of Work (CTR-FORM-002)
→ Rate and Payment Terms → Approval → Work Performed → Invoice (CTR-FORM-003)
→ Verification → Payment Authorization (CTR-FORM-004) → Payment → Accounting
→ 1099 Determination (CTR-SOP-001) → Closeout
```

## 2. Procedure

1. **Business need.** Document why an independent contractor is needed and the work to be performed.
2. **Classification review.** Complete CTR-CHK-001 **before** engagement. If the result is “employee,” use the HR lifecycle instead.
3. **Selection and conflict review.** Select the contractor; complete the conflict check (GOV-POL-004).
4. **W-9.** Collect and verify W-9 (CTR-CHK-002).
5. **Agreement and scope.** Execute the agreement (CTR-FORM-001) with scope of work (CTR-FORM-002), rate, and payment terms. Approval per FIN-CTRL-001.
6. **Work.** Contractor performs per the scope.
7. **Invoice and verification.** The contractor submits an invoice (CTR-FORM-003). The responsible department verifies the work was completed as scoped before approval.
8. **Payment.** Payment authorization (CTR-FORM-004) and payment per FIN-PROC-002. No payment before verification.
9. **Accounting.** Expense posted to the correct program/event/account; recorded in the contractor register and master register.
10. **Year-end review.** At year end, determine 1099-NEC reporting per CTR-SOP-001.
11. **Closeout.** Confirm the scope is complete, all invoices paid, and the register row closed.

## 3. Contractor ID

Each contractor receives `CTR-YYYY-NNN` from CTR-REG-001 at engagement, referenced on the agreement, invoices, and payments.
