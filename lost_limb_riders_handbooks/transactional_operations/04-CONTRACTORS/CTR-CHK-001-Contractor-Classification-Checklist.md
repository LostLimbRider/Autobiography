Complete this checklist **before** work begins. More “contractor” answers indicate independent contractor status; more “employee” answers indicate the person should be hired as an employee.

### Behavioral Control

| Question | Contractor | Employee |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Does the organization direct how, when, and where the work is done in detail? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Does the worker set their own schedule? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Does the organization provide training on how to do the work? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Does the worker perform work for other clients/the public? | ☐ | ☐ |

### Financial Control

| Question | Contractor | Employee |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Does the worker have an un-reimbursed investment in their own tools/equipment? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Can the worker realize a profit or loss? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Is the worker paid by the job (not by time)? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Does the worker market their services to others? | ☐ | ☐ |

### Type of Relationship

| Question | Contractor | Employee |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Is there a written contract describing the relationship? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Are employee-type benefits provided (health, PTO, pension)? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Is the work a core, ongoing part of the organization’s operations? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Is the relationship indefinite/continuous rather than project-based? | ☐ | ☐ |
| Does the worker perform key services for the organization personally? | ☐ | ☐ |
