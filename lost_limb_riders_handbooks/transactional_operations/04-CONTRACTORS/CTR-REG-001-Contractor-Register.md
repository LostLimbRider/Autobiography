## 1. Purpose

The Contractor Register assigns `CTR-YYYY-NNN` IDs and tracks every contractor engagement and its payments. Completed rows contain TIN-adjacent data and are maintained in restricted records (REC-REF-001).

## 4. Year-End

At year end, the register is the source for the 1099 review (CTR-SOP-001): total paid, form required, and filing status are recorded in the row.

## 5. Linkage

Each row cross-references invoices (CTR-FORM-003), payment authorizations (CTR-FORM-004), payment records (PAY), and the master register (ADM-REG-001).
