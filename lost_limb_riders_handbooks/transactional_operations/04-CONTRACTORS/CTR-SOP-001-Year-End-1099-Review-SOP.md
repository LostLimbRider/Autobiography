## 1. Purpose

At year end, the organization determines which payments must be reported to the IRS and Iowa, produces the correct information returns, and files them by the legal deadline.

## 2. Step 1 — Compile Payments

Pull all contractor payments for the calendar year from FIN-REG-004/CTR-REG-001 and the expense register.

## 3. Step 2 — Classify Each Payee

For each contractor, determine:

- **Total payments** to the payee for the year (non-employee compensation).
- **Type of payment** (services, rent, royalties, etc.) — 1099-NEC is used for non-employee compensation; other form types may apply for other payment classes.
- **Exclusions** that do not count toward the reporting threshold (e.g., payments to corporations in many cases — verify current IRS rules; payments for merchandise).
- **Threshold:** report payments meeting the current IRS threshold for the applicable form.

## 4. Step 3 — Verify W-9 Data

- W-9 on file for each payee (CTR-CHK-002).
- Name and TIN match; correct form type.
- Obtain a corrected W-9 before filing where data is missing or stale.

## 5. Step 4 — Produce and File

- Prepare 1099-NEC (and any other required forms) from the compiled data.
- Furnish copies to payees by the legal deadline (generally January 31 — confirm current IRS/Iowa deadlines).
- File with the IRS by the legal deadline.
- File with Iowa per CMP-IA-001 requirements (confirm whether Iowa requires a separate filing).
- File 1096 summary with the IRS as required.

## 6. Step 5 — Reconcile and Retain

- Reconcile 1099 amounts to payments in the registers.
- Retain copies and W-9s per REC-MATRIX-001.
- Log completion in CMP-CAL-001 with the confirmation reference.

## 7. Common Errors

- Missing the threshold determination for corporations (verify current IRS rule).
- Paying from employee records by mistake (misclassification).
- Failing to obtain a W-9 (backup withholding and penalties).
- Late filing (penalties apply).

**Confirm current forms, thresholds, and deadlines annually with the CPA/payroll professional.**
