## 5. Maintenance

- New forms are created in their department folder with ID `DEPT-FORM-NNN` and added to this index.
- Forms are never renamed without updating every procedure that references them (validator enforces cross-references, CMP-PROC-001).
- Obsolete forms are marked Superseded in the index and retired per GOV-POL-003.
