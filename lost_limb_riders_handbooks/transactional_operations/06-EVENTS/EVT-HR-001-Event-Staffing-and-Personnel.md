## 1. Purpose

To staff events legally and safely: employees are paid and tracked correctly; volunteers are managed per the volunteer policy; minors are handled per applicable rules; no one works outside their approved role without authorization.

## 2. Staffing Plan

The event staffing plan (with EVT-AUTH-001) identifies:

- **Employees:** roles, hours, rates, and schedule. Time is recorded per HR-SOP-005 (HR-TIME-001); overtime per applicable law; all hours feed payroll (FIN-PROC-004).
- **Volunteers:** roles, shifts, registration, and supervision per VOL-POL-001.
- **Contractors** (performers, security, vendors): per CTR-POL-001 with executed agreements.
- **Minors:** any minor roles are approved in advance, comply with child labor rules, and are supervised per policy.
- **First aid / safety roles:** trained personnel per SAF-POL-001.

## 3. Records

- Time records are approved by the event lead and submitted per the payroll calendar.
- Volunteer hours are recorded per VOL-POL-001 (not as wages).
- Contractor invoices are paid per CTR-FORM-004 / FIN-PROC-002.

## 4. Closeout

Personnel-related items (final time, volunteer hours, contractor payments) are settled in EVT-CLOSE-001.
