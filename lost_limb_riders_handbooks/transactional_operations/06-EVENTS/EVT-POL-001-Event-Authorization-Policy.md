## 1. Purpose

No event occurs without authorization: scope, purpose, dates, venue, budget, revenue projections, staffing, safety, volunteer needs, insurance, and financial risk are approved before commitment. This implements the Organization Handbook’s event planning procedures.

## 2. Authorization

- Every event is authorized via **EVT-AUTH-001** before financial or contractual commitment.
- **Board-level authorization** is required for events with material financial risk, unbudgeted cost, or Board-level exposure (per GOV-SOP-001 thresholds).
- **Executive Director + Events Director** authorize routine events within the approved event budget per FIN-CTRL-001.

## 3. Required Components

Each event package includes: purpose/mission fit, dates/venue, budget and revenue projection (EVT-FIN-001), staffing plan (EVT-HR-001), volunteer plan (VOL-POL-001), safety and risk plan (SAF-POL-001), insurance confirmation, contracts, and donation/sponsorship expectations.

## 4. Financial Feasibility

EVT-FIN-001 must show projected revenue vs. expenses; the event runs within its approved budget. Deviations require re-approval per the approval matrix.

## 5. Restriction and Compliance

Event revenue and expenses are tracked per the event register (EVT-REG-001) and closed out per EVT-CLOSE-001. Event raffles/games of chance require the applicable permits; see CMP-IA-001 and CMP-IRS-001.
