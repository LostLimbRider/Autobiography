## 1. Purpose

To confirm before commitment that an event is financially viable and to track event finances to closeout.

## 2. Pre-Approval Feasibility

1. Prepare EVT-FORM-001 (Event Budget): itemized expenses and revenue by source, with assumptions.
2. Determine the break-even point and projected net.
3. Identify cash-flow needs (advances, deposits) and funding source.
4. Identify restricted/grant funding constraints on the event.
5. Attach to EVT-AUTH-001 for authorization per FIN-CTRL-001.

## 3. During the Event

- Revenue and expenses are recorded against the event’s budget as incurred (EVT-REG-001, EVT-FORM-001).
- Significant deviations from budget are reported and re-approved per the approval matrix.

## 4. After the Event

1. Complete EVT-FORM-002 (Event Financial Summary) within the closeout timeframe (EVT-CLOSE-001).
2. Reconcile: revenue recorded = tickets/sales/deposits; expenses approved and paid; all event-specific money accounted for.
3. Reconcile restricted/grant funds used for the event.
4. Report results to Finance Committee/Board in the month-end package (FIN-CHK-002).
5. Close the event in the register (EVT-REG-001) and master register (ADM-REG-001).
