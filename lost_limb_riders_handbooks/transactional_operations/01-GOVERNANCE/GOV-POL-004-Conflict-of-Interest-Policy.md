## 1. Purpose

Lost Limb Riders is a 501(c)(3) public charity in formation/operation. Federal law prohibits **private inurement** and restricts **private benefit and excess benefit transactions**. This policy ensures that people in positions of influence cannot steer organizational money or property to themselves, their relatives, or their businesses, and that any transaction with an interested person is authorized, documented, and at arm’s length.

## 2. Definitions

- **Interested person:** any director, officer, key employee, or member of a committee with governing powers, plus their family members and entities they own or control.
- **Financial interest:** any direct or indirect interest in a contract, purchase, grant, employment, compensation arrangement, or other transaction involving the organization.
- **Excess benefit transaction:** a transaction in which an economic benefit is provided to or for the use of an interested person in excess of the fair value of what the organization receives in return.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- employment and compensation of directors, officers, and key employees (see GOV-POL-005);
- purchases and contracts with interested persons;
- sponsorship, grants, and donations that benefit an interested person;
- hiring, promotion, and contractor selection involving relatives;
- any transaction where the interested person participates in the decision on their own interest.

## 4. Duty to Disclose

Any interested person who has a financial interest in a proposed transaction must disclose the interest **before** discussion and voting, in writing, and the disclosure is recorded in the transaction record and the board minutes.

## 5. Process for Interested-Person Transactions

1. The interested person discloses the financial interest.
2. The interested person **leaves** the discussion and does not vote.
3. The remaining decision-makers determine whether the transaction is fair, reasonable, and in the organization’s best interest, using comparability data where the amount is material.
4. The decision is documented in the transaction record, including the basis for approval.
5. The decision is recorded in GOV-REG-001 (Board Action Register) and the board minutes.

## 6. Compensation of Interested Persons

Compensation of directors, officers, and key employees follows GOV-POL-005. No interested person participates in setting or approving their own compensation.

## 7. Annual Disclosure

Directors, officers, and key employees complete an annual conflict-of-interest disclosure, which is reviewed by the Board and retained in governance records.

## 8. Violations

An undisclosed conflict or an unauthorized interested-person transaction is a control failure. It is reported to the Board, remediated (including possible repayment or IRS Form 1023/990 reporting), and addressed per applicable law and the disciplinary procedure (HR-SOP-007).

## 9. External Review

Where a transaction raises questions of fair value, excess benefit, or inurement, the Board obtains an independent comparability review or professional advice before acting.
