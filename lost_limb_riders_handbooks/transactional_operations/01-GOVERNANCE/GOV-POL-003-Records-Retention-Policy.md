## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the organization’s obligation to retain records for defined periods and to destroy them securely when retention ends.

## 2. Retention Authority

The Master Retention Matrix (REC-MATRIX-001) is the single source of truth for retention periods. It lists each record class, its owner, location, retention period, destruction method, and legal basis or source.

## 3. Longest-Applicable Requirement

Where retention periods differ across federal law, Iowa law, grant requirements, contractual terms, and insurance requirements, the organization retains for the **longest applicable requirement** unless legal counsel directs otherwise.

## 4. Starting Point

- Financial records generally retain from the end of the fiscal year to which they relate.
- Employment records generally retain from the end of the employment relationship or the record date, whichever the applicable rule specifies.
- Grant records retain for the award period plus the period required by the funder or by law.
- Litigation holds suspend all retention for records related to pending or reasonably anticipated legal matters.

## 5. Prohibition on Early Destruction

No record is destroyed before its retention period ends. No record subject to a litigation hold, audit, investigation, or regulatory request is destroyed until the matter concludes.

## 6. Secure Destruction

Destruction follows REC-SOP-002. Confidential records are shredded or securely wiped; a destruction log records what was destroyed, when, by whom, and under whose authority.

## 7. Annual Review

The Records Compliance Officer reviews the retention matrix and records inventory annually (REC-CHK-001) and arranges authorized destruction.

## 8. Departures

Any retention departure requires written approval by the Executive Director and Records Compliance Officer, with the reason documented.
