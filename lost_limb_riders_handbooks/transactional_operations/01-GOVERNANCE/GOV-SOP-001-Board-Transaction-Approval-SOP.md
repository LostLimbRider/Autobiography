## 1. When Board Approval Is Required

Per the approval matrix (FIN-CTRL-001), Board approval is required for:

- expenditures above the Board threshold;
- unbudgeted long-term obligations;
- compensation of insiders, officers, and key employees (GOV-POL-005);
- interested-person transactions (GOV-POL-004);
- entering or amending major contracts;
- grants and sponsorship agreements at Board level;
- asset acquisitions and disposals above the Board threshold;
- budget adoption and material budget amendments;
- any item the Executive Director or Finance Committee elevates to the Board.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Package.** The requesting department prepares the transaction package: description, purpose, amount, funding source, related transaction IDs, and supporting documents (feasibility, comparability, quotes, contract draft).
2. **Review.** The Finance Committee or Executive Director reviews the package for completeness and compliance.
3. **Conflict check.** GOV-POL-004 conflict review is performed; interested persons disclose and withdraw from discussion and voting.
4. **Presentation.** The package is presented at a regular or special Board meeting, or by unanimous written consent if permitted by the Bylaws.
5. **Decision.** The Board approves, denies, defers, or amends. The decision and basis are recorded in minutes.
6. **Record.** The action is logged in GOV-REG-001 with a Board Action ID, and the transaction register is updated with the authorization reference.
7. **Execute.** The responsible department proceeds only after authorization, citing the Board Action ID.

## 3. Emergency Authorization

True emergencies (threat to health, safety, or imminent material loss) may be authorized by the Executive Director within the emergency-expenditure limit in FIN-CTRL-001 and reported to the Board at the next meeting for ratification.

## 4. Documentation Requirements

- Board Action ID on every downstream document
- Minutes excerpt or resolution reference
- Conflict disclosures
- Comparability or quotes where required

## 5. Closeout

The transaction’s closeout (per its department checklist) references the Board Action ID, completing the audit trail.
