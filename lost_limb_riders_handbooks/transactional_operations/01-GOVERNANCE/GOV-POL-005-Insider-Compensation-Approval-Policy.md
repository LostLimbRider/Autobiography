## 1. Purpose

The organization establishes **reasonable compensation for actual services**. No founder, officer, director, or key employee sets or approves their own pay. This policy prevents private inurement while making legitimate employment possible.

## 2. Organizational Principle

Compensation for legitimate work is an **organizational operating expense**, not a distribution of nonprofit earnings. Lost Limb Riders is permitted to employ people and pay reasonable wages and salaries for services actually performed.

## 3. Who Is Covered

- Founder/CEO
- Executive Director and Deputy Executive Director
- All directors and officers
- Key employees (anyone who earns above the applicable IRS threshold for a 990 reporting year or who exercises substantial influence)
- Family members of the above

## 4. When Approval Is Required

Compensation for covered persons requires **advance approval by the disinterested members of the Board**, evidenced by a board resolution recorded in GOV-REG-001, before the pay arrangement takes effect.

## 5. The Compensation Worksheet

Every covered position is documented on HR-FORM-005 (Position Compensation Worksheet) with:

- position, duties, and authority;
- expected hours and employment classification;
- geographic market and comparable organizations/positions;
- compensation sources and comparability data;
- proposed rate or salary, benefits, and total compensation;
- effective date;
- conflict status and disclosure;
- approving authority and board resolution reference.

## 6. Comparability

Before approving material compensation, the Board obtains comparability data from three or more sources: industry salary surveys, comparable nonprofit compensation databases, published wage data, or written comparability studies. This creates a **contemporaneous written record** that the compensation is reasonable.

## 7. Process

1. Compensation proposal is prepared on HR-FORM-005 with comparability data.
2. The interested person discloses and withdraws from discussion and voting.
3. Disinterested Board members review and vote; the outcome is recorded in minutes and GOV-REG-001.
4. The approved amount, effective date, and resolution reference are filed in the personnel file and finance records.
5. Compensation is reviewed at least annually and upon any material change in duties.

## 8. No Self-Authorization

The following is **prohibited**: a founder, officer, director, or key employee deciding their own compensation, drawing a salary without an approved worksheet, or receiving payment for duties not actually performed and documented.

## 9. Rebuttable Presumption

Following the process above (approval by disinterested directors, prior to payment, with documented comparability) creates the IRS **rebuttable presumption of reasonableness** for the approved amount, strengthening the organization’s excess-benefit protection.

## 10. External Review

The Board may obtain a CPA or compensation professional review for high-value or contested arrangements.
