## 1. Purpose

This SOP governs how Lost Limb Riders places, receives, supervises, verifies, and closes individuals who perform work or service for the organization through **work-study, community service, court-ordered service, internships, practicums, workforce or work-experience programs, vocational programs, school programs, agency referrals, or any other comparable arrangement** (a “special placement”).

The goal is that any responsible staff member can take a special placement from the first contact through record closure **without inventing a step**.

## 2. Scope and Applicability

- Applies to any individual seeking or performing work or service via work-study, community service, court-ordered service, an internship, a practicum, a workforce program, a work-experience program, a vocational program, a school program, or an agency referral.
- Applies to the Placement Coordinator, all supervisors, program leaders, and organizational leadership who interact with special-placements.
- This SOP does **not** govern ordinary volunteers (see VOL-PROC-001) or employees (see the HR packet). Where a person is classified as an employee or an ordinary volunteer, this SOP does not apply and the relevant process governs.

## 6. Classification Process

Determine and document the correct category **before** any duty begins. Classification follows the actual relationship and governing law (HR-POL-001, PLP-POL-001). The Placement Coordinator completes PLP-FORM-002.

1. If the person occupies an authorized, paid, supervised, ongoing position and meets the employee criteria → **employee** (refer to HR-EMP-001; do not use this SOP).
2. Otherwise, if the person is undertaking ordinary unpaid volunteer service with no sponsoring program/obligation → **volunteer** (refer to VOL-PROC-001).
3. Otherwise, classify as the appropriate special placement type using PLP-FORM-002 checks:
   - work-study; intern; practicum; community service (court vs. school vs. agency); workforce/work-experience; vocational; other agency placement.
4. **Escalation:** If classification is uncertain, if the person appears to be filling a paid staff role, or if the arrangement may constitute employment, the Placement Coordinator must escalate to the Executive Director (and obtain legal guidance where warranted) **before** the person performs any work. No uncertain classification may be guessed.

### Classification Decision Tree

- Is the person in an authorized paid position? → Yes: **EMPLOYEE** (HR packet). Stop.
- Is the person performing unpaid service with no sponsoring obligation? → Yes: **VOLUNTEER** (VOL-PROC-001). Stop.
- Does the service satisfy a work-study, community-service, court, internship, practicum, workforce, or agency requirement? → Yes: **SPECIAL PLACEMENT** (this SOP).
- Is there compensation or does the role replace a staff position? → Yes: escalate (possible employee). Do not proceed until resolved.

## 7. Initial Intake

1. **Who receives:** Reception or Placement Coordinator receives the request (in person, phone, email, web, or referral).
2. **Collect:** Complete PLP-FORM-001 with name, contact, sponsoring organization, placement type, requested purpose, duties, schedule, hours, and required documentation.
3. **Requested documentation:** Program/agency authorization letter, court order or referral, work-study authorization, school or program form, and any release or consent.
4. **Questions to ask:** What external program must be satisfied? How many hours and by when? Who is the external contact? Is compensation involved? What does the sponsoring program require of the organization?
5. **Verify:** identity and that the request comes from a legitimate program/agency (see §8).
6. **Record:** File PLP-FORM-001 and route to the Placement Coordinator.
7. **Missing info:** If required information is missing, request it; do not proceed to approval.
8. **Inappropriate placement:** If the request cannot be supported, the Placement Coordinator recommends rejection to the Executive Director (decision recorded on PLP-FORM-004).
9. **Potential employment:** If the service may constitute employment, route through §6 escalation before approval.

## 8. External Verification

The Placement Coordinator verifies the referring or obligating organization **before** service begins.

- **What is verified:** legitimacy/active status of the sponsor, the nature of the program, the required hours, the requirements imposed on the organization, any funding/compensation terms, and the external point of contact.
- **When required:** Always for court-ordered, agency, workforce, work-study, and school placements; otherwise as applicable.
- **Method:** Contact the sponsoring organization directly, obtain an authorization letter/order, and confirm in writing where possible.
- **Documented on:** PLP-FORM-003.
- **Storage:** In the placement record (REC-REF-001).
- **Discrepancies:** Any discrepancy (e.g., hours, duties, or legal terms that conflict with organizational policy) is documented and escalated to the Executive Director.
- **Unverifiable sponsor:** If the external organization cannot be verified, the placement is **not accepted** and the file is recorded as declined.

## 9. Approval

Approval workflow: **REQUEST → REVIEW → DECISION → AUTHORIZATION → RECORD**.

- **Who can approve:** Executive Director (or the authority designated in FIN-CTRL-001).
- **Who cannot approve:** The prospective participant, their unpaid-placing supervisor, or any volunteer.
- **When executive review is required:** court-ordered, internships/practicums, workforce, any compensation, any classification uncertainty.
- **When rejected:** unverifiable sponsor, prohibited duties, employee-replacement concern, or a conflict with law/organizational policy.
- **Documented:** on PLP-FORM-004 (decision, authority, date, terms, restrictions). No placement begins until approval is recorded.

## 11. Screening

- Screening depends on the duties and the program. Use PLP-CHK-002.
- Consider: identity; references; background screening when the participant will work with minors/vulnerable individuals, handle money, drive participants, or access confidential information; driving requirements; safeguarding; and program-specific or legally required checks.
- Where no screening is legally required and duties do not warrant it, record that screening is not applicable. Do not invent screening that only a supervising authority / project can require. The Placement Coordinator determines applicability with the Program Director.

## 12. Orientation

- Before first service, the participant completes orientation per PLP-CHK-003, covering expectations, conduct, confidentiality, safety, boundaries, supervisor, duties, prohibited activities, incident reporting, emergency procedures, organizational property, and communication.
- Orientation is documented with the checklist signed/dated.

## 13. Training

- Required and division-specific training per PLP-FORM-005, provided by the supervisor or designated trainer, completed before the participant performs duties requiring it. Incomplete training blocks performance of those duties.

## 14. Duty Authorization

- Authorize duties using PLP-FORM-006, which explicitly lists **authorized duties** and **prohibited duties**, decision-making authority, required supervision, restricted information, system/facility access, money/property access, participant contact, transportation authority, and organizational-representation authority.
- A participant may **never** gradually acquire authority merely from extended presence. Additional authority requires a documented change approval (PLP-FORM-009) reviewed by the Program Director.

## 15. Supervisor Assignment

- Every active placement has a named supervisor (PLP-FORM-007). The supervisor is responsible for assigning work, monitoring performance, attendance, hours, training, conduct, safety, incidents, changes, external communications, and completion.
- A special-placement participant does not supervise employees unless the authorized structure expressly provides for it and the arrangement is formally approved.

## 16. Service and Hours Recording

- Complete PLP-FORM-008 (date, activity, start/end, hours, location, project/program, supervisor).
- The participant records hours; the supervisor verifies them. Frequency and corrections are documented. Disputes are escalated.
- Missing records are flagged; falsification of hours is treated as a conduct incident (§18).
- **Not compensation:** Recorded hours are service/placement hours reported to the sponsoring program — they are not wages, and they are not represented as employee time. Where a work-study placement is paid, compensation is handled through the appropriate payroll/contracting route, not as volunteer or service hours.

## 17. Placement-Type Subsections

### 17.1 Work-Study
- Verify the educational institution and program authorization (PLP-FORM-003).
- Clarify funding/compensation structure; work-study is **not** assumed to be volunteer service. If paid, follow the correct employment/contracting classification (§6) — do not treat paid work as volunteer hours.
- Set authorized hours, duties, supervisor, reporting, evaluation, documentation, changes, completion, and termination per this SOP.

### 17.2 Internship / Practicum
- Verify institution, program, educational requirements, learning objectives, and required hours (PLP-FORM-003).
- Identify the academic contact and supervisor.
- Define duties and training; document evaluation and completion.
- Apply the classification/escalation process (§6) when the arrangement may constitute employment.

### 17.3 Community Service (Court, School, Agency)
- Distinguish the source: court-ordered, probation-related, diversion, agency-directed, school-required, or other mandated service.
- Verify the obligation (PLP-FORM-003) and record the exact required hours and deadline (PLP-FORM-008).
- **Court (17.3.1):** Never alter a court’s requirements. Verify the order; track hours; complete required external verification/reporting (PLP-FORM-013); comply with prohibited duties; handle incidents per §18; suspension per §20; termination per §21. Personnel may not modify the court’s conditions.

### 17.4 Workforce / Work-Experience
- Verify the workforce agency, job-training program, vocational program, work-experience program, rehabilitation placement, or employment-readiness program (PLP-FORM-003).
- Identify external program requirements that must be verified before placement.
- Follow the classification process; escalate if the arrangement may be employment.

## 18. Incident Management

Workflow: **INCIDENT → IMMEDIATE RESPONSE → SUPERVISOR → DOCUMENTATION → ESCALATION → EXTERNAL NOTIFICATION IF REQUIRED → REVIEW → CORRECTIVE ACTION → CLOSURE**.

- Cover injuries, accidents, misconduct, unauthorized activity, confidentiality breaches, safety violations, property damage, falsified hours, complaints, and violations of external-program requirements.
- Any significant incident is documented on **SAF-FORM-001** per SAF-POL-002 and logged to the Incident Register.
- For court/agency placements, external notification is coordinated with the Placement Coordinator and Executive Director (PLP-FORM-013).

## 19. Placement Change Control

- A **material change** includes changes to duties, supervisor, schedule, hours, location, duration, access, classification, funding, or sponsoring program.
- Document material changes on PLP-FORM-009; obtain documented approval (Program Director, and Executive Director when it affects authority, classification, compensation, or a court/agency requirement).
- Reclassification or a change that may make the role an employee position triggers §6 review.

## 20. Suspension

- Grounds: immediate safety concern, investigation of an incident, failure to meet program requirements, serious misconduct, or organizational necessity.
- Immediate removal for safety (remove the participant immediately; document after).
- Notification: participant, supervisor, and — for court/agency/hiring program — the sponsoring organization (PLP-FORM-013).
- Document suspension on PLP-FORM-010 (reason, authority, scope, any permitted duties, return conditions, follow-up owner).
- Review determines reinstatement or termination.

## 21. Termination

Separate the possible outcomes:

- Voluntary withdrawal
- Completion / expiration
- Termination by the organization (for cause)
- Termination by external program / revoked authorization
- Serious misconduct / safety removal / loss of authorization

Document each termination path on PLP-FORM-011 with the decision authority (Executive Director for organizational termination), effective date, reason/category, access/property return, and external notification.

## 22. Completion and Verification

- On completion (or the external deadline) the supervisor verifies total hours, duties, completion requirements, incidents, property, access, evaluations, and any external forms.
- Complete PLP-FORM-012 with the final verified hours, the external form(s) required by the sponsoring program, and final external communications (PLP-FORM-013).
- **Court/agency verification:** final verified hours are reported to the external authority exactly as recorded — never inflated or altered.

## 23. Record Closure

- The record is **not** closed merely because the participant stops appearing. Use PLP-CHK-004 (Closure Checklist).
- Confirm: all required forms present; final hours verified; disposition (completion/termination/withdrawal/expiration); property and access returned; closed incidents; external verification forms completed; retention classification (REC-MATRIX-001).
- Then the Records Administrator places the record in the applicable retention status.

## 24. Decision Trees

1. **Classification:** employee vs. volunteer vs. special placement (§6).
2. **Acceptance:** can the organization accept this placement? (verify sponsor → approvals → prohibited duties).
3. **Screening:** does the duty/program require additional screening? (§11).
4. **Duties:** are the proposed duties permissible? (authorized vs. prohibited on PLP-FORM-006).
5. **Change:** does the change require reauthorization? (§19).
6. **Incident:** does the incident warrant suspension/escalation? (§18, §20).
7. **Completion:** is the placement eligible for closure? (§22, §23).
