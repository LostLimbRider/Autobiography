## 1. Purpose

Financial controls keep money handled clean, documented, and boring. Controls are proportional to the organization’s size but remain meaningful.

## 2. Segregation of Duties

The **requester → approver → payer → reconciler** roles are separated where practical:

- The person who requests an expenditure is not the sole approver.
- The person who approves is not the person who cuts the check/initiates the transfer.
- The person who processes payments is not the person who reconciles the bank account.
- The Treasurer or a designee who does not perform bookkeeping reviews bank statements monthly.

**Compensating controls where size prevents full segregation:** a second person reviews and approves each stage; the Board reviews financial reports at every regular meeting; annual review/audit per §6.

## 3. Authorization

No expenditure occurs without authorization per FIN-CTRL-001. No one authorizes their own expense, reimbursement, or compensation.

## 4. Reconciliation

- Bank accounts reconciled monthly (FIN-PROC-007).
- Donations, restricted funds, grants, payroll, and expenses reconciled monthly (FIN-CHK-002).
- Unreconciled differences are investigated and documented.

## 5. Cash Handling

- Cash is counted by two people where practical and deposited promptly (FIN-POL-002, FIN-CHK-004).
- No cash is commingled with personal funds.

## 6. Annual Review and Audit

- The Board approves an annual financial review process.
- When revenue reaches a board-defined threshold or grant requirements demand it, the organization obtains an independent review or audit (consistent with the Organization Handbook).
- The independent review threshold is a Board decision; the completion report flags it for Board action.

## 7. Restricted Funds

Restricted funds (donor-designated or grant) are tracked separately and spent only for the stated purpose (FIN-PROC-006, FIN-REG-003).

## 8. Reporting

The Board receives financial reports at every regular meeting (statement of activity, statement of financial position, budget-to-actual, cash, restricted funds, payables, grant status, fundraising pipeline) per FIN-PROC-008.

## 9. Violations

Control failures, suspected fraud, or unauthorized transactions are reported per GOV-POL-006 (Whistleblower) and remediated; serious matters are reported to the Board and, where required, to authorities.
