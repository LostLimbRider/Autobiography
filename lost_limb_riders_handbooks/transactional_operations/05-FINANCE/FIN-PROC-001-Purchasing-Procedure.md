## 1. Purpose

Purchases serve mission needs, fit the approved budget, use reasonable pricing, and are approved before commitment.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Need and budget check.** Confirm the purchase serves a mission need and is within the approved budget (or approved separately).
2. **Requisition.** Complete FIN-FORM-004 (Purchase Requisition) with item, quantity, estimated cost, vendor, justification, and program/event.
3. **Approval.** Approve per FIN-CTRL-001. For purchases above the Board-approved threshold, obtain multiple quotes unless impractical, and document the quote comparison.
4. **Conflict check.** No purchase from an interested person without GOV-POL-004 process.
5. **Commit.** Place the order with the approved vendor; record the PO/requisition number.
6. **Receive.** Verify goods/services received match the order; note discrepancies immediately.
7. **Pay.** Route the invoice with the approved requisition to payment (FIN-PROC-002).
8. **Record.** Log in the expense register (FIN-REG-001) and master register.

## 3. Thresholds

| Amount | Control |
|--------|---------|
| Up to $250 | Department Director approval (within budget) |
| $250.01 – $1,000 | Executive Director approval |
| $1,000.01 – $5,000 | Finance Committee review and approval |
| Above $5,000 / unbudgeted long-term | Board approval + competitive quotes where practical |

These are **internal control** standards (FIN-CTRL-001), subject to Board adoption.

## 4. Emergency Purchases

Emergency expenditures up to the emergency limit in FIN-CTRL-001 may be made by the Executive Director and are reported to the Board at the next meeting for ratification.

## 5. Record Keeping

Keep the requisition, quotes, invoice, receipt, approval, and payment record together under the EXP transaction ID.
