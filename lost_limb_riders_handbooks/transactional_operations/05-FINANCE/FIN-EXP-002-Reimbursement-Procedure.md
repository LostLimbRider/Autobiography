## 1. Purpose

Every expense is documented before payment: who spent, when, where, how much, why, what program/event, what account, whether authorized, the receipt, reimbursement or direct payment, who approved, when paid, and the accounting reference.

## 2. General Rules

- Expenses must be incurred for legitimate organizational purposes and, where applicable, within the approved budget or grant terms.
- Advances require prior approval; unused advances are returned.
- Reimbursement requires approval **by someone other than the requester** (FIN-CTRL-001).
- No reimbursement for expenses already reimbursed or paid by the organization.

## 3. Mileage

- Mileage is claimed on FIN-EXP-003 (Mileage Log) at the rate in effect per the organization’s policy (commonly the IRS standard mileage rate — confirm the current rate; the organization adopts a rate by policy).
- The rate is verified each year; it is not a hard-coded number (see CMP-REF-001).

## 4. Receipts

- Receipts are required for all expenses.
- **Missing receipts are the exception, not the rule.** If a receipt cannot be obtained, the requester completes FIN-EXP-004 (Missing Receipt Declaration) with the basis, and a supervisor (other than the requester) approves the declaration.
- The missing-receipt process is **not a loophole**: repeated missing receipts, vague explanations, or expenses without any substantiation are denied and escalated per GOV-POL-006.

## 5. Procedure

1. Requester completes FIN-EXP-001 with all lines, receipts, and the related program/event/grant IDs.
2. Mileage lines use FIN-EXP-003.
3. Missing receipts use FIN-EXP-004.
4. Supervisor approves (not the requester); Finance reviews for policy, budget, and grant compliance; Executive approval where FIN-CTRL-001 requires.
5. Payment issued per FIN-PROC-002.
6. Expense logged in FIN-REG-001 with the EXP ID; posted to the ledger.
7. Reimbursement to employees is generally not taxable wages when properly substantiated as an accountable plan (confirm treatment with the CPA).

## 6. Deadline

Expense reports are submitted per the payroll/billing calendar — generally within a defined number of days after the expense.
