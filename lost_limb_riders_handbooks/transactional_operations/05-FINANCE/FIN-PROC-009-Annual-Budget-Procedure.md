## 1. Purpose

An annual budget is approved by the Board before each fiscal year, per the Organization Handbook financial procedures.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Timeline.** The Executive Director and Finance Director prepare the annual budget before each fiscal year.
2. **Department requests.** Department directors submit budget requests with program goals, expected revenue, expenses, staffing needs, and assumptions.
3. **Draft.** Finance consolidates requests into a draft budget with revenue, expenses, staffing, and cash-flow assumptions.
4. **Review.** The Finance Committee reviews the draft and recommends approval to the Board.
5. **Approval.** The Board approves the final budget before implementation; the approval is recorded in GOV-REG-001 (BRD ID).
6. **Monitor.** Actual results are compared to budget monthly (FIN-PROC-008); material variances are explained.
7. **Amend.** Material budget changes require Finance Committee/Board approval per FIN-CTRL-001.

## 3. Budget Content

- Projected revenue by source (donations, grants, sponsorship, events, program revenue, other).
- Projected expenses by program and function (program, fundraising, administration).
- Staffing and compensation (consistent with approved worksheets).
- Capital purchases and reserves.
- Cash-flow plan.

## 4. Nonprofit Context

Revenue may exceed expenses; retained funds support programs and reserves. Compensation is budgeted as an operating expense (GOV-POL-005).
