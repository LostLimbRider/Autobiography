**Register notes:**
- Sponsorships where the sponsor receives advertising or promotional benefits are generally **not** charitable contributions for donor deduction purposes; they are recorded as revenue and acknowledged accordingly (FUND-POL-001, FIN-PROC-005).
- The deductible portion (amount exceeding the fair market value of benefits) is documented with the same good-faith standard used for donation acknowledgments.
- Reconcile to deposits monthly; report in fundraising revenue monthly (FIN-CHK-002).
- Completed registers retained per REC-MATRIX-001; blank layout only in this handbook.
