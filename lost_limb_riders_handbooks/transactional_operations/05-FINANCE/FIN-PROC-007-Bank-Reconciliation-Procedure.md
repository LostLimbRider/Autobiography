## 1. Purpose

To verify that recorded cash matches bank records monthly and to detect errors, fraud, or unauthorized activity.

## 2. Segregation

The person who reconciles does not perform bookkeeping/processing (FIN-POL-001). The Treasurer or a designee who does not perform bookkeeping reviews the completed reconciliations and bank statements monthly.

## 3. Procedure

1. Obtain the bank statement and the ledger cash balance.
2. Compare deposits per ledger to the statement (tick off each).
3. Compare payments per ledger to the statement (tick off each).
4. Identify outstanding checks and deposits in transit; prepare a schedule.
5. Adjust for bank fees/interest and recording errors; document each adjustment.
6. Reconcile the ending balance: bank balance + deposits in transit − outstanding checks ± adjustments = ledger balance.
7. Investigate and resolve any unexplained differences; document the outcome.
8. Have the reconciliation reviewed by a second person (Treasurer/designee) per FIN-POL-001.
9. File the reconciliation and statement per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 4. Red Flags

- Differences that persist month to month.
- Deposits recorded but not appearing on the statement.
- Payments to payees not in registers.
- Altered or missing statements.

Any red flag is investigated immediately and reported per GOV-POL-006 if suspected misconduct.
