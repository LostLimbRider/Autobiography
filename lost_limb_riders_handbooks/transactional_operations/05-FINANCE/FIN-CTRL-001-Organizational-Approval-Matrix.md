## 1. Purpose

This matrix defines who may authorize expenses, purchases, contracts, payroll, reimbursements, events, compensation, accounts, grants, asset disposals, and emergency expenditures, with monetary thresholds.

## 2. Status of These Thresholds

The dollar thresholds below are **Lost Limb Riders internal control standards**, not legal requirements. They carry forward the standards already stated in the Organization Handbook’s financial procedures (Department Director $250 / Executive Director $1,000 / Finance Committee above $1,000 / Board above $5,000) and are **subject to formal Board adoption**. Where a higher or lower threshold is legally required, the legal requirement controls.

## 3. Approval Matrix

| Action | Authority (signature required) | Threshold / Condition |
|--------|-------------------------------|------------------------|
| Routine expense / purchase | Department Director | Up to $250 per transaction, within approved budget |
| Expense / purchase | Executive Director | $250.01 – $1,000 |
| Expense / purchase | Finance Committee | $1,000.01 – $5,000 |
| Expense / purchase; unbudgeted long-term obligation; capital asset acquisition | Board | Above $5,000 or any unbudgeted long-term obligation |
| Contract / agreement | Authorized officer (ED) per Board | Within approval matrix; major contracts to Board |
| Payroll run | Finance Director + Executive Director | Every run; amount per approved payroll |
| Reimbursement | Supervisor + Finance Director (approval by someone other than the requester) | All amounts |
| Event acceptance | Events Director + Executive Director | Per EVT-AUTH-001; Board for Board-level financial risk |
| Insider compensation (founder, officers, directors, key employees) | Disinterested Board members | Always (GOV-POL-005) |
| Employee compensation (non-insider) | Executive Director | Per HR-FORM-005 |
| Open/close bank accounts | Board | Always |
| Signing authority (checks/EFT) | Two authorized signers as designated | Above internal limit; dual control where practical |
| Grants application | Fundraising Director + Executive Director (Board for major) | Per GRT-PROC-001 |
| Sponsorship agreement | Sponsorship Coordinator + Executive Director (Board for major) | Per FUND-PROC-002 |
| Asset disposal | Finance Director (Board above threshold) | Per FIN-REG-005 |
| Emergency expenditure | Executive Director | Up to $2,500, reported to Board at next meeting for ratification |
| Write-off / disputed amount | Finance Committee / Board | Material amounts to Board |

## 4. Segregation of Duties

The **requester → approver → payer → reconciler** roles should not be the same person. Where the organization’s size makes complete segregation impractical, the compensating control is: a different person reviews/approves each stage, and the Treasurer or designee who does not perform bookkeeping reviews bank statements monthly. See FIN-POL-001.

## 5. Departures

No one may authorize their own expense, reimbursement, or compensation. Any departure from this matrix requires Board approval and is recorded in GOV-REG-001.

## 6. Review

The matrix is reviewed annually and after each fiscal year’s first budget, as noted in the Organization Handbook, with any changes adopted by the Board.
