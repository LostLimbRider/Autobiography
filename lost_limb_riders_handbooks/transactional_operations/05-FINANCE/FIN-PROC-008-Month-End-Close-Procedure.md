## 1. Purpose

To close each month completely: all transactions recorded, reconciled, and reported, with open items identified.

## 2. Procedure (per FIN-CHK-002)

1. Complete all expense, donation, payroll, grant, and event transactions for the month.
2. Record all journal entries (depreciation, allocations, corrections).
3. Reconcile bank accounts (FIN-PROC-007), donations, restricted funds, payroll, and grant activity.
4. Produce the monthly financial package:
   - Statement of activity (income/expenses)
   - Statement of financial position (balance sheet)
   - Budget-to-actual report
   - Cash balance
   - Restricted funds report
   - Major payables/receivables
   - Grant status and spending
   - Fundraising pipeline
5. Review the package with the Finance Committee; present to the Board at the next regular meeting (per the Organization Handbook).
6. Update the master transaction register (ADM-REG-001); close closed transactions; flag open ones.

## 3. Open Items

Any transaction not closed is flagged with its closeout status; unresolved items are reviewed monthly until closed.
