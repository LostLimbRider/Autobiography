## 1. Purpose

Restricted funds — contributions or grants limited by the donor/grantor to a stated purpose or time — are tracked separately and spent **only** for the stated purpose.

## 2. Classification

At receipt, each gift is classified:

- **Unrestricted** — may be used for any organizational purpose.
- **Restricted by purpose** — e.g., “for the Ride Forward Program.”
- **Restricted by time** — e.g., funds for a specific period.
- **Grant-restricted** — per the grant agreement.

## 3. Procedure

1. **Record the restriction** at receipt in FIN-REG-002 and FIN-REG-003 (donor, amount, restriction, source).
2. **Tag expenditures** with the restricted-fund reference when spending.
3. **Review** each expenditure against the restriction before payment; the approval records the restriction.
4. **Report** restricted activity in the monthly financial reports (FIN-PROC-008).
5. **Reconcile** restricted balances monthly; verify they equal cash set aside less qualifying spending.
6. **If the purpose cannot be fulfilled:** obtain donor/grantor agreement to redirect or return the funds, and document it (consistent with the Organization Handbook donation rules).

## 4. Prohibitions

- Spending restricted funds for other purposes without donor/grantor agreement.
- Commingling restricted funds such that the balance cannot be identified.
- Releasing restricted funds at closeout without grantor approval where required.

## 5. Records

Restricted fund records are retained per REC-MATRIX-001 and support Form 990 reporting.
