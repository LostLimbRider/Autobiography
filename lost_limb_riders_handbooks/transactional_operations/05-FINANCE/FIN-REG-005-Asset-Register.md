**Register notes:**
- Capital purchases (board-defined threshold, commonly items exceeding the capitalization threshold with a useful life beyond one year) are recorded at acquisition with the approved requisition.
- Restricted/grant-funded assets note the funding source so grant/restriction closeouts can be met.
- The register reconciles to the ledger at year-end (FIN-CHK-003).
- Dispositions are authorized per FIN-CTRL-001 (Board above threshold) and recorded with date, proceeds, and authority.
- Completed registers retained per REC-MATRIX-001; blank layout only in this handbook.
