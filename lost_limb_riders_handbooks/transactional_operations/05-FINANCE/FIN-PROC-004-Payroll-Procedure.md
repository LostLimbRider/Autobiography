## 1. Purpose

To pay employees accurately and on time, withhold and remit taxes, record payroll, and file required returns. **Do not hard-code tax rates or deposit schedules** — use the current rates and schedules published by the IRS, Iowa Department of Revenue, and Iowa Workforce Development (see CMP-REF-001 and CMP-CAL-001).

## 2. Payroll Workflow

```text
Time Records (HR-TIME-001)
    ↓
Supervisor Verification
    ↓
Payroll Review
    ↓
Gross Wage Calculation
    ↓
Withholding (Federal + Iowa)
    ↓
Employer Taxes
    ↓
Payroll Approval (FIN-CTRL-001)
    ↓
Payment
    ↓
Payroll Journal Entry
    ↓
Tax Deposit (per EFTPS/schedule)
    ↓
Payroll Reconciliation
    ↓
Record Filing
```

## 3. Procedure

1. **Collect time records.** Each non-exempt employee’s HR-TIME-001 for the pay period is submitted and supervisor-approved (HR-SOP-005).
2. **Review.** Finance verifies approvals, classification, and rate (HR-FORM-005), and that the employee is set up (HR-FORM-006).
3. **Calculate gross.** Hours × approved rate; overtime per current FLSA/Iowa rules for non-exempt employees.
4. **Withhold.** Federal income tax (per W-4), FICA (Social Security/Medicare per current rates), Iowa withholding (per Iowa W-4), and other authorized deductions.
5. **Employer taxes.** Employer share of FICA, federal unemployment tax (FUTA), and Iowa unemployment insurance per current rates.
6. **Approve.** The payroll run is approved per FIN-CTRL-001 (Finance Director + Executive Director).
7. **Pay.** Issue payments (direct deposit/checks) on the pay date in the payroll calendar.
8. **Journal entry.** Post gross wages, withholdings, employer taxes, and net pay to the ledger per the chart of accounts.
9. **Tax deposits.** Deposit federal taxes per the current EFTPS deposit schedule; remit Iowa withholding per Iowa rules; pay UI per the IWD schedule.
10. **Reconcile.** Reconcile payroll to time records, bank, and ledger (FIN-CHK-001).
11. **File.** Retain payroll records per REC-MATRIX-001; file quarterly (941) and year-end (W-2/W-3) returns per CMP-CAL-001.

## 4. Payroll Runs

- **Regular payroll** — per the payroll calendar.
- **Correction payroll** — for errors; corrections are documented and follow the same approval path.
- **Final payroll** — per HR-CHK-005 with final payment timing per applicable law.
- **Year-end payroll** — W-2 production, Iowa year-end reporting, and reconciliation (FIN-CHK-003, FIN-CHK-001).

## 5. Controls

- Approval by two people (Finance Director + Executive Director) where practical.
- Recalculation of a test employee per run by a second reviewer.
- Payee verification against HR-FORM-006.
- No payments without approved time records.

## 6. Professional Requirement

**Before the first payroll run and annually**, a CPA or payroll professional must verify current rates, deposit schedules, and filing requirements. This procedure does not replace professional payroll administration.
