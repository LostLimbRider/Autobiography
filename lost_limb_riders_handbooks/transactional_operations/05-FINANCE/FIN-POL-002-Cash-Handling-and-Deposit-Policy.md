## 1. Purpose

To receive, protect, record, and deposit all money — cash, checks, and electronic — promptly and with controls.

## 2. Cash Handling

- **Two-person counting:** cash donations and event cash are counted and signed for by two people where practical.
- **Prompt recording:** money is recorded the day received with donor/transaction ID, amount, date, method, restriction, and campaign.
- **Prompt deposit:** money is deposited per the deposit schedule (FIN-CHK-004); cash is never kept overnight in an uncontrolled location.
- **No commingling:** organizational funds are never mixed with personal funds.

## 3. Checks and Electronic Payments

- Checks are restrictively endorsed (“For Deposit Only”) immediately on receipt.
- Electronic payments (card, online, ACH) are reconciled to the payment processor statements.

## 4. Deposits

- Each deposit is logged on FIN-FORM-007 (Deposit Log) with a cross-reference to the related donations/transactions.
- Deposits are made by an authorized person; a different person reconciles (FIN-PROC-007).

## 5. Access

- Bank accounts are opened and signers designated by the Board.
- At least two authorized signers are named; dual control for large or all disbursements where practical.

## 6. Shortages and Overages

Any shortage or overage is investigated and documented; recurring issues are reported per GOV-POL-006.
