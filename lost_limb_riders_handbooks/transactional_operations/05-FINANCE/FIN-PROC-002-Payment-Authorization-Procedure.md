## 1. Purpose

No money leaves the organization without authorization, supporting documentation, and a defined approval path.

## 2. Preconditions for Any Payment

- A valid underlying transaction (approved purchase, invoice, expense report, payroll run, reimbursement).
- Supporting documentation (invoice/receipt, approved requisition, time records).
- Approval per FIN-CTRL-001.
- A different person reviews the payment where practical (segregation).

## 3. Payment Flow

1. **Request.** The requester submits the invoice or expense with supporting documents on FIN-FORM-005 (Payment Authorization) or the applicable form.
2. **Verify.** Finance verifies: amount matches invoice, work/goods received, math correct, budget/funding source valid, no duplicate payment, vendor matches W-9/records.
3. **Approve.** Approval per FIN-CTRL-001 (not the requester).
4. **Pay.** The authorized signer(s) issue the check/EFT per the signing policy.
5. **Record.** Post the payment to the ledger and registers (EXP/PAY), with the accounting reference.
6. **Reconcile.** The payment is reflected in the bank reconciliation (FIN-PROC-007).

## 4. Controls

- **Two-person review** for all payments where practical.
- **Dual signatures** or dual control for checks/EFT above the internal limit.
- **No cash payments** without exception and documented approval.
- **Duplicate payment check** by invoice number/vendor.
- **Payee verification:** pay to the party in the W-9/agreement, not a third party.

## 5. Audit Trail

Every payment links: transaction ID → approval reference → supporting documents → payment record → bank reconciliation.
