## 1. Purpose — Single Source of Truth

This folder — `lost_limb_riders_handbooks/transactional_operations/03-HUMAN-RESOURCES/` — is the **one authoritative HR packet** for Lost Limb Riders employee lifecycle. Every document uses the `HR-*` ID scheme. It supersedes the earlier numbered HR tree under `lost_limb_riders_operations/03-HUMAN-RESOURCES/`, which is retained only as history (see its superseded notice).

**A new ED, bookkeeper, or HR handler follows this packet — nothing else.**

## 3. Authoritative-for-Transactions

Per the Master Index (`00-START-HERE/MASTER-INDEX.md`) and HR-POL-001, this packet is the **single** HR/employment standard. Contractor and volunteer lifecycles live in their own folders (`04-CONTRACTORS`, `08-VOLUNTEERS`) and are deliberately not merged here.

## 4. First-Hire Focus

The first paid employee under this packet is the **Executive Director** (the only full-time salaried role; see `employees/07-Executive-Director.md`). For that first hire, the Board is the hiring authority and the approver of compensation (HR-FORM-001, HR-FORM-005, GOV-POL-005). HR-POL-002 (Employee Handbook) is written to be handed to the ED on day one and reused for every subsequent hire.

## 5. Change Control

Any change to this packet follows ADM-PROC-002 (change control) and updates this index. New documents take the next free `HR-*` ID and link to their policy/SOP/checklist/form per the chain POLICY → SOP → CHK → FORM → REG.
