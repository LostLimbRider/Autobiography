## 1. Purpose

Before the organization hires and pays its first employee, it must be registered and set up as an employer. This procedure covers the one-time setup; the checklist (HR-CHK-001) is the execution tool.

## 2. Prerequisites

- Incorporated entity and EIN (obtained from IRS).
- Board approval to employ and a payroll account.
- Designated payroll processor or payroll professional.

## 3. Federal Setup

1. **EIN** — obtained from the IRS (the organization’s Employer Identification Number).
2. **Federal employment tax accounts** — deposits and filings for federal income tax withholding, Social Security, and Medicare are made via the Electronic Federal Tax Payment System (EFTPS); the organization enrolls in EFTPS.
3. **Form SS-4** (EIN application) and **Form 941** (quarterly payroll tax return) are registered; **Form W-2/W-3** year-end process is established; **Form W-4** collected from each employee.
4. **I-9 process** established (each new hire completes I-9 within the legally required timeframe; see CMP-IRS-001).

## 4. Iowa Setup

1. **Iowa withholding** — register with the Iowa Department of Revenue for withholding tax (Iowa W-4 collected from employees; Iowa quarterly/annual withholding returns and payments).
2. **Unemployment insurance** — register with Iowa Workforce Development (IWD) for employer account, and pay quarterly unemployment insurance contributions/reports.
3. **New-hire reporting** — report each new hire to the Iowa New Hire Reporting Program within the legally required time (see CMP-IA-001).
4. **Workers’ compensation** — obtain workers’ compensation coverage as required under Iowa law with a licensed carrier; display the required notice.
5. **Required postings** — display required federal and Iowa employment posters (wage/hour, OSHA, anti-discrimination, workers’ compensation, unemployment, etc.).

## 5. Payroll Setup

1. Select a payroll processor or use a qualified payroll professional.
2. Establish pay periods, pay dates, and a payroll calendar.
3. Collect HR-FORM-006 (payroll setup) and W-4 (federal) and Iowa W-4 from each employee.
4. Configure direct deposit and/or pay card if used.
5. Define the payroll approval path (FIN-CHK-001) and journal entry process (FIN-PROC-004).

## 6. Documentation

Keep copies of all registration confirmations, account numbers, and notices in the compliance files per REC-REF-001. Log each setup item in HR-CHK-001.

## 7. Professional Review

**Before the first payroll run**, a CPA or payroll professional must review the setup. Tax deposit schedules and rates change; do not hard-code rates. Refer to CMP-IRS-001 / CMP-IA-001 sources and the compliance calendar for current deadlines.
