## 1. Organizational Principle

Lost Limb Riders is permitted to earn revenue and employ people. **Compensation for legitimate work is an organizational operating expense**, not an impermissible distribution of nonprofit earnings. This policy exists to make lawful employment possible while preventing private inurement, undocumented labor, and unreasonable insider compensation.

## 2. Three Worker Categories

The organization engages people in exactly three categories. The category is determined by the actual facts of the relationship, not by what the parties call it.

| Category | Basis | Compensation | Key Docs |
|----------|-------|--------------|----------|
| **Employee** | Organization controls how, when, and where work is done; paid on payroll | Wages/salary + payroll taxes withheld | HR lifecycle (this folder) |
| **Independent contractor** | Person controls how work is done; paid by invoice; no withholding | Contract amount per agreement | 04-CONTRACTORS |
| **Volunteer** | Unpaid service to the organization | No compensation (reimbursement only) | 08-VOLUNTEERS |

Calling someone a contractor does not make them one. Classification is a legal determination made on the facts (see CTR-CHK-001).

## 3. Employment Standards

- **Authorization:** No position is filled and no person is paid without an approved position authorization (HR-FORM-001) and, for covered persons, an approved compensation worksheet (HR-FORM-005).
- **Work authorization:** Every new employee completes Form I-9 within the legally required time and, where applicable, employment eligibility verification per law.
- **Taxes:** Wages are paid through the payroll system with required federal and Iowa withholdings, employer taxes, and filings (FIN-PROC-004).
- **Records:** Every employee has a personnel file per HR-CHK-003; completed records stay in restricted storage (REC-REF-001).
- **Timekeeping:** Hourly employees record all hours worked on HR-TIME-001; exempt employees follow the timekeeping procedure (HR-SOP-005).

## 4. Prohibited Practices

- Paying an individual “off the books.”
- Paying a worker as a contractor when the facts make them an employee.
- Paying an insider without approved compensation (GOV-POL-005).
- Failing to withhold or remit payroll taxes.
- Committing confidential employee records to the public repository.

## 5. Equal Opportunity and Lawful Hiring

The organization recruits, hires, and promotes without regard to protected status, and follows all applicable federal and Iowa employment laws. Discrimination, harassment, and retaliation are prohibited.

## 6. Employment at Will

Unless a written employment agreement states otherwise, employment is at-will: the employee or the organization may end the relationship at any time for any lawful reason.

## 7. Compliance Note

Employer obligations (I-9, new-hire reporting, withholding, unemployment insurance, workers’ compensation, postings/notices) are detailed in HR-CHK-001 and CMP-IA-001. Professional payroll/CPA review is required before the first payroll run.
