## 1. Purpose

To select the best candidate and to establish compensation through a controlled process that prevents “founder decides his own salary” and other self-dealing.

## 2. Selection Procedure

1. The hiring authority reviews interview evaluations (HR-FORM-003) and references.
2. The selected candidate is identified with the documented basis.
3. A **conflict check** is performed (GOV-POL-004): does the candidate have a relationship with a board member, officer, employee, or family member? Any conflict is disclosed and managed before an offer.
4. The offer is extended only after compensation is approved.

## 3. Compensation Approval Procedure

1. **Prepare** the compensation worksheet (HR-FORM-005) with position data and comparability sources.
2. **Route** for approval:
   - **Non-insider, below Board threshold:** Executive Director approves.
   - **Insiders (founder, officers, directors, key employees) and amounts at Board level:** disinterested Board members approve **before** the arrangement takes effect, using comparability data, with the action recorded in GOV-REG-001 (Board Action ID).
3. **The interested person** discloses and does not participate in their own approval.
4. **Record** the approved rate, effective date, and approval reference on HR-FORM-005.
5. **File** the worksheet in the personnel file and finance records.

## 4. Prohibited

- Paying an insider without an approved worksheet.
- An insider approving their own compensation.
- Paying a rate unsupported by an approved worksheet.
- Backdating compensation approvals.

## 5. Reasonableness

Compensation must be reasonable for the actual services performed (GOV-POL-005). For covered persons, the approved worksheet plus comparability data creates the contemporaneous record supporting reasonableness.
