## 1. Purpose

Every non-exempt (hourly) employee records all hours worked. Time records feed directly into payroll. No hours are paid without an approved time record.

## 2. Who Records Time

- **Non-exempt employees:** record all hours worked on HR-TIME-001 for each pay period.
- **Exempt employees:** record hours per organizational policy (typically a simple attendance record or exception basis), per FLSA rules.

## 3. Procedure

1. **Record.** The employee records date, start, end, unpaid break, total hours, and the project/event/program for each day worked.
2. **Submit.** The employee certifies the record and submits it by the deadline in the payroll calendar.
3. **Approve.** The supervisor reviews, corrects discrepancies with the employee, and approves. Unapproved records are not paid.
4. **Process.** Finance uses approved time records to calculate gross wages (rate from HR-FORM-005), overtime, and deductions (FIN-PROC-004).
5. **File.** Approved time records are filed with the payroll records per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 4. Rules

- Record actual hours worked, including overtime as actually worked. Do not pre-record hours.
- Unpaid meal breaks are excluded from compensable time.
- Rounding must be consistent and neutral; confirm the policy complies with FLSA/Iowa wage law.
- Employees must not work unauthorized overtime; overtime requires supervisor approval.

## 5. Overtime

Non-exempt overtime is paid at the rate required by the Fair Labor Standards Act and Iowa law. Verify the current threshold and rate with the payroll professional; do not hard-code (see CMP-IRS-001 / CMP-IA-001).

## 6. Corrections

Errors are corrected before approval. A corrected record must be signed by the employee and supervisor; the correction is documented (who, when, what).

## 7. Audit Trail

Each time record links to the employee’s EMP ID, the payroll run (PAY ID), and the project/event (e.g., EVT-2026-001), enabling tracing from hours worked to pay issued.
