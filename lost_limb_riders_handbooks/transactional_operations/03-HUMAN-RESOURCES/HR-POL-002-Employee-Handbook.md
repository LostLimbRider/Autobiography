## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Lost Limb Riders. You are not joining a company; you are joining a mission — helping amputees get back on two wheels and back into life. “I Can. I Will.” is not a slogan; it is how we operate.

If you are reading this as our first paid team member — the Executive Director — then you are the person who will turn this handbook into practice. You are the first hire, and the way you are onboarded, paid, and supported sets the standard for everyone who comes after you. This handbook is written for you first, and for every employee who follows.

## 2. How This Handbook Works

This handbook is a plain-language summary of the policies found in the HR packet (`HR-REF-002-HR-Packet-Index.md`). The controlling documents are the procedures, checklists, and forms behind it. Where this handbook and a procedure differ, the procedure governs for the specific step, and the conflict is resolved through the change-control process (ADM-PROC-002).

This handbook is not a contract of employment.

## 3. Employment at Will

Employment with Lost Limb Riders is **at-will**. You or the organization may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or advance notice, except as otherwise required by law or by a signed written employment agreement. Nothing in this handbook, any policy, or any practice creates a contract of employment.

By signing the acknowledgment (section 22), you confirm you have read and understand the policies in this handbook. It does not guarantee continued employment.

## 4. Equal Opportunity

Lost Limb Riders provides equal employment opportunity and prohibits discrimination, harassment, and retaliation on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law. This applies to recruitment, hiring, promotion, pay, benefits, discipline, and all other terms and conditions of employment.

## 5. Reasonable Accommodation

If you have a disability and need a reasonable accommodation to perform the essential functions of your job, or to apply for a position, request it. Requests are confidential and are considered through an interactive process. Contact the Executive Director (or, for the ED, the Board Chairperson) to begin a request. The organization complies with the Americans with Disabilities Act and Iowa law.

Lost Limb Riders was built around the reality of disability — a missing limb is not an obstacle to who we are. We extend that same standard to our team: we accommodate first, and we ask what you need.

## 6. Harassment

Lost Limb Riders is committed to a workplace free of harassment of any kind, including harassment based on any protected characteristic. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates a hostile work environment or results in a tangible employment action. Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature.

**Reporting.** If you experience or witness harassment (or discrimination or retaliation), report it promptly. For employees other than the ED, report to the Executive Director. Because the ED is the first hire, reports about the ED (or from the ED) go to the **Board Chairperson**. You may also report to any other member of management or the Board you are comfortable with; every report is taken seriously.

**No retaliation.** Lost Limb Riders prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

**Complaint procedure.** All concerns are investigated promptly and confidentially to the extent possible. The organization will take appropriate corrective action, up to and including termination, where a violation is found. A complaint procedure that explains your options is included at section 7.

## 7. Complaint Procedure

1. **Report.** Report the issue to the Executive Director, or to the Board Chairperson if it involves the Executive Director.
2. **Investigation.** The organization (or a Board-designated investigator) will investigate promptly, impartially, and with reasonable confidentiality.
3. **Resolution.** You will be informed of the outcome to the extent appropriate. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited and itself a violation of policy.
4. **External options.** Nothing here limits your right to file a charge with the Iowa Civil Rights Commission, the EEOC, or another appropriate agency, or to pursue any other legal remedy.

## 8. Worker Classifications

Lost Limb Riders engages people in exactly three categories: **employee**, **independent contractor**, and **volunteer** (see HR-POL-001). Your status and classification are set in your offer letter and payroll setup (HR-FORM-004, HR-FORM-006). Classification is a legal determination based on the facts, not on labels — see the contractor policy (04-CONTRACTORS) for the employee/contractor distinction.

Within employment, you are classified as:

| Classification | Meaning | Overtime |
|----------------|---------|----------|
| Non-exempt | Paid for each hour worked; time recorded on HR-TIME-001 | Paid at legal overtime rate for hours over the applicable limit |
| Exempt | Salaried; paid a fixed amount per pay period | Not entitled to overtime under FLSA |

The Board, with professional review, determines an employee’s exempt/non-exempt status. If your duties change materially, the classification is re-evaluated.

## 9. Compensation and Payroll

- **Pay basis and rate** are set in your approved compensation worksheet (HR-FORM-005) and confirmed in your offer letter (HR-FORM-004). Compensation must be reasonable and comparable to similar roles in Iowa (see `employees/Wage-Structure-and-Benefits.md`).
- **Pay frequency and pay dates** are established by Board resolution; your first pay date is confirmed at onboarding (HR-FORM-006).
- **Payroll deductions** (federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, Iowa withholding where applicable) are made as required and filed/paid by the organization through the payroll system (FIN-PROC-004).
- **Pay statements** are provided each pay period.
- **Executive Director compensation** (the only full-time salaried role) is set by the Board and is subject to the insider-compensation approval requirements of GOV-POL-005 (IRC §4958 excess-benefit rules). No Board member sets their own pay.

## 10. Timekeeping

- **Non-exempt employees** record all hours worked each pay period on HR-TIME-001. No hours are paid without an approved time record. Unauthorized overtime is not permitted; overtime requires supervisor approval.
- **Exempt employees** are paid on a salary basis and do not record time for FLSA purposes, but maintain an attendance record for organizational planning as directed.
- Record actual hours worked. Do not pre-record time. Correct errors before approval (see HR-SOP-005).

## 11. Paid Time Off (PTO)

- **Executive Director (full-time):** 15 days of PTO annually, combining vacation and sick time, per the compensation framework.
- **Other paid (stipend) positions:** 5 days of unpaid leave annually for absences beyond normal scheduling flexibility.
- Requests for time off should be submitted and approved in advance where practical. There is no paid holiday schedule beyond normal operational closure.

## 12. Unpaid Leave / Family & Medical

The organization is currently below the size thresholds that trigger federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) obligations, and that status will be re-evaluated as staff grows. Regardless of legal thresholds, the organization supports reasonable unpaid leave for medical, family, and personal needs consistent with law and operational needs. Requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Iowa-specific leave protections, where they apply, are honored (see CMP-IA-001).

## 13. Expenses and Reimbursement

Reasonable, documented, mission-related expenses are reimbursed per the reimbursement procedure (FIN-EXP-002) and the compensation framework:

1. Pre-approval required for any single expense over $100.
2. Receipts required for all reimbursements.
3. Mileage reimbursed at the IRS standard business rate in effect (currently $0.70/mile for 2025–2026).
4. Reimbursement requests submitted within 60 days of the expense.
5. The Finance Director reviews and processes reimbursements monthly.

## 14. Standards of Conduct

You are expected to:

- Carry out your duties honestly, lawfully, and in the organization’s best interest.
- Protect the mission, participants, members, and the organization’s reputation.
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them per the Conflict of Interest policy (GOV-POL-004).
- Not use organizational resources or your position for private gain.
- Not engage in fraud, theft, or misuse of funds or property.
- Keep participant, member, donor, and employee information confidential.

Conduct that violates these standards may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination (HR-SOP-007).

## 15. Confidentiality and Privacy

You will have access to sensitive information — participant records, donor information, financial data, and other employees’ personal information. This information is confidential.

- Access only the information you need to do your job.
- Do not share confidential information without authorization, except as required by law.
- Completed personal, financial, tax, and medical records are never placed in the public repository; they live in the restricted records system (REC-REF-001).
- Medical information and accommodation records are kept in a separate, restricted file (HR-CHK-003, File C) as required by law.

## 16. Use of Organizational Resources (IT / Acceptable Use)

Organizational equipment, accounts, email, and systems are provided for organizational purposes.

- Use organizational resources responsibly and for mission-related work.
- Do not use organizational accounts or credentials for unauthorized access, personal financial activity, or unlawful purposes.
- Safeguard passwords and credentials; do not share them.
- Report suspected security incidents or phishing promptly to the Executive Director (or Board, for the ED).
- Acceptable use may be further defined in the organization’s administrative or IT procedures; where such a procedure exists, it is part of this policy.

## 17. Remote Work

The organization supports flexible and remote work where it does not interfere with duties, supervision, or mission delivery. Remote arrangements are set at onboarding and may be adjusted as operational needs change. Regardless of location, you remain responsible for your hours, deliverables, confidentiality, and compliance with this handbook.

## 18. Workplace Safety

You are expected to work safely and to report hazards, injuries, and incidents promptly (see the safety/risk packet, 09-SAFETY-RISK). Lost Limb Riders’ program work — rides, events, hospital visits — has real physical risk; safety is non-negotiable. Workers’ compensation coverage is required under Iowa law and is being secured before the first hire (see CMP-REF-002); once in place, work-related injuries are reported per the workers’ compensation notice and the incident procedure.

## 19. Discipline and Performance

Performance is reviewed on a regular schedule (introductory period, then annual — HR-FORM-007). Conduct or performance problems are addressed fairly and consistently through progressive discipline: investigation, discussion, warning (HR-FORM-008) where appropriate, and corrective action proportional to the issue. Serious misconduct — fraud, theft, harassment, retaliation, safety violations, or conflicts of interest — may justify immediate action, up to and including termination (HR-SOP-007). Decisions are based on documented facts, with consistent treatment and no retaliation.

## 20. Separation

Employment may end by resignation or termination. On separation (HR-SOP-006):

- Final wages are paid by the date required by applicable federal and Iowa law.
- Organizational access (systems, email, keys, badges) is revoked and property is returned.
- Final time records are approved and the final payroll run is completed (HR-CHK-005).
- Records are closed and retained per the retention matrix (REC-MATRIX-001) and the personnel file checklist (HR-CHK-003).

## 21. Acknowledgment Requirement

Your signed acknowledgment is kept in your personnel file (HR-CHK-003, File A). It confirms you have received, read, and understood the policies summarized here.
