## 1. Purpose

To review performance consistently and to address performance or conduct problems fairly and with documentation.

## 2. Performance Review Procedure

1. Introductory review within the probationary period; annual reviews thereafter; more often as needed.
2. Supervisor completes HR-FORM-007 with ratings and comments.
3. Review meeting with the employee; employee comments captured.
4. File the completed review in the personnel file (HR-CHK-003, File A).
5. Compensation changes resulting from the review follow HR-FORM-005 and GOV-POL-005.

## 3. Discipline Procedure

1. **Investigate.** Gather facts, evidence, and witness statements; document.
2. **Meet.** Discuss the issue with the employee; the employee may respond.
3. **Determine action.** Verbal or written warning (HR-FORM-008), suspension, or termination, proportional to the issue and consistent with policy.
4. **Document.** Complete HR-FORM-008 with facts, corrective expectations, and consequences.
5. **File.** Place in the disciplinary file (HR-CHK-003, File E).
6. **Follow up.** Verify correction within the stated timeframe.

## 4. Serious Misconduct

Fraud, theft, harassment, retaliation, safety violations, or conflicts of interest may justify immediate action and escalation per GOV-POL-006 and SAF-POL-002.

## 5. Fairness Standards

- Consistent treatment across employees.
- No discrimination or retaliation.
- Decisions based on documented facts, not assumptions.
