### Notification and Authorization

- [ ] Separation notice (HR-FORM-009) completed

### Access and Property

- [ ] All system/email/account access revoked (date and by whom recorded)
- [ ] Keys, badges, phones, laptops returned
- [ ] Organizational property accounted for (uniforms, equipment, tools)
- [ ] Passwords changed where necessary
- [ ] Contacts informed of departure (as appropriate)

### Final Payroll

- [ ] Time records finalized and approved (HR-TIME-001)
- [ ] Final wages calculated and paid per applicable law and pay schedule (HR-CHK-005)
- [ ] Accrued/unused leave handled per policy
- [ ] Final pay record filed (FIN-REG-004)

### Benefits and Notices

- [ ] Benefits termination notices handled
- [ ] COBRA notice provided where applicable (if benefits offered)
- [ ] Return of benefits cards/ID

### Records

- [ ] Personnel file updated (HR-CHK-003)
- [ ] Separation file created with notice, final payroll, property return, access revocation
- [ ] I-9 retention computed (later of 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination)
- [ ] Payroll records retained per REC-MATRIX-001

### Exit Review

- [ ] Exit interview completed (optional) and notes filed
- [ ] Master transaction register updated (closeout = separated)
- [ ] Compliance calendar updated (no final filings remain)
