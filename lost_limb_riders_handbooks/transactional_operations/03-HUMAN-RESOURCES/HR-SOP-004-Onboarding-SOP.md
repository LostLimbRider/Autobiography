## 1. Purpose

To bring each new employee into the organization lawfully and consistently, completing all required documentation and setup.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Before day one.** Prepare the workspace, accounts, and position manual. Send the employee the start date, time, and first-day instructions.
2. **Day one.**
   - Complete employee identification and the master onboarding checklist (HR-CHK-002).
   - Complete I-9 within the legally required timeframe (verify identity/work eligibility documents; file I-9 separately).
   - Collect W-4 (federal) and Iowa W-4.
   - Submit new-hire reporting within the legal deadline.
   - Complete payroll setup (HR-FORM-006).
   - Issue keys/badges/credentials and log them.
3. **First week.**
   - Complete required safety and policy training (see 09-SAFETY-RISK).
   - Acknowledge handbooks and policies in writing.
   - Establish first-week and 30/60/90-day goals.
4. **First month.**
   - Conduct a check-in; verify the employee is set up for timekeeping and payroll.
   - File all records per HR-CHK-003 and REC-REF-001.

## 3. Timing Requirements

- I-9: complete within the legally required time after hire (see CMP-IRS-001); confirm the current rule.
- New-hire reporting: submit within the legal deadline (see CMP-IA-001).
- First payroll: verify the employee appears in the first applicable payroll run.

## 4. Confidentiality

Completed onboarding records contain personal and financial information and are filed in the restricted records system (REC-REF-001), never in the public repository.
