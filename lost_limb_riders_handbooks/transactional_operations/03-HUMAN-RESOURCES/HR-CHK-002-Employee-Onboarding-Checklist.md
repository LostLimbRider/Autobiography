### Employee Identification

- [ ] Legal name verified (government-issued ID reviewed)
- [ ] Address, phone, email recorded
- [ ] Emergency contact recorded
- [ ] Start date, job title, department, supervisor confirmed
- [ ] Employment status and classification (exempt/non-exempt; full/part/seasonal)
- [ ] Compensation and pay frequency confirmed (per approved HR-FORM-005)

### Required Tax and Employment Documentation

- [ ] Form W-4 (federal) completed
- [ ] Iowa W-4 completed (where applicable)
- [ ] Form I-9 completed within the legal timeframe; documents inspected; I-9 filed **separately** from personnel file
- [ ] New-hire reporting submitted within the legal deadline (Iowa New Hire Reporting)
- [ ] Direct deposit authorization (if used) with account verification

### Payroll Setup

- [ ] HR-FORM-006 Payroll Setup completed and verified
- [ ] First payroll date confirmed and communicated

### Organizational Access and Training

- [ ] Keys/badges/credentials issued and logged
- [ ] Email/computer/phone set up
- [ ] Organizational accounts created per role; access logged
- [ ] Systems training completed
- [ ] Required safety training completed (see 09-SAFETY-RISK)
- [ ] Policies and handbook acknowledged in writing (HR-POL-001, HR-POL-002 — signed handbook acknowledgment filed in File A)
- [ ] Timekeeping instructions provided (HR-TIME-001)
- [ ] Position manual reviewed (HR-REF-001)

### First-Day and First-Week Tasks

- [ ] Workspace ready
- [ ] Introductions completed
- [ ] First-week schedule and priorities set
- [ ] 30/60/90-day expectations communicated
