## 1. Purpose

To recruit candidates fairly and lawfully once a position is authorized.

## 2. Prerequisite

The position must have an approved position authorization (HR-FORM-001). Do not recruit before authorization.

## 3. Procedure

1. **Confirm the job description** from the position manuals (HR-REF-001).
2. **Post the position** in appropriate channels (job boards, community networks, organizational website).
3. **Collect applications** using HR-FORM-002.
4. **Screen** applicants against the position requirements; document the screening decision for each applicant.
5. **Schedule interviews** with qualified candidates; the panel includes the hiring authority and a second interviewer where possible.
6. **Evaluate** each interview using HR-FORM-003.
7. **Check references** for finalists.
8. **Record the outcome** for each applicant (selected, not selected, withdrawn) and retain per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 4. Standards

- Nondiscriminatory job postings and interview questions (no questions about protected status).
- Consistent screening criteria applied to all applicants.
- No offers before selection is complete and compensation is approved (HR-SOP-003).
