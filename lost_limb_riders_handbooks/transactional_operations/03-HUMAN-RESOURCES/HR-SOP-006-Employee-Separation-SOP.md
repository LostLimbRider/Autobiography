## 1. Purpose

To end employment in a controlled way: final wages paid lawfully, access revoked, property returned, and records closed.

## 2. Procedure

1. **Initiate.** Document the separation decision and type (HR-FORM-009). For terminations, follow the disciplinary process (HR-SOP-007) and document the basis.
2. **Notify.** Notify the employee of the separation date and any notice period, per policy and law.
3. **Revoke access.** Revoke system, email, account, key, and badge access on or before the last day; record who did it and when.
4. **Collect property.** Recover organizational property (equipment, keys, uniforms, tools) and log it.
5. **Finalize time and pay.** Collect and approve final time records; calculate and pay final wages per applicable law and the pay schedule (HR-CHK-005).
6. **Close records.** Update personnel and separation files (HR-CHK-003), issue required notices (benefits/COBRA where applicable), and set I-9 retention.
7. **Update registers.** Close the employee record in HR-REG-001 and the master register; update payroll and compliance records.

## 3. Final Pay Requirements

Final wages are paid by the date required by applicable federal and Iowa law. Confirm the rule with the payroll professional before the final run.

## 4. Documentation

The separation file (HR-CHK-003, File F) contains the notice, final payroll, property return, and access revocation records, retained per REC-MATRIX-001.
