## 1. Scenario

The organization buys a hypothetical $600 trailer ramp and later disposes of it. All details are hypothetical.

## 2. Steps

- [ ] Asset need is identified; FIN-FORM-004 (Purchase Requisition) is approved per FIN-CTRL-001.
- [ ] Purchase is made; invoice and receipt are retained.
- [ ] Asset is tagged and recorded in FIN-REG-005 (Asset Register) with location, custodian, and purchase date.
- [ ] Payment is processed per FIN-PROC-002; accounting treatment is applied.
- [ ] Asset condition is reviewed at intervals; maintenance is documented.
- [ ] On disposal: disposal is approved per FIN-CTRL-001/GOV-SOP-001.
- [ ] Disposal is recorded; any proceeds are recorded in the accounting records and FIN-REG-005.
- [ ] Asset register is updated to show disposed status and disposition date.
- [ ] Records are retained per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 3. Expected Result

The asset is traceable from purchase approval through disposal, with proceeds accounted for and the register kept current.
