## 1. Scenario

A donor gives $500 by check, designated for the youth mentorship program. A second donor gives $150 cash at a ride with no designation. Amounts are hypothetical.

## 2. Steps

- [ ] Cash is counted by two people at the ride; both sign the count.
- [ ] Donations are recorded in FIN-REG-002 (Donation Register) on receipt.
- [ ] Restricted donation is flagged in FIN-REG-003 (Restricted Funds Register) with donor intent and date.
- [ ] Deposit is prepared and completed per FIN-CHK-004; FIN-FORM-007 (Deposit Log) is completed.
- [ ] Deposit is made within the deposit timing in FIN-POL-002.
- [ ] Bank receipt matches the deposit log.
- [ ] FIN-FORM-006 (Donation Acknowledgment) is issued to the $500 donor, stating the gift, the restriction, and whether any goods/services were provided.
- [ ] Acknowledgment for the cash donation is issued where the donor can be identified.
- [ ] Restricted funds are only spent on the youth mentorship program per FIN-PROC-006.
- [ ] Reconciliation matches donation register, deposit log, bank statement, and acknowledgment.

## 3. Expected Result

Every donation is receipted, deposited intact, acknowledged, and — if restricted — spent only as designated. Segregation of cash handling is demonstrated.
