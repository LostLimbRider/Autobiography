## 1. Scenario

A hypothetical local business pays $1,000 to sponsor a ride and receives banner and program recognition. All details are hypothetical.

## 2. Steps

- [ ] Prospect is approached per FUND-PROC-002.
- [ ] The payment is classified as sponsorship (a purchase of recognition), not a charitable donation — per FUND-POL-001 and FIN-PROC-005.
- [ ] A written sponsorship agreement (FUND-SPON-001) is signed before payment, stating the amount and the deliverables.
- [ ] Payment is received and deposited per FIN-PROC-005/FIN-CHK-004.
- [ ] Sponsorship is recorded in FIN-REG-006 (Sponsorship Register).
- [ ] Deliverables (banner, program, recognition) are delivered as agreed.
- [ ] Acknowledgment distinguishes sponsorship from a contribution; no charitable deduction is asserted.
- [ ] The sponsor relationship is reconciled in the event closeout (EVT-FORM-002).
- [ ] Records are retained per REC-MATRIX-001.

## 3. Expected Result

Sponsorship is contracted, received, delivered, and reported distinctly from donations, keeping the tax treatment clean.
