## 1. Purpose

A running record of what works and what does not at events. Completed by the event lead during closeout (EVT-CLOSE-001, step 8). Kept generic — no participant, donor, or staff names, and no incident detail beyond what the safety register already records.

## 2. How to Use

- One entry per event, appended below.
- Write three short lines: what went well, what did not, what we will change.
- Keep it factual and specific to process — this file exists so the next event does not repeat mistakes.
- Never record medical, disciplinary, or personal information here.
